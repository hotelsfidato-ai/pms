Rules to keep

Do

  • Use Action Orange for the primary button and Readable Orange for orange text.
  • Give each view one primary; a button next to Cancel says what it is.
  • Show a missing figure as a grey dash, never 0; never invent a price.
  • Use tabular figures and right-align numbers.
  • Separate surfaces with a 1px hairline; shadows only for what floats.
  • Put a section's description under its heading.
  • Say what failed and how to recover; say why a control is off.

Don't

  • Set text in Mist (grey-400), or small text in a full-strength brand colour.
  • Put the logo gradient on a button, heading or background.
  • Colour a count orange when it is zero.
  • Add a shadow to a card, tile or table.
  • Use a second icon family, or emoji as icons.
  • Go below 12px text or a 44px touch target on a phone.
  • Show a failed read as an empty list or a row of dashes.
