## The ten worst

1. Tax invoice for a split bill is billed to Agoda but carries the guest's coffee; the ₹6,000 room charge is on no invoice.
2. Settle on a split bill always works on the guest's main bill; "Bill to company" lands on the guest's bill, Agoda still owes.
3. Reception can post a negative charge (a discount with no approval) and reverse any charge or PAYMENT on one click, no reason.
4. A settled order's uncooked dishes vanish from the Kitchen screen (room service / pay-first orders are lost).
5. A confirmed booking cannot be cancelled (only voided); the Void dialog points to a Cancel that does not exist.
6. Banquet: "A bill is open for this function" but no screen reaches that bill: no advance, settle or invoice.
7. After a credit note, the stay cannot be invoiced again.
8. Zero prices accepted silently: rack rate ₹0, pricing base ₹0, menu dish ₹0, stock cost ₹0, PO line ₹0; unset pricing differences read as ₹0.
9. Guest ID: walk-in checks in an Indian guest with no ID or reason; Aadhaar "12ab" accepted; full Aadhaar shown (D21-18); expired passport/visa and a future birth date accepted; FRRO number "!" accepted.
10. "The guest's page link" after a booking always says "No link" (the screen expects an `ok` flag the server never sends).

Also high: running "bal" column wrong after a reversal; interim bill has no Print button and Total ≠ Outstanding;
occupancy differs by screen (Dashboard vs Calendar/Revenue outlook/Manager report); the chef (and other no-Dashboard roles)
can open the revenue Dashboard by address; children -1 and nights -2 saved; no screen can correct a guest's details.
