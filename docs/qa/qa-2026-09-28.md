# QA - every screen, every button - 28 September 2026

Asked by the owner (28 Sep): "The code is finished... Let's start QA testing: all buttons."
Found and reported only. Nothing was fixed.

**How it was run:** a copy of cp6 in `.claude\worktrees\wonderful-carson-923224`, its own database
`fidato_s20` (the demo hotel 5 Elements Akkalkot, business date 25 Aug 2026), API on 8847, website
on 5247, screens in the Claude app's browser pane at 1280 wide (and 390 wide for the phone pass).
Roles: Owner, Admin, GM, Hotel manager, Revenue manager, Reception, Accountant, Kitchen manager,
Chef, Inventory manager, Housekeeping, plus a Waiter (`pos`) and a Viewer (`read_only`) tester
added to this database only, because the sign-in page has no button for either.
**Skipped:** Housekeeping and Work orders (another session was fixing them).

**Severity:** BLOCKS WORK - a normal job cannot be done. WRONG RESULT - a wrong figure, record or
document is saved or shown (money, tax and guest ID are always at least this). Cosmetic - wording,
layout, a missing message. Question - needs the owner or the CA before anybody changes it.

**Counts:** 9 block work · 53 wrong result · 52 cosmetic · 12 questions for the owner or the CA (and 8 rows marked Question in the tables) · 8 checks that passed and 7 notes, listed so nobody re-tests them.

## The ten worst

1. **RO-1 - The server does not limit reads by role.** The Chef, Waiter, Housekeeping, Inventory manager, Kitchen manager, Revenue manager and Viewer all get the manager report (revenue), every stay and guest, guest bills, invoices, payments and the cash drawer from the API; only writes are guarded. The owner's rules on who sees money and guests hold only because the menu hides the screens (RO-2, RO-3).
2. **FI-1 - Tax invoice for a split bill is wrong.** Billed to Agoda, it carries the guest's coffee under the accommodation code; the ₹6,000 room charge is on no invoice.
3. **FI-2 / FI-3 - Settling a split bill.** Settle always works on the guest's main bill: "Bill to company" lands there and Agoda still owes; the guest is asked for the company's share; Company accounts disagrees with the City ledger report.
4. **FI-4 / FI-5 / FI-6 - Money off a bill with no control.** Reception can post a negative charge, and reverse any charge or payment on one click with no reason; the running balance is then wrong.
5. **POS-1 - The kitchen loses orders.** A sent order that is paid or charged to a room before it is cooked disappears from the Kitchen screen.
6. **BK-1 - A confirmed booking cannot be cancelled** (only voided), so a guest's cancellation and its fee cannot be recorded.
7. **BQ-1 - A banquet function's bill cannot be reached**: no advance, settle or invoice anywhere.
8. **FI-7 - After a credit note the stay can never be invoiced again.**
9. **X-1 - Zero prices are saved silently** (rack rate, pricing base, menu dish, stock cost, purchase line), and Pricing counts an unset difference as ₹0 (RT-3).
10. **Guest ID** - the walk-in skips the ID step (FD-1); Aadhaar "12ab" is accepted at the desk and on the guest's own page (FD-2, GP-1); the whole Aadhaar shows on the check-in summary (FD-3, against D21-18); expired passport and visa and a future birth date are accepted (GU-2); "!" is accepted as an FRRO number (GU-3).

Close behind: BK-2 (the guest's page link from a new booking always says "No link"), POS-2 / NA-4 (Close unpaid uses a browser prompt, so the restaurant day can be stuck), NA-1 (the night audit runs on one click), FI-8 (the interim bill cannot be printed), RP-1 (occupancy differs by screen).
