How this book is laid out

Why it works this way

The owner's decision behind what the screen does, with its number, for example D27-249.

Watch out

The unusual cases: what happens with no connection, and what a role cannot see.

  • Steps are numbered. Do them in order.
  • Words in bold are exactly what the screen shows: a button, a heading, a menu entry.
  • A message the system shows is written like this: “No arrivals today”.
  • Each figure is a picture of the screen. The orange numbers in the picture match the numbered list under it.
  • A dash (-) on a screen means the system does not hold that figure. It is never a zero.
  • An address such as /h/5EL/arrivals is what follows pms.fidatohotels.com in the browser's address bar.
