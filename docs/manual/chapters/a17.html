Chapter A17

Settings

For the owner, an Admin and the GM. How the hotel is set up: its profile, the step-by-step walk-through that takes a new hotel to its first booking, logo and pictures, policies, rooms, rate plans and taxes, the money lists, the lists the desk picks from, the front desk's times and rules, guest messages and report emails, people and security, and the modules and outside connections. Every other screen reads what is set here.

Who sees what

  • The owner and an Admin see every page.
  • The GM sees the same, except Roles and permissions, Sign-ins and devices, Modules and Integrations (Figure A17-50).
  • Nobody else has the Settings space.

Watch out

  • Many switches save the moment they are tapped, with no Save button: an amenity's switch, a payment method's or a list's On/Off, the choices on Front desk rules, the guest messages' switches, the modules and Rivals' prices. Change them only when you mean to.
  • The hotel a page changes is the one chosen in the top bar (A0.5). Check it before you change anything.

Why it works this way

Settings as its own space, with its own menu, for the owner, Admin and GM: D27-249. Settings shows only what somebody can change: D27-220. The GM opens Settings; the module switches stay the owner's and an Admin's: D17-36.

A17.2Profile: what the hotel still needs, its details and its numbers

Profile is the first page. It holds three things: the checklist of what the hotel still needs, the hotel's details that print on its invoices, and its registration numbers.

The checklist

The yellow card “Before 5 Elements Akkalkot can take a guest” lists only what is still missing, each line with why it matters. Tap a line to open the screen that settles it. Nothing on it is ticked by hand: each line asks the system the same question the work it guards asks. When everything is settled, the card goes; it comes back by itself if something stops being true. Above the list, a link, Set up this hotel, step by step, opens the walk-through below.

A line that blocks means “until then a booking, a bill or the night audit will refuse and say so.”

Set up this hotel, step by step

The checklist says what is missing. Set up this hotel walks a new hotel from nothing to its first booking in 18 steps. It is second in the Property group of the Settings menu, under Profile; the checklist links to it, and so does the Dashboard's Still to do card (below). It is one page for the hotel chosen in the top bar. The owner, an Admin and the GM have it; for anybody else the server says “A hotel is set up by its owner, general manager or Admin, or by a group administrator. Ask one of them.”

The steps come in three groups, each with a count such as “2 of 5 done”: Must be done before the first booking, steps 1 to 5 (“These five are what the hotel needs before it can sell a room and bill it correctly.”); Before opening, steps 6 to 13 (“The hotel can take a booking without these, but should not open its doors until they are done.”); and Optional, steps 14 to 17 (“Worth doing, and nothing waits on them.”). Step 18, Ready to go live, is the summary.

Each step is a row: its number (a tick once it is done), its name, one line on what it is, and its state: Done, To do, Later or Remind me with the day. Tap a row to open it. It says What it is and Why it matters, and, while the step is not done and the system can tell, What is missing: “Still missing: the GSTIN, the phone.” Under them is one button for each real screen the step needs (Open Taxes, Open Room types) and the choices. A screen your role cannot open is grey, and a line says so: “Your role cannot open … Ask the owner or an Admin.”

Where a step never ticks itself, or the hotel has nothing to set, you say so yourself. The four choices:

  • Completed: shown only on a step the system cannot see for itself. The row then reads Marked done by your name and the day. On a step the system can see it is not offered, and the system's word wins over anything chosen.
  • Next: goes to the next step. It changes nothing.
  • Later: puts the step aside. It waits on this page, and on the Dashboard: for the owner, an Admin and the GM, a card Still to do (“Steps of setting up this hotel that you put off.”) lists up to four, then “and 3 more”, and links back here. It shows only while something waits.
  • Remind me: Tomorrow, In 3 days, In a week, or a date after today (“Pick a day after today.”). On that day the bell shows “Setup: Taxes” and “You asked to be reminded today.” to every owner, Admin and GM of the hotel, and opens that step. The bell and the Ready to go live lines name a step as this page does: step 5 is Prices in all three.
  • Undo: takes back a Later, a Remind me or a tick by hand.

Back to setup. A screen opened from a step carries a slim orange bar under the top bar: “Setting up this hotel: step 2 of 18, Taxes”, with Back to setup and a ✕. It stays on every screen you visit in that browser tab until you press Back to setup, which returns to that step, or close it with the ✕. It is not drawn on the Set up this hotel page itself, where you can already see the step, and opening that page makes the bar forget its step: the next screen you open by hand has no bar until you open one from a step again.

Ready to go live changes nothing. Once steps 1 to 5 are done it says “Ready to go live. The five things a first booking needs are done. Nothing is locked: change anything, at any time.” Until then, “Not ready yet. Still needed before the first booking: Taxes, Rooms.” Under it are two lists: Still to do, with the steps you put off first, each opening its step, and Done.

Watch out

  • A step's state is worked out each time the page opens. It is never stored, so it can go back: delete the last room type and step 3 is to do again.
  • Ready to go live switches nothing on and locks nothing. It is a summary. Every setting can be changed at any time.
  • The invoice series is not a step. It stays on the Profile checklist, above.
  • The GM cannot open Modules or Integrations (A17.1), so on steps 13 and 17 those buttons are grey for them, with the line about the role. The GM may still mark step 13 Completed.

To change the hotel's details

  1. On the Profile card, tap Edit. The window Edit 5 Elements Akkalkot opens: “These print on this property's invoices.”
  2. Change what is wrong: City, State, Address, Address line 2, PIN code (six digits), GSTIN, Phone, Email, and further down Property type, Stars, Latitude and Longitude (the map point: both, or neither), Reservations contact, Reservations phone, Website and PAN.
  3. Tap Save.
  • The hotel's name is a group administrator's to change (the owner, or another person who reaches every property). For anybody else the Name box is locked and says “A group administrator changes the name.”
  • Reservations contact prints on the booking voucher as “Contact”. Reservations phone, Website and PAN are kept here and print on no document.

The FSSAI number and more registration numbers

  • FSSAI licence number: tap Change, type the 14 digits from the licence (blank for none) and save. It prints on food bills.
  • More registration numbers: tap Add, type a name (a trade licence, say) and the number, then Save. Up to four.
  • Under them, Open on map once the profile has a latitude and longitude; until then “No map point yet: it is set with the profile's latitude and longitude.”

Why it works this way

The checklist derives itself and is a yes or a no with the reasons, never a percentage: a hotel opening on “80% ready” is usually missing the tax number that prints on every bill. The name is a group administrator's to change: D27-214, D27-308. The walk-through, 18 steps in the owner's order, each ticking itself where the system can see it done, with Completed, Next, Later and Remind me and a summary that locks nothing: D27-311. Back to setup stays until pressed or closed, a reminder shows to every owner, Admin and GM of the hotel, and step 15 opens both Group and Policies: D27-312. A test hotel's Profile step needs no GSTIN, Cancellation terms may be marked Completed by hand, the bar is not drawn on the setup page, and the bell and the summary use the page's step names: D27-316. More registration numbers in Settings only, never printed: D27-257.

A17.3Logo and photos, Highlights, Amenities

These three pages are what the hotel shows: its logo on its documents, and its photos, texts and amenities on the guest's own page, after the guest's code.

  • With no logo of its own, the hotel prints the group's (A17.4). The voucher PDF prints a PNG logo; an SVG logo shows on the bills, and the group's logo on the voucher.
  • Up to 20 photos. “Never a guest in one.” Until one is added, guests' pages show none.

A text left blank is not shown. Saved blank, it is removed.

  • Each hotel switches on what it has. The switch saves at once.
  • The list itself is the owner's and an Admin's: they add, rename, move up or down, turn off and delete. Everybody else sees only the switches.
  • Each room type picks its amenities from the same list (A17.5).

Why it works this way

One amenities list for the whole group, each hotel switching on its own; highlights and amenities shown to guests after the code, not on the voucher; plain text with paragraphs: D27-257.

A17.4The group, and the policies

  • The group's name prints on every bill after the hotel's name, as “by” the group. Blank for none.
  • The group's logo prints at every hotel that has no logo of its own.
  • Office details (Office address, Phone, Email, Website, GSTIN, Instagram) are the footer of every booking voucher and every tax invoice (printed and e-mailed, A4.11). Until one is typed, the footer is the hotel's own. The tax invoice also prints the group's logo beside the hotel's own (A17.3).
  • They are typed once, for every hotel. The owner, an Admin or the GM may change them.
  • House policies: tap Edit. One policy a line, in the order they print; at most 8, each up to 200 characters. With none, the voucher prints the booking's terms and the times only. The registration card prints them over the guest's signature (A3.9).
  • Policy texts: Parking and arrival, Family and extra guests, Reservation terms. Each has its own box (2000 characters) and its own Save.
  • The child ages are words only. Prices do not change with them: the desk types the number of children as today, and a child costs the room type's extra-child rate once the room's own places are taken. Not set: “the desk decides, as today.”
  • Cancellation terms opens the Front office screen where they are written (A2).

Why it works this way

The group's office details on every voucher's footer, the bank details and the house policies per hotel: D27-215. One Policies page; the child ages as words only, for now: D27-257.

Documents

Documents, last in the Property group, shows what the hotel prints and sends, one tab each: Tax invoice, Proforma bill, Registration card, Booking voucher and Restaurant bill. Each tab says when the document is printed, links to where that is set, and shows a sample: the real document with this hotel's name and address and a made-up guest, “SAMPLE · NOT VALID” across it and no GSTIN. Nothing on the page is saved or sent.

  1. Open Settings › Documents and pick the tab.
  2. Click Print a sample. The print window opens: pick the printer to test it, or Save as PDF to keep a file. The tax invoice prints in the hotel's copies (A5.9). The booking voucher opens as its PDF in a new tab; print it from there.

Why it works this way

The documents as the hotel's old system printed them, in Fidato's design, with a page to see and test each: D27-344. A sample is printed, or saved as a PDF from the print window: D27-348. The sample's figures are a sample's; nothing reads them.

A17.5Room types and rooms

Room types is what the hotel sells and what it sells it in. The heading says how many: “2 room types · 18 rooms, 18 sellable”. No price is asked here: a new room type is unpriced until its price is set on Rates (A15).

To add a room type

  1. Tap Add a room type. The window says “What the property sells. The rate is set separately, on Rates.”
  2. Type its Code (two to ten letters or digits; it prints on the tape chart) and its Name.
  3. Type Adults the rate covers (before an extra-person charge), Children it covers and Sleeps at most. If you need them: Adults at most (empty: only the total), Children at most (fewer than the total), Size (sq ft), Beds, View, Colour (on the Calendar and the rate plans).
  4. Tap Save.

A room type in use is not removed: untick Sold at this property at the foot of Basic instead. That is refused while rooms are still of this type; off keeps its bookings and prices.

To add a room

  1. Tap Add a room.
  2. Type its Number (at most 10 letters, digits and -; a letter is saved as a capital, so 111a is room 111A) and choose its Type.
  3. Floor: 0 (the ground floor) to 99; leave it blank if the property has one floor. Tick Smoking room if it is one.
  4. Tap Save.

Tap a room's tile to change it. Unticking Sellable takes it out of the sellable house; for a fault with an end date, use a block instead. A room with somebody in it cannot be retired.

To remove a room added by mistake

  1. Tap the room's tile, then Remove at the foot of its window.
  2. The window asks first. Tap Remove room 309 to remove it, or Keep it. It then says “Room 309 removed”.

Only a room nothing has used can be removed: no booking, stay, housekeeping, key card, block, work order, lost item, wake-up call, equipment or guest link, and nobody in it. A used room is refused and says what used it: “Room 101 has been used (a booking or stay), so it cannot be removed. Switch Sellable off instead.” Untick Sellable instead, so old bills and Form C keep it. A room type whose rooms are all removed can then be removed too. A room with a connecting door is removed with its door: the other room is no longer connected, and the window says so: “Room 309 removed. Its connecting door to room 310 was taken off.”

To add several rooms at once

  1. Tap Add a room and switch on Several rooms. Number becomes From and To.
  2. Type the first and the last number, each as the hotel numbers its rooms: letters, digits and -, at most 10, shown in capitals. The two are the same except one number that counts up, or the last letter: From 101, To 109 adds 101 to 109; A101 to A110, G01 to G12, VIL1 to VIL8 and A-101 to A-110 work the same way; From 101A, To 109A adds 101A, 102A and on to 109A; From 111A, To 111D adds 111A, 111B, 111C and 111D. Zeros typed in From are kept: G01 to G12 adds G01, G02 and on to G12. Anything else is refused with its reason under To: a To below From, “To is below From.”; two parts changing, 101A to 109B or A101 to B101, “Only one number (or the last letter) may change between From and To.”; more than 100, “At most 100 rooms at a time.”
  3. Choose the Type, type the Floor if there is one, and tick Smoking room if they are. These are the same for every room.
  4. The button counts them: Add 9 rooms. Tap it. The window then says what was added: “Added 8 rooms: 101, 102, 103, 104, 106, 107, 108, 109”. A number the hotel already has is skipped and named, “105 already exists, skipped”, and the rest are added. Tap Done.
  • The floor is the one you type, for all the rooms: it is never worked out from the number, and left blank it stays blank. For rooms on several floors, add one range for each floor.
  • With Several rooms off, Add a room adds one room exactly as above.

Why it works this way

Nothing in use is deleted: reservations, rates and a year of bills point at a room type, and every stay at its room. A new hotel starts with no room types, and a room type nothing uses can be removed: D27-263. Room types in Settings, with their amenities, photos and the rooms in order: D27-258. Several rooms at once: one range, one floor typed for all, a number that exists skipped and named, the rest added: D27-309. Room numbers with letters, both kinds of range, and always in capitals, so 111a can never be a second room beside 111A: D27-335. A range as loose as each hotel's numbering, at most 100 rooms at a time, and a room nothing has used removed (a used one only switched off, so old bills and Form C keep it): D27-336; a connecting door removed with its room: D27-365.

A17.6Rate plans and meal plans

Prices are set on Rates ↗, the Front office screen (A15). Rate plans and meal plans set how those prices are offered; they never hold a second price.

KindMeans
MasterThe base room on room only. Changing it moves every derived plan.
DerivedFollows the master or its own room only, by a difference or the meal add-on.
IndependentIts own base, typed on Rates.

A plan is named by itself: “Superior · Room only (EP)”. Switched off, its prices and bookings stay. Drag a plan by its handle to change the order.

To change a plan

  1. Tap Edit. The editor has three steps; the Summary on the right follows your choices.
  2. Step 1, What it is: the room type, the meal plan, the name and the meals. Tap Next.
  3. Step 2, Its price: choose how it is priced. A derived room-only plan asks how much more than the master, in ₹ or %. Tap Next.
  4. Step 3, Who sells it: tick the booking sources that sell it. None chosen: every source sells it. Tap Update.

Meal plans

For each plan, tick which meals are served on the Check-in day, the Stay-over days and the Check-out day, then Save. Until then the plan says “Not said yet.” The days show on the rate plan's summary and on the guest's voucher. The price of a meal is its add-on, on Rates.

Why it works this way

Rate plans as a tree over the one pricing, so there is one price to change: D27-251. Independent, a plan switched Off, meal days on the summary and the voucher: D27-258.

A17.7Taxes

Taxes holds the GST rules, “One set of rules, for the front desk and the till”. Each heading (Rooms, Restaurant, Bar, Banquet, Spa, Laundry, Everything else, and Alcohol where the hotel serves it) has its rates and its HSN/SAC code.

To set or change a heading's rates

  1. Tap Edit (or Set it). The window says what the heading covers.
  2. One row is one rate. In the first row, leave On everything from at zero and type the GST %.
  3. To charge more above a figure, add a second row: Above the figure, and its GST %.
  4. If a rate starts or ends on a day, type From and Last day (empty: always, and current). Write a Note: why this band exists. It shows on the card.
  5. Type the HSN/SAC code your accountant gives you: a SAC is six digits; for goods sold under the heading (bottled water in the minibar, say), its HSN, of 4, 6 or 8 digits.
  6. Tap Save.

The till's half

Watch out

  • Not set is not zero. A heading with no rate shows a dash and Not set, and nothing under it can be billed until a rate is set: the bill or the till refuses and names it. A laundry charge, for one, is refused until Laundry has its rate.
  • A menu section under a heading with no rate is named in a yellow line at the top of the card: “The till will refuse those items rather than bill them at nothing.”
  • There are no rates built in. Every rate here is the hotel's own, confirmed by its accountant.
  • Alcohol is VAT, not GST: one rate, one VAT line on the bill, kept off the GST invoice. Its heading opens only where Serves alcohol is on (A17.14).

Why it works this way

One set of rules for the desk and the till, so a bar's whisky is never billed at the restaurant's rate by a default nobody chose. A SAC code for each heading: D27-258; an HSN in the same box for goods, printed on the proforma bill and the restaurant bill too: D27-344. Alcohol as VAT: D21-91.

A17.8Bank details and payment methods

Bank details

The hotel's bank details print on its booking voucher. With none set, the voucher prints no bank details. The group's owner is told whenever they change.

  1. Open Bank details and tap Set bank details (or Change).
  2. Type the Account name, the Account number (digits only, 9 to 18), the Bank, the Branch and the IFSC (eleven characters, the fifth a zero). Add a UPI ID if guests may pay to one.
  3. Tap Save. The card then says who changed them last, and when. Remove takes them off the voucher.

Payment methods

“How guests pay here: what the bill, New booking and a function's bill offer.”

  1. Tap Add a method.
  2. Type its Name: what the bill and the receipt print (“Paytm”).
  3. Choose In the books: where the reports count it. A booking site that collected the money is Bill to company: “Agoda” prints as Agoda and is owed by Agoda in the City ledger.
  4. Tap Save.
  • To change the default, open a method with its pencil and tap Make default. The default stays On. Times and rules shows it as Way of paying (A17.11).
  • Drag a method by its handle to change the order.

Why it works this way

Bank details per hotel, changed by the owner, the GM or an Admin, every change told to the group's owner: D27-215. The default way of paying set on Payment methods; a booking site's payment counted as Bill to company: D27-259.

A17.9Extra charges, discounts, commission, pickup and drop

Extra charges

What a booking may add, and the early check-in and late check-out fees. The bill's Add charge picks from this list.

  • A fee left blank is no fee, never ₹0.
  1. Tap Add a charge.
  2. Type its Name and its Price. Leave the price blank when the rate includes it at no charge (a welcome drink).
  3. Choose the Bill's column: where the money lands in the reports (a cake under Food, a pickup under Other charges).
  4. Switch on A pickup or a drop if it is one.
  5. Tap Save.

Discounts

Named discounts on rooms for New booking: a percentage or rupees off each night. By hand the desk may give up to 10% of a night, with a reason. A bigger discount is given only by picking one of these.

Commission

What each booking source and agent takes. A change applies to bookings made after it.

An agent's commission is set on the company's contract; Agent contracts, below the sources, lists them.

Pickup and drop

Airport, station and bus stand runs a booking may add. Each is an extra charge marked A pickup or a drop, added the same way. The time goes in the booking's note.

On all these lists: the switch turns one On or Off at once, the pencil edits it, and the bin shows only for one never used.

Why it works this way

Extra charges as the inclusions list plus the early and late fees, picked from the bill's Add charge; rupees off each night; free-hand discounts capped at 10% with a reason; commission per source on Money and a contract's on the contract; pickup and drop as extra charges marked so: D27-259.

A17.10The lists

The lists are what the desk picks from instead of typing. Booking sources ↗ opens its Front office screen (A2).

The list pattern

  • Market segments: who the guest is, for the reports, offered on New booking. A booking keeps its segment's words even if the segment is renamed or switched Off later.
  • Delete is refused for one something uses: switch it Off instead.

Reservation types

The colour of each kind of booking on the Calendar: Confirmed, Tentative (waiting for the hotel), In house, Due out, Checked out, No-show. Colours only: these are the states a booking can be in today.

Ready reasons

One tapped fills the reason box. Typing your own still works, and the reason is kept exactly as it was written.

Holidays, ID types, VIP levels

Holidays are shaded on the Calendar and Rates like a weekend, with the holiday's name on the Calendar. No price reads them. The bin beside one deletes it.

One switched Off leaves the check-in, New booking and the guest's online form. A document already recorded stays as it was.

A guest is one record at every hotel: marking a guest VIP asks which level, and the level travels with them. Each level has a colour.

Why it works this way

Reservation types as colours only for today's states; VIP levels one group list; ID types Off also leave the guest's online form; holidays shaded, with no effect on a price: D27-260.

A17.11The front desk's times and rules

Times and rules

A guest arriving before the first time, or leaving after the second, needs a reason. The charge reaches their folio unless the owner, the GM or a group administrator waives it, and the reason is kept either way. A late departure counts only on or after the departure date, and is recorded once (A1.6).

Night audit opens at (16:00 to begin) is the time, on the hotel's own clock, before which nobody can run today's night audit; a day already behind can always be closed (A7.3). Only the owner or an administrator can change it; the GM still saves the other hours.

  • An advance is never asked of a company's booking, a room the group's host pays for, or a booking prepaid on a booking site.
  • With The desk may overbook off, a booking (or a group) past a full house is refused.
  • Nationality: two letters, IN for India. Title: what a new guest's title starts on.

Front desk rules

This page repeats the rules card at the top, then the rest of what the desk's screens start on and allow. Each choice here saves the moment it is made.

  • A new booking starts as: the desk can still change both on each booking.
  • A booking must carry: Phone, Email, Nationality, Address, Expected arrival time, Market segment, Business source, Sales person. A booking from a booking site is never held for them.
  • Housekeeping: a room a guest leaves at check-out is always sent for cleaning. The switches decide the rest: a room a guest moved out of; a room back from a block (off: it comes back as it was before the block); and whether the night audit sends every room with a guest staying on for its daily clean.
  • The tax invoice at check-out (A5.9): Issue it and print it, by itself (the hotel starts on this), Offer an Issue tax invoice button, or Nothing at check-out: issue it on Invoices. And 2 copies (the hotel starts on this) or 1 copy: two print as “ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT” and “DUPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER”, wherever the invoice is printed. Who may issue an invoice does not change.
  • The restaurant's round-off: Manual: the Round off button, Automatic: every bill paid there, to ₹1, or Off: no Round off button. A restaurant bill paid in cash is always taken to the rupee. Room bills and invoices are never rounded.
  • How the figures count: occupancy = rooms sold ÷ rooms available; ADR = room revenue ÷ rooms sold; RevPAR = room revenue ÷ rooms available. Rooms blocked that night are not available. Two switches: A complimentary room counts as sold and A day use counts as half a room sold. The Manager report and the statistics reports (Monthly occupancy, Monthly statistics, Yearly statistics) follow them (A11).

Why it works this way

The default guarantee and bill-to, the fields a booking must carry (never refusing a channel's booking), the housekeeping switches, the round-off for restaurant bills only, and the figures in plain words: D27-260. The tax invoice issued and printed at check-out by default, in two copies, each hotel's choice: D27-344. The fees live on Extra charges, and the way of paying on Payment methods: D27-259.

A17.14Modules and connections

Channel manager ↗ (A15), CRS bookings ↗ (A2) and Integrations ↗ (A12) open their Front office screens.

  • The owner and an Admin only. A switch saves at once: “Turned on” or “Turned off”.
  • The modules: Point of sale, Banquet hall, Reception can use the POS, Channel manager, Laundry, Spa, Serves alcohol, Loyalty, Bar stock. Each says under it what Off does. For most, the screens go and the server refuses them: not just a hidden menu entry. Point of sale off also stops an outside till posting bills to this property.
  • Reception can use the POS off hides the point of sale from Reception only; every other role is unaffected.
  • Bar stock is on at every property open before release 14 until it is turned off; a property added since starts with it off (D27-369). Off, the Bar stock screen, Inventory's Bar tab, the bar bills, the Bar stock report and the FLR registers go and the server refuses them, KOTs take nothing off the bar's stock, and the night audit does not wait for a bar count. Bar KOTs still print at the bar (A9.11).
  • A second card, New booking, holds the sections that fold open on New booking (Company section, Notes section). Off hides one at this property; nothing already booked changes.

Switched on, each nearby hotel's lowest price on Google Hotels is checked for the next 30 nights, on Mondays and Thursdays from 06:00, the hotel's time (about 260 searches a month). What is found shows beside each suggestion on Revenue outlook (A15). Nothing here changes a price.

Every outside connection, for the hotel in the top bar: Emails (n8n), WhatsApp (Meta), CRS bridge, Firebase sign-in, Backups and Rivals' prices. Backups and Firebase are the server's own. The line under the list says whether the owner is emailed when a connection turns red. Nothing is changed here: a connection is set up on the server.

Why it works this way

The module switches are the owner's and an Admin's: D17-36. Rivals' prices, 30 nights twice a week, the lowest public price: D27-270. One page for every outside connection: D27-276.
