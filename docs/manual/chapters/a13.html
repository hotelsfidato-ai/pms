Chapter A13

Banquets, spa and laundry

For the general manager, reception and housekeeping, at a hotel that runs a banquet hall, a spa or a laundry. How each is switched on, how a function is booked, fed and billed, how a spa treatment is booked and put on the room bill, and how a laundry bag goes out, comes back and is charged.

A13.1The three parts, and their switches

Not every hotel has a banquet hall, a spa or a laundry. Each is switched on hotel by hotel. Where it is on, the Front office menu has a part for it: Banquet, Laundry and Spa. Where it is off, that part of the menu is not there at all.

ScreenWho uses itWhat it is for
DiaryThe GM; the owner, an Admin, the hotel manager. Reception may also register and confirm a function (open it with Find a screen)Each hall and the functions in it: register, confirm, feed, bill (A13.3, A13.4).
Halls and packagesOwner, Admin, GMThe hotel's halls, and its catering packages (A13.2).
Spa diaryReception; the owner, an Admin, the GM, the hotel manager. Housekeeping sees it and does not bookOne day, therapist by therapist: book, charge to the room, cancel (A13.5).

DocketsReception and housekeeping (in their menus); the owner, an Admin, the GM, the hotel managerEvery laundry bag, a guest's and the hotel's own: collect, record what came back, charge to the room (A13.6).
Price listThe same people, one search awayWhat the hotel charges for each thing it launders (A13.6).

To switch one on

Only the owner or an Admin can. Other roles do not see the page.

  1. Choose the hotel. Click Settings in the switcher at the top (A0.4).
  2. In the menu, under Modules and connections, open Modules (/settings/connections/modules). The card Modules says “What this property runs.”
  3. Tap the switch beside Banquet hall, Laundry or Spa. The message “Turned on” names it, and the menu shows its part at once.

Each switch says what it runs, in the screen's words:

  • Banquet hall: “The diary, the halls and their packages. A hall cannot be sold twice for the same session.”
  • Laundry: “A guest's laundry, charged to their room, and the hotel's own linen going out and coming back.”
  • Spa: “Treatments, the day's diary, and each one charged to the guest's room.” Settling at the spa is not built: a treatment is always put on the room bill (A13.5).

What staff see while one is off

The menu has no part for it. A screen opened by its address, or from an old link, says it is not available, with the reason (Figure A13-x2). Nothing can be booked or changed, even by typing the address: the server refuses it as well.

Watch out

  • The message says “Turn it on in the property's settings if that is wrong.” Only the owner or an Admin can. Anyone else: ask one of them. The server's answer to anyone else is “Switching a module on or off is for the owner or an administrator. Ask one of them.”
  • Each part starts off at a hotel until somebody turns it on. Nothing is set up for you when it is: no hall, no treatment, no price (A13.2, A13.5, A13.6).

Why it works this way

The module switches are the owner's and an Admin's; the GM may open Settings: D17-36. Laundry and the spa, each behind its own switch, shown only where it is on: D17-54. A part of the menu that is off is absent, not greyed out, and the server refuses its work on its own, because a hidden screen is still an address anybody can type.

A13.2Banquets: halls and packages

Before the first function, enter the hotel's halls. Open Halls and packages (or the button of that name on the Diary). The screen says “Nothing here is created for you, and no price is ever assumed.” Only the owner, an Admin or the GM can add or change anything on it. For anybody else, Reception for one, Add a hall, Edit, Add a package and the packages' switches are grey, and each card says who may: “The owner, an administrator or the GM adds and edits them.”

To add a hall

  1. Tap Add a hall.
  2. Type its Name (“Emerald Hall”). Seated capacity and Standing capacity are optional: leave them blank if they have not been measured.
  3. Usual rate is optional too. It is only ever offered on the booking form; what is billed is what is agreed for each function. Tick Free only if the hall really is free.
  4. Tap Save. Change a hall later with Edit.

To add a package

A package is food sold by the head, such as a dinner buffet. Packages are optional: a function can be fed off-menu, from the restaurant, or not at all.

  1. Tap Add a package.
  2. Type its Name, and if you wish its Rate a head (or Free), its Kind (“Veg, non-veg, jain…”) and What is in it.
  3. Tap Add. A package with no rate reads “no rate - agreed per function”.

To take a package off offer

A package is never changed once it is added: “Never changed once added. Switch one off to stop offering it; functions keep what they were sold.” For a new rate or a new menu, switch it off and add another.

  1. Under Packages, tap the switch at the right of the package's line. The line shows Off, and the message says “Dinner buffet is off: not offered for functions now”.
  2. Tap it again to offer it again: “Dinner buffet is offered again”.

A package that is off is not in the list when a course is added (A13.4), and the server refuses it for any function. A function that already has it keeps its line as it was sold: its name, plates and rate.

Watch out

  • A package is never changed once it is added, only switched off. Name it carefully, and add a new one for a new rate or menu.
  • Changing a hall's usual rate never changes a function already booked: each function keeps the figures agreed for it. A package is never changed at all.

Why it works this way

A package can be taken off offer but not changed, so a function sold on it, and its bill, never move: D27-305. Halls and packages are set up by the owner, an Admin and the GM; every button is grey, with a sentence saying who may, for anybody else: D27-307.

A13.3Banquets: the Diary, and booking a function

The Diary answers one question: is the hall free on that day? Each hall is a row, with its functions under it, over 30 days from the hotel's business date. ← Earlier and Later → move 30 days; From today comes back. A hall with nothing booked says “Free for the whole window.”

Each function shows its name, its state, its reference, when it runs, the host, the guests expected, who registered it, and the agreed amount, or a dash if none is agreed yet. Tap it to open it.

StateMeans
EnquiryWritten down. It holds nothing: two families may ask about the same Saturday.
ConfirmedIt holds the hall for that time, and its bill is open.
CompletedBilled: the hall and food are on its bill (A13.4).
CancelledCancelled, with the reason. The hall is free again.

To register a function

  1. On the Diary, tap Register a function. The window says “This holds nothing yet. Confirming it takes the hall.”
  2. Choose the Hall. Type What is the function? (“Sharma sangeet”), From and Until (a date and a time each), and the Host. Telephone, Expected guests and Notes are optional.
  3. Agreed amount is the hall's price. Leave it blank if it is not agreed yet: it stays unpriced, not free. Use usual fills in the hall's usual rate; Free means no charge.
  4. Tap Register. The message gives its reference and says it is “an enquiry until it is confirmed.” The function opens.

If the guests expected are more than the hall holds, the form warns “More than this hall holds”; it is a warning, not a refusal.

To confirm it

  1. Open the function and tap Confirm and hold the hall.
  2. The message “The hall is held, and a bill is open.” means it is done.
  3. If another confirmed function has the hall at that time, it is refused, and the message names it: “The hall is taken: …” with its name, reference and times.

The function's page

  • What and when: from, until, the host and telephone, guests expected and guaranteed, the hall, who registered it.
  • The money: Hall, Catering, Restaurant orders and the Total. If anything is unpriced the total is a dash, and a line says what is missing. Set the hall price (or Change the hall price) sets the agreed amount; an empty box makes it unpriced again.
  • Catering: the courses (A13.4). With none, it says “Nothing is being served. That is a complete booking - add a course only if food is part of it.”
  • Restaurant orders: what the restaurant charged to this function (A13.4).
  • The bill: once confirmed (A13.4).

To cancel a function

  1. Tap Cancel. Type Why?: it is required, and it stays on the record.
  2. Tap Cancel the function (or Keep it). The message says “The hall is free again.”

A function with an advance may be cancelled; the advance stays as the host's credit. A billed (Completed) function cannot be cancelled.

Why it works this way

A hall is booked from a time until a time, and only a confirmed function holds it, so it cannot be sold twice; an enquiry holds nothing. No default prices: a blank amount is unpriced and shows a dash, and a price of 0 is kept only when ticked Free: D27-120. Cancelling a function with an advance, and never a billed one: D27-135.

A13.4Banquets: food, and the function's bill

To add a course

  1. On the function, under Catering, tap Add a course.
  2. Choose a Package, or Off-menu. A package fills in its name and offers its rate. Only packages that are on are listed (A13.2).
  3. Check What is being served?, type Plates (at least one) and Rate a head. Leave the rate blank if it is not agreed yet; the total says so until it is.
  4. Tap Add. The bin beside a course takes it off.

Food from the restaurant

A confirmed function running today can take food from the restaurant. When taking the order on Tables, the Till or the Terminal (A8), choose it under Banquet function. That order is then paid on the function's bill, not at the till. It shows under Restaurant orders; an open order is not in the total until it is settled.

The bill

Confirming opens the function's bill. Its card, The bill, shows what is charged, the GST, what is paid and the balance. Only the owner, an Admin and the GM take money and bill; the Accountant may also issue the tax invoice.

  1. An advance: tap Take an advance, choose the Method, type the Amount, tap Take payment. No GST receipt voucher is issued for an advance yet.
  2. Bill it: tap Bill the function. The hall and every course go on the bill under the Banquet GST rate. After this the price, the courses and the restaurant orders are fixed, and the function is Completed.
  3. Settle: tap Settle and take the balance the same way.
  4. Invoice: tap Issue tax invoice. It takes the next number of the series, and a number once taken cannot be taken back. The invoice then shows on the card.

Bill the function is refused while anything is unpriced, while a restaurant order on the function is still open, or while the hotel's banquet tax is not set: “Banquet tax is not set: ask the GM to set it before the bill.” While it is not set, the function's page says so at the top.

More than ₹1,00,000 in cash at once asks for the payer's PAN, or Form 97. Paying more than the balance after the bill is kept only if you tick Hold it as the host's credit.

To correct a billed function

Before the tax invoice: Reopen the bill, type Why, and tap Reopen the bill. The hall and every course come off the bill, with the reason on each line; advances, payments and restaurant orders stay. Change what is wrong, then bill the function again. A bill posted on a day the night audit has closed is refused: “correct it on Closed days.” After the tax invoice, only a credit note corrects it.

Why it works this way

Restaurant food on a function's bill: D17-21, posted like a room charge so it is on the invoice: D27-133. Who takes the money, and the Accountant's invoice: D27-132. The warning while banquet tax is unset: D27-124. Billing any time after Confirm, its refusals, and the advance with no receipt voucher: D27-138. The invoice's SAC, 996334 with food and 997212 for a hall alone, for the CA to confirm: D27-134. Reopening: D27-154. A function's bill lives on its own page, not on Folios: D27-137.

A13.5The spa

The spa has two screens: the Spa diary, and Treatments (its heading is Treatments and therapists). Reception books, charges and cancels. Housekeeping sees the diary and does not book; the diary tells them so.

Setting it up (owner, Admin, GM)

  • Treatments: Add a treatment with its Name, How long, in minutes, and Price. Leave the price empty if none is set; it reads a dash, and a price added later applies to new bookings only. Change it later; Take off the list and Put back on the list.
  • Therapists: Add a therapist with a Name. It is a name, not an account: nobody signs in as a therapist. Take off the list when someone leaves; past bookings stay as they were.

The diary

One day at a time, opening on the hotel's business date: ← Day before, Today, Day after →. Each therapist is a row with their treatments: the time, the treatment, the guest and room, and the price, or a dash. A therapist with nothing booked says “Free all day.”

To book a treatment

  1. Move the diary to the day of the treatment. Tap Book a treatment.
  2. Choose Which guest: guests in the house, or arriving (with their arrival date).
  3. Choose Which treatment. Its length places it in the day, so nothing asks when it ends.
  4. Choose Which therapist, type What time, and tap Book it. The message says “The treatment is in the diary.”
  • Every treatment is for a guest's stay, on a day from their arrival to their departure. Otherwise: “The guest is not staying on that day.” A guest who is not staying cannot be booked.
  • One therapist is never in two places at once. If the time is taken, the message names who is in the way: the therapist, the guest, the treatment and the time.

When the treatment is given: Charge to room

  1. On the treatment, tap Charge to room.
  2. The window says the price “goes on their bill as its own line, taxed at this property's spa rate.” Tap Charge to room (or Not yet).
  3. The message names the room. The treatment now shows On the room.
  • The guest must be checked in: “The guest has not checked in yet, so it cannot go on their room bill.”
  • If the hotel has not set its spa GST rule, the charge is refused, and the message says where GST is set.
  • To undo a charge, reverse the line on the guest's bill (A4). A charged treatment can no longer be cancelled.

To cancel a treatment

Tap Cancel, type Why, and tap Cancel the treatment (or Keep it). The message says “The hour is free again.”

Watch out

  • Price the treatment before you book it. The price is copied onto the booking when it is taken. A booking taken while the treatment had no price shows a dash and “Unpriced”, has no Charge to room, and pricing the treatment afterwards does not change that booking: “Unpriced - it cannot be closed for money. A price added to the treatment later applies to new bookings only: cancel this one and book it again.” Cancel it and book it again.
  • Settling at the spa (cash at the spa) is not built yet: every treatment goes on the room bill.

Why it works this way

Booked by telephone to the desk: D18-6. Run by reception and housekeeping, with no new roles and no therapist sign-ins: D18-8. Reception books and closes, housekeeping only sees, and the spa's own GST rule per hotel: D18-21. On the room bill as its own line: D18-7; settling at the spa set aside for later: D18-22. Booked only on a day of the stay, charged only once checked in: D27-136.

A13.6The laundry

One place for everything that goes to the laundry: a guest's bag, and the hotel's own linen. A docket is one bag: whose it is, what is in it, and what has come back. Reception and housekeeping run it; Dockets is in their menus.

The price list

Open Price list (or the button on Dockets). Its heading is Laundry price list. One line is one thing and one service: a shirt washed, a saree dry-cleaned.

  1. Tap Add an item. Type What is it, choose What is done to it (Wash, Press or Dry-clean), and the Price if the hotel has decided one.
  2. A price can be typed later in the list itself; it is saved when you leave the box, and it applies to new bags only: a bag already collected keeps its dash. Free means free. Retire takes a line off the list; dockets already written keep their own copy.

The hotel's own linen goes on the list with no price: it is counted, never charged.

To collect a bag

  1. On Dockets, tap Collect a bag.
  2. Answer Whose is this first: A guest's bag or The hotel's own linen. Nothing is filled in for you.
  3. For a guest, choose Which stay. Only guests in the house are listed: a bag is collected only from a checked-in guest.
  4. Under What is in the bag, pick each thing from the price list and type How many. Add another thing for the next.
  5. Anything to note is optional (“Ink on the left cuff”). Tap Collect it. The message gives the docket's number, and the docket opens.

The list of dockets

Everything, Guests' bags or The hotel's linen. Every row says whose it is, its state (Out, Back or Cancelled), the pieces, how many are still out, the total, and when it was collected. What is still out is listed first.

One docket

  • When it comes back: tap Record what came back and type how many of each have come back in total, not how many arrived this time. Tap Record it. When nothing is still out, the docket marks itself Back.
  • A guest's bag: tap Charge to the room. It goes on the guest's bill as its own line, with the docket number and what was in the bag, under the hotel's laundry GST rate. The message says it “is on the room's bill as its own line.”
  • The hotel's own linen has no charge button: it is never charged to anybody. The docket says it, in a card headed The hotel's own linen: “Counted out and back, never charged to anybody.”
  • Print prints the docket. Cancel works until it is on a bill; after that the charge is reversed on the bill instead. A docket already on a bill carries a card, Already on the bill: “It is its own line on the room's folio. A charge is reversed on the folio, never by cancelling the docket under it.”

Watch out

  • Price the list before you collect. The description and price are copied onto the docket when the bag is collected. A bag holding an item with no price has no total and cannot be charged, and pricing the item afterwards does not change a bag already collected: “A price added to the price list later applies to new bags only: cancel this one and take it again.” Cancel the bag and take it again.
  • If the hotel has not set its laundry GST rule, the charge is refused: “This property has no GST rule for laundry.”
  • Not in this version: linen par levels per room type, an outside laundry's bills, express surcharges, and a guest sending a bag from their own page.

Why it works this way

A guest's laundry and the hotel's own linen in one place, every docket saying whose it is: D18-5. Straight onto the room bill as its own line: D18-7. Run by reception and housekeeping: D18-8; Dockets in their menus where the hotel has laundry on: D22-22. Collected only from a checked-in guest: D27-136. Laundry's own GST heading, refused until set: D27-194. The docket's own words, with no decision codes on a staff screen: D27-316.
