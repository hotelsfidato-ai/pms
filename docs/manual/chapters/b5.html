Chapter B5

Connections

For the owner. Every way the PMS talks to something outside it: the sending workflow in n8n, WhatsApp and its templates, email, the CRS bridge, the booking sites through SU, outside tills, the guest's ways of proving who they are, the leads app, and the provisioning key. What each does, what it needs from you, and what happens when it fails.

B5.1The connections at a glance

A connection is switched on by its settings on the server, which only the owner types in (they are secrets, and never go in the code, a screen or a chat). Until they are there, the connection does nothing, and the screens say so in plain words rather than offering a button that would fail.

B5.2The one door out: the sending workflow

Every message the hotel sends a guest leaves through one part of the API, and from there through one workflow in n8n, PMS send · email and WhatsApp (CRS). The PMS decides what goes, to whom and when; it sends the figures as data with the exact PDF the desk prints, and n8n writes the message and sends it D17-87 D17-91 D18-20. Since 30 September that one workflow sends everything; the PMS runs no n8n workflow of its own for guests D27-186.

What it needs

  • Three settings on the server: the workflow's address, its secret, and the WhatsApp number's Meta id. All three, or nothing sends (deploy/n8n/IMPORT.md, part C).
  • The pace: how many messages a minute. It is the owner's number, from what the WhatsApp account allows; until it is given, the job that sends vouchers sends nothing at all, rather than everything at once D21-34.
  • In n8n: the mailbox the guest sees (until it is filled, every email is refused with a sentence the desk sees), and the template names (B5.3).

When it fails

  • A message that fails is written down with the reason ("The receiver did not answer within twenty seconds", or the refusal itself) and shown on Guest messages, which the desk reads. A message never attempted is not written down at all.
  • It is tried again, the next minute, up to three times. Each try carries the same message id, and the workflow remembers what it has sent for 30 days, so a try after a lost answer never reaches the guest twice.
  • A booking never fails because of a message: the message is sent by a separate job, after the booking is saved.
  • n8n down, or the server's n8n unreachable: every message fails the same way and Connection health turns Emails red after three failures in a row. On 16 September it was found that every deploy had been cutting n8n off from the network without anybody noticing; the service that puts its network back after a deploy was switched on then (MEMORY 279).

B5.4Email

Email goes from the mailbox the sending workflow names, the CRS's crs@fidatohotels.com, through the mail login already in n8n D27-266. The PMS sends only the guest's address and the figures; the wording of each email is in the workflow, changed there without a release D17-88 D17-87. An email kind the workflow has no wording for is refused with a sentence on Guest messages ("No email for … in this workflow"): the alert, report and invoice emails needed the workflow updated on 5 October (deploy/n8n/IMPORT.md, part C).

B5.5The CRS bridge

The CRS is the sales team's app, for the partner hotels Fidato does not own. When a salesperson books one of the group's own hotels there, the PMS must hold the room and the hotel must confirm D27-35.

  1. Every five minutes the PMS signs in to the CRS with a read-only login of its own and reads the bookings changed since its last read. The CRS's document id is the key, so a booking read twice is one booking.
  2. A new confirmed CRS booking for a connected hotel becomes a booking Waiting for the hotel, its room held, the salesperson as its sales person, its rate the CRS's quote before tax D27-171. If the hotel is full, nothing is booked.
  3. A booking that cannot be made by itself waits under Needs attention with the reason: a CRS room type with no match, no rate, a price that does not split exactly, a date before the hotel's business date.
  4. The hotel confirms; then the one voucher goes, in the PMS's own design, sent once D27-171.
  5. A later change in the CRS, a cancellation included, is a note for the desk with Done; nothing is changed by itself D27-171.
  6. Later, with a workflow in the CRS's n8n: the hotel's answer (Confirmed with the PMS reference, Cancelled, or "Partly confirmed: N of M rooms") is written into the CRS's booking register, where the salesperson sees it D27-106 D27-174. Until then, answers wait.
  • Setting it up, once per hotel, is the owner's (deploy/README.md, "Connect the CRS"): a read-only login in the CRS's Firebase, its four settings on the server, the card filled and saved switched off, a script run on the server that makes the account the reader works as (a Viewer at that one hotel, with no sign-in), then the switch on, then one test booking. Only bookings made after the switch is on are read; earlier ones are typed by hand.
  • Until the CRS's n8n is changed, the CRS still sends its own voucher at once when "Send the confirmation to the guest" is ticked, before the hotel confirms. So the sales team unticks it for the group's hotels, and types "PMS" as "Confirmed by" D27-286.

When it fails

  • The card says why in words: the login not in the settings, a wrong password, a missing permission in the CRS, the CRS's key refusing the server.
  • Nothing is booked while the read fails; the salesperson must phone the hotel. Connection health turns the CRS bridge red when a read fails or nothing has been read for 30 minutes.
  • Switching the card off stops the reading at the next turn; bookings already made stay.

Why it works this way

  • The PMS reads; it never trusts a push from the CRS. The plan of 27 September had the CRS's webhook call n8n. But the CRS is a browser app with no server, and its webhook key is readable by every CRS user, so a call could not prove it came from the CRS. Reading with the PMS's own read-only login proves it (src/modules/crs/MODULE.md).
  • Waiting, not confirmed: the CRS has no availability of its own, so the hotel's yes is what makes it real; the room is held meanwhile so nobody else sells it D27-35.
  • One voucher, once, whoever made the booking, so a guest is never sent two D27-35 D27-99.

B5.6The booking sites, through SU

SU (Su channel manager) is the wire to Agoda, Booking.com, Expedia, Goibibo and the rest D17-30 D17-66. Built in four parts, all done: SU's login, the hotel's content, rooms left and prices out, bookings in D27-178. It is not connected: nothing reaches SU or any site until the owner has SU's keys in the server's settings, the hotel is switched on, and the owner says yes to the first live send.

  • Bookings come in by push: SU sends each one to the API's own door the moment the site takes it D17-79.
  • No room free: the booking is not made, SU is told it was received, 0 rooms left goes at once for that night, and the GM sees it as "Refused: no room free" to cancel on the site and call the guest D17-80.
  • Waits for a person: nights at different prices, a change to a booking, a request to book, a booking not in rupees D27-183. A cancellation cancels by itself under the site's policy, unless the guest has arrived D27-183 D17-44.
  • Card numbers are never kept: dropped on arrival, never stored, logged or shown D17-83.
  • Commission: SU's figure when it sends one, else the source's percentage, else a dash D17-84.
  • Who: the SU setup is the owner's and an Admin's; the GM sees the bookings and deals with the refused ones D17-85 D27-183. Each hotel is switched on separately D17-45.

B5.7Outside tills

A restaurant that runs its own till can charge a guest's meal to their room through four calls to the API (docs/POS-INTEGRATION.md): which rooms may be charged, charge this bill, did my bill land, and void it. The hotel's own restaurant does not need this: it is part of the PMS (A8).

  • A key per outlet, made on the server (npm run pos:outlet) and shown once. Only its fingerprint is kept, so it cannot be read back from the database or a backup; a lost key is replaced and the old one stops at once. The key belongs to one outlet at one hotel, and is refused where the hotel's restaurant module is off.
  • The till's own bill number makes a retry safe: the same number sent again returns the first charge, never a second.
  • The PMS dates every charge with the hotel's business date; the till never sends a date.
  • A void is a cancelling line, never a deletion; refusals are recorded with their reason. Charges to rooms (A8.1) shows everything an outside till sent, refusals included.
  • Two guests in one room on a changeover day: the PMS will not guess; it asks the till which guest.

When it fails: a till that timed out asks "did my last request land?" instead of sending again; the answer "not received" means send it, "posted" means it is on the bill.

Said differently in the documents

The worked example in docs/POS-INTEGRATION.md sends the till's calls to https://pms.fidatohotels.com/api/pos. That address is the website, which answers every path with its own page; the API, and the till's door, is api.fidatohotels.com. A till set up from the example would get a web page back.

B5.8The guest's ways in: codes, Google, DigiLocker, ID photos

  • A code to the booking's phone on WhatsApp or to its email, or Continue with Google, where the Google account's verified email must be the booking's D27-207. Nothing is sent until the guest presses a button D27-210. During the stay a guest may type the room and phone number; the code goes only to the booking's own number, and only when both match D27-235 (2.4).
  • DigiLocker, through API Setu as a DigiLocker partner: prepared and switched off until the partner keys and the lawyer's answers arrive D27-235 (2.5). When on, the DigiLocker account's mobile must be the booking's own number; only the masked Aadhaar number and the name are kept; the ID waits for the desk like any other submission. A fuller ID number already on file is kept D27-247.
  • ID photos go through a second workflow in n8n, with its own secret, into Google Drive: one encrypted file per upload, opened only with that hotel's password; the server keeps nothing D27-208 D27-209 D27-211. A hotel with no password set is not offered the upload. A failed delete is shown to the owner, Admin and GM with "Try again".

Watch out

  • A hotel's ID photo password cannot be recovered. A changed password does not open earlier files, and a lost one means those photos can never be opened. The owner accepted this: no master password, no rotation D27-211. Keep each hotel's password in the password manager.

B5.9What the PMS only reads: the leads app and rivals' prices

  • The leads app (the market-to-revenue app) keeps running on its own; the PMS shows its enquiries, conversations and spend, read live every time and never copied, with a link to reply there D27-100 D27-170. It reads with the server's own Google credential, borrowing a short-lived token, because the organisation allows no keys D27-186. At most 5,000 reads a day, since the app's free plan is shared with the app itself; at the cap the screen says so and reads nothing. A guest erased in the app disappears from the PMS's view too; their stays and bills stay D27-108.
  • Rivals' prices: a search service (SerpApi) reads Google Hotels' lowest public price per night for nearby hotels, 30 nights ahead, twice a week, for a hotel switched on D27-270. With the switch off, the prices already checked stay visible with their date D27-272.

B5.11The provisioning key

Creating a hotel makes its invoice series and the home of all its bills, and its code appears in every address and every invoice number it will ever issue; the code cannot be changed afterwards (deploy/README.md). So it needs more than an owner's sign-in: it needs the provisioning key, and so does making somebody a group administrator.

  • The key is never in the database. Only its fingerprint is, so a copy of the database or a backup cannot be used to create anything (migration 029). It is not in the server's settings either: the person creating the hotel types it on the screen, so no ordinary session can create a hotel.
  • The owner makes it once on their own computer and stores its fingerprint on the server; the key itself goes straight into the owner's password manager and is never sent to anyone (docs/go-live/RELEASE-3OCT.md, steps F5 and F6) D21-54.
  • Without it, the database refuses to create a hotel, whatever the screen shows. A server with no key at all cannot have a hotel created on it by anyone.

Risks, and what was left out

Watch out

  • n8n carries every message, the mailbox login and the WhatsApp login. It is open to the internet behind its own sign-in D21-96; two-step sign-in there matters, and its encryption key is a master password (HANDOVER.md § 5).
  • Selling the system with n8n inside: n8n's licence allows "your own internal business purposes"; a copy Influvate sells may need n8n's commercial licence. A question for the lawyer (docs/PLATFORM-PLAN.md, R8).
  • A guest's WhatsApp reply reaches the leads app's inbox as a new enquiry until the "booked guest" step is used (B5.3).
  • Our own WhatsApp templates: on hold while the CRS's approved ones are used D27-266.
  • Online check-in is on for every hotel by default; a hotel can switch it off D27-281.
  • The leads app moved into the PMS: withdrawn; it keeps running on its own D27-100.
  • A payment gateway: excluded for now D17-57 D25-36.
