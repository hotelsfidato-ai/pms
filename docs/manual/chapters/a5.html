Chapter A5

Check-out

For reception, the hotel manager, the general manager, an Admin and the owner: everyone who may check guests out. How to take the last payment, print the bill, check a guest out, what happens when a guest leaves after the check-out time or before the booked day, how to give back money a guest paid too much, a foreign guest's next destination, the tax invoice that follows, and why every departure is checked out before the night audit.

A5.1Checking a guest out

Check-out ends the stay: the bill closes, the room goes to housekeeping as dirty, and the guest leaves the In house list. It is done on the guest's Bill tab, with the last button on it, Check out. The button opens only when the bill owes nothing.

To check a guest out

  1. Open Due out (A1.6), the guests leaving today, and click the guest. The panel opens on the Bill tab. For any other guest, one who leaves early or stays past the date, open In house (A1.5), click the guest and click Settle and check out, the orange button at the top of the panel. From Folios (A4.1) a guest also opens on the bill.
  2. Read the bill (A4.2). Total charges is everything posted, with tax.
  3. If money is owed, take the payment first (A5.2). If the guest asks to see the bill, print it (A5.3).
  4. For a guest from abroad, type their Next destination (A5.8).
  5. Click Check out, the full-width button at the foot of the bill. It turns orange once nothing is owed.
  6. The window Check … out? asks you to be sure (A5.4). Click Check out again.
  7. Depending on the date and the hour, the window may first ask why the guest is leaving late (A5.5) or early (A5.6).

When it is done, a message says “A5 Test Rane checked out” (the guest's name) with “Room 303 is now dirty and on housekeeping's board.” The panel stays open on the bill, and the guest's tax invoice is issued and printed by itself, two copies, unless the hotel has chosen otherwise (A5.9).

When Check out is grey

A button that cannot be used yet says why: the reason is written under it in red, and shows when you point at the button (Figure A4-04).

Watch out

  • Who may: the owner, an Admin, the GM, the hotel manager and reception. A role that sees bills but may not change bookings, such as the accountant, sees the bill without the button.
  • A stay with no bill at all shows a dash, never ₹0, and can be checked out without settling.
  • A guest stays In house until checked out, even after the departure date has passed (A1.5).
  • A group's rooms are checked out one at a time, each from its own booking. A room whose nights a group's company pays has those nights on the group's own bill, so the guest's Check out counts only the guest's own bill (A4.8). The group's bill is settled on the group's page (A2.6).
  • A guest who checks in and out on the same day owes that night, charged at check-in (A3.1): Check out waits until it is paid, or the charge is reversed with a reason (A4.4).

Why it works this way

Check-out is shown greyed with its reason, never hidden: a button that vanishes teaches nobody why. It asks "are you sure" first, because it closes the bill and sends the room to housekeeping: D27-117. It follows the one balance on screen, so it opens when a payment brings the bill to zero, and no longer greys with no reason for a few seconds after a payment: D27-313. A guest's own figures count only the guest's own bill: D27-226. A stay with no bill shows a dash: D17-16.

A5.2Taking the last payment

Check out needs the bill at zero. Take the payment with Settle: choose the Method, check the Amount (it is filled in with what is owed; change it only for a part payment) and click Take payment. A4.7 has every step and every warning; a stay with two bills settles each bill on its own (A4.8).

Cash is taken to the rupee

A room bill has paise in it, because GST is worked out on each night's rate, and nobody hands over paise. So when the method is cash, the cash taken is the nearest rupee, and the window shows how: under Paying it gives the exact amount, then Cash to take and Round-off, a plus or a minus, never more than 50 paise. For the demo's one night of ₹3,699 with its GST, for example, the bill is ₹3,883.95: Cash to take is ₹3,884.00 and Round-off is +₹0.05. The message afterwards reads “₹3,884.00 cash taken”.

  • The bill is credited with the exact amount, so it settles to ₹0 and Check out opens. The GST invoice keeps its exact figures.
  • The drawer expects what was really put in it: the rupees taken (A4.10).
  • UPI, cards and every other way pay the exact amount, with no round-off.
  • The restaurant's own Round off is a different thing (A8).

The payment shows under Payments received, for example “Received - UPI”, and the bill's closing row reads Settled. Check out turns orange as soon as the bill reads zero.

Watch out

  • A payment is reversed only by the owner, an Admin, the GM or the accountant (A4.4). Reverse a payment taken by mistake before the guest leaves.
  • More than the bill is held as the guest's credit, once the desk ticks Hold it as the guest's credit (A4.7). The bill is then overpaid, and Check out waits for the refund (A5.7).
  • Cash above ₹1,00,000 at once asks for the guest's PAN, or Form 97 (A4.7).

Why it works this way

Nobody hands over 37 paise, so the drawer was a few paise over at every count. Only cash is rounded, the bill is credited exactly and the invoice never changes: D27-317. The rule is the room bill's: the restaurant keeps its own Round off. More than the bill is warned of, then held as the guest's credit: D27-4, D27-11.

A5.3Printing the bill for the guest

The guest asks to see the bill before paying. On the Bill tab click Proforma bill. The window shows the print preview. Click Print: the bill opens on a page of its own, and you print it from there (A4.9).

  • It says “Proforma Bill”, with the booking's reference and the stay's dates and room under it, and the line “A proforma bill: what the stay comes to so far. It is not a tax invoice; the tax invoice is issued at check-out.”
  • It is laid out as the tax invoice: the hotel's address, GSTIN and PAN, the stay in two columns, every line with its HSN/SAC, its taxable value and each tax (drinks with their VAT, never CGST and SGST), the payments and the tax by rate.
  • At the foot: the totals and the Total, then Paid, any night To be posted tonight, and Balance due (or Overpaid).
  • The total is the exact figure, with no round-off.

Watch out

  • It is not a tax invoice. It carries the booking's reference and no invoice number; the numbered invoice is issued after check-out (A5.9).
  • Printing it closes nothing. If the guest is charged more, a new copy shows it.
  • A test hotel's bill is stamped “TEST · NOT VALID” and carries no GSTIN.

Why it works this way

The desk's print never says "Tax Invoice", so nobody takes it for the numbered tax invoice: D22-7; the button says what it opens: D24-25. Its total is the exact figure: D27-126. The interim bill became the proforma bill, laid out as the tax invoice: D27-344. The test hotel's mark: D27-296.

A5.4The Check out window

Check out asks first. Click Check out on the bill and a small window opens: Check A5 Test Rane out? (the guest's name), the room under it, and the line “The bill closes and the room goes to housekeeping as dirty.” Not yet closes the window and changes nothing. Check out finishes it.

  • The bill closes. Its lines stay as they are.
  • The room is released and goes to Needs cleaning on housekeeping's board (A6.2).
  • A loyalty member's points for the stay are added (A14.4).
  • The guest's tax invoice is issued and printed by itself, or offered, or left to Invoices, as the hotel has chosen (A5.9).

Watch out

  • If the server refuses, a message, “Could not check out”, says why, and the guest stays in house.
  • The server asks the same questions as the screen and decides: “₹… is still outstanding. Settle the folio, or transfer it to the city ledger, before checking out.” and “The guest has overpaid by ₹…. Refund it before checking out.”

Why it works this way

A check-out closes the bill and sends the room to housekeeping, and it used to go on one click: D27-117. A room is vacated and made dirty by the same step that releases it, so it reaches housekeeping's board the moment the guest walks out.

A5.5After the check-out time: Leaving after check-out time

A guest still in the room after the hotel's check-out time has kept it out of tonight's sale, and housekeeping cannot turn it round for an arrival already on the chart. So a late departure is written down. The hotel's check-out time is set on Settings, Check-out by (A17.11), for example 11:00.

The window Leaving after check-out time comes up when you confirm Check out, and only when all three are true:

  • the hotel's business date is on or after the departure date (A1.1);
  • the hour, on the hotel's own clock, is past its check-out time;
  • the stay is not a day use, which has its own hours.

It is asked once for a stay: a late departure already recorded, with its reason and its charge or its waiver, is not asked again or charged again.

To record a late departure

  1. Click Check out on the bill and confirm. The window Leaving after check-out time opens with the guest and the room. Its yellow note starts “Check-out here is 11:00.”
  2. Type Why. It is required: a flight time, a manager's say-so, a room not ready on arrival (“Late flight, agreed at reception”).
  3. Read the line under it. Where the hotel does not charge: “This property does not charge for a late departure, so nothing will be added. It is still recorded.” Where it does: “₹1,000 will be added to the folio. A manager can waive it - that is a decision about money and it is theirs to make.” The owner, the GM and a group administrator have a tick instead, Waive the ₹1,000 charge: “Your decision, recorded against your name. The late departure is still written down - waiving the money does not unwrite the fact.”
  4. Click Check out. If a charge was added, it goes on the bill under Room, with its GST, before the balance is looked at.

If a charge was added, the bill is no longer zero, so the check-out cannot finish. The window closes with “Recorded - now settle the bill” and the amount. Take the payment (A5.2), then click Check out again: it does not ask again and charges nothing more. If nothing is owed after the late departure (no charge, or it was waived), the guest is checked out at once, and the message says so: “The late departure is recorded. The property does not charge for one.”, “₹… added to the folio.” or “The late departure is recorded and the charge waived - both are on the stay.”

Watch out

  • Anyone at the desk records a late departure; only a manager waives the charge. The server asks again, and refuses a waiver from anybody else.
  • A guest who leaves before the departure date is not asked this at any hour and is never charged a late fee (A5.6). The date that counts is the business date, not the clock's.
  • A day use is never asked and never charged a late fee.
  • The check-out time and the late fee are the hotel's own settings (A17.9, A17.11). Where no fee is set, the window says so.

Why it works this way

The server used to refuse a late check-out without a reason, and the screen had no way to give one: press, read, press again. Now the window is the way through. Recording is an observation, waiving is a decision about money, so the waiver is a manager's. The question is asked only on or after the departure date, and once, so a guest who paid after it is not asked or charged again: D27-314. A day use is never late: D27-319. The late fee sits under Room on the bill: D27-223.

A5.6Leaving early

A guest who leaves before the booked departure date, by the hotel's business date, is leaving early, at any hour. They are never asked Leaving after check-out time and never charged a late fee. They are asked one other thing: why.

To check out a guest who is leaving early

  1. Find them on In house now (A1.5): a guest who leaves before the departure date is not on Due out. Click the guest and click Settle and check out.
  2. On the bill, Charges to come are the nights the night audit has not posted yet (A4.2). They are only shown, and Due counts them. But Check out needs only what is posted: take the payment for that (A5.2).
  3. When what is posted is paid, Check out opens, even with nights still to come.
  4. Click Check out. The window shows the leaving-early step above its usual line: “Booked to leave 13 Oct 2026. Today is 11 Oct 2026.” and “Nights not stayed: 1 - it is not charged and the room goes back on sale.” (For more than one night: “they are not charged”.) For a stay from a booking site it adds “Tell the booking site the guest left early.”
  5. Type Why are they leaving early? It is required, and kept on the booking: “Plans changed”. Check out in the window stays grey until it is typed.
  6. Click Check out.

What it changes

  • The stay now ends on the later of today and the day after the last night already posted, so the nights not stayed are not charged and go back on sale. The room's hold is shortened with it.
  • Nights already posted are never taken off. The night audit posts no more nights for the guest.
  • The reason is kept in the booking's notes: “Left early on 2026-10-11, booked to 2026-10-13: Plans changed”.
  • A guest who leaves on the day they arrive keeps their first night charged: it was posted at check-in (A3.1).
  • There is no fee for leaving early.

The step appears only when at least one night is freed. A day use never has it, and neither has a stay whose remaining nights are already charged.

Watch out

  • If the guest paid ahead for more than is posted, the bill reads overpaid before check-out, and Check out stays grey: “This bill is overpaid. Refund the difference first.” Give the money back (A5.7), then check out.
  • The dates are shortened for every stay. Money is given back only on a guest's own bill: a company's credit and a booking-site stay get a notice instead (A5.7).
  • For a booking-site stay the system does not tell the site. The desk does.
  • If you click Check out in the window before the step has appeared, the server refuses with “Booked to leave on …. 1 night is not stayed: say why the guest is leaving early.” Click Check out on the bill again and type the reason.
  • The business date, not the computer's clock, decides what "early" is (A1.1).

Why it works this way

Before this, an early leaver's dates were not shortened, so the nights not stayed stayed booked, and unused prepaid nights blocked the check-out with no way to refund them: D27-314. The step follows the night audit's own rule for what is charged, so a night posted on a group's master bill counts; nights go back on sale; there is no fee for leaving early; and the refund is a step of its own: D27-315. Leaving early is never a late departure: D27-314.

A5.7A guest who paid too much: Refund

A bill can owe the guest money: paid ahead for nights not stayed, or more than the bill held as the guest's credit (A4.7). It shows as Overpaid, and Check out stays grey: “This bill is overpaid. Refund the difference first.” The server says the same: “The guest has overpaid by ₹…. Refund it before checking out.” On Folios such guests come first (A4.1).

Under the bill's buttons a yellow step appears: “₹… is owed back to the guest.” Then one of three things:

  • The owner, an Admin, the GM and the accountant see a Refund button.
  • Anyone else reads: “Refunds are given by the owner, an administrator, the general manager or the accountant. Ask one of them.”
  • A notice, where the money is not refunded here. A company's credit: “This credit is the company's. It is settled with the company on the City ledger, not refunded to the guest here.” A booking-site stay with nothing paid at the hotel to give back (the booking site was paid): “A booking-site stay: the booking site refunds the guest. Tell the booking site; nothing is refunded here.”

A booking-site guest who paid the hotel, for example the whole stay by UPI at the desk and then left early, is refunded here like any guest, with the same people, the same ways and the same ₹20,000 cash rule. Only what was paid at the hotel goes back, and only by the ways it was paid there: Bill to company is never one. What the booking site was paid stays the booking site's to refund.

To give a refund

  1. Click Refund. The window Refund says “₹… is owed back on this bill”.
  2. Choose Back by: the way the guest paid, each with the most it may give back, for example “UPI - up to ₹…”. “The way the guest paid. Never cash for a card or UPI payment.” The Amount is filled in with the most it may be.
  3. For any way but cash, type the Reference: the UPI reference, the card approval code, the cheque number, the transfer number (UTR) or the wallet reference.
  4. Type Why. It is required, and stays on the refund.
  5. Click Give refund. The message is “₹… refunded” with the way. On the bill the line reads “Refunded - UPI (the reference)” under Payments and refunds, with a Refunded total, and the bill is back to ₹0. Check out opens.
  6. Click Print the refund receipt, under the step, for the guest to sign.

The receipt is a page of its own, with a Print button. It shows the hotel, the receipt's number and date, the guest, the booking and room, Amount refunded, Back by, Reference, Why and Given by, and two signature lines, the guest's and the hotel's. A refund that is reversed afterwards prints “This refund was taken back.”

Watch out

  • Cash only below ₹20,000 on a bill; above it, by bank transfer. The window says it: “Cash is given back only below ₹20,000 on a bill; above it, by bank transfer. This limit stands until the hotel's CA confirms it.” Two refunds of ₹15,000 count as ₹30,000. If bank transfer is not one of the hotel's ways of paying, the refusal says to add it in Settings first.
  • A cash refund comes out of the drawer of the person who gives it (A4.10).
  • The server refuses a way the bill was not paid by: “This bill was not paid by UPI. A refund goes back the way it was paid: …; never cash for a card or UPI payment.” And more than the limit: “At most ₹… goes back by UPI on this bill.” And no reason: “Say why it is being refunded. The reason stays on the refund.”
  • It is not the GST refund voucher: “This prints a plain refund receipt. The GST refund voucher (Rule 51) waits for the hotel's CA.”
  • For a company's credit, and a booking-site stay with nothing paid at the hotel, the notice is all the screen offers, and Check out stays grey while the bill as a whole is overpaid. Until a company's credit can go to its City ledger at check-out, a manager reverses the payment the same day (A4.4).

Why it works this way

A refund is its own line on the bill, back the way the guest paid, with a reference, the reason and the name of who gave it, so nothing is edited and money is never given back untraceably. It is for the four who may reverse a payment: D27-125, D27-315. Cash stays below ₹20,000 until the hotel's CA confirms the limit; the plain receipt stands in for the GST refund voucher until the CA says what it must hold: D27-315. A booking-site stay gives back what was paid at the hotel, like a guest's own bill; a company's credit to its City ledger comes after launch: D27-328.

A5.8A guest from abroad: the next destination

A foreign guest is reported when they leave too: Form III's departure goes to the portal within 24 hours of the guest leaving, and it carries where they are going next (A3.4). So on a foreign guest's bill, above Check out, is a box: Next destination, starred, with “Form III reports where a foreign guest goes next.” Check out stays grey until it is filled in. Type where they go, then check out as usual.

  • If it is missed, the server refuses: “Form III asks where a foreign guest is going next. Enter the next destination to check them out.”
  • A foreign adult recorded on the stay of an Indian guest (A3.3) is asked for too. The refusal comes when you press Check out, and the box opens: type where they go and check out again.
  • What you type goes on the guest's row and on every foreign adult's who has no next destination of their own.
  • Afterwards, on the Form III screen (A3.5), the Departure cell of each row takes the number the portal gives back for that person's departure. Complete details can add or change the next destination after check-out.

Why it works this way

Rule 17 asks the foreigner for the address they are going to at departure, so it is asked while they are still at the desk, and the check-out refuses without it: D27-25.

A5.9After check-out: the tax invoice

The proforma bill is not the tax invoice. Once the guest has left and the bill is settled, the numbered GST invoice is issued. What Check out does with it is the hotel's choice, on Settings › Front desk rules (A17.11):

  • Issue it and print it, by itself (the hotel starts on this). Check out issues the invoice and opens the print window with it, in two copies: “ORIGINAL FOR RECIPIENT” for the guest and “DUPLICATE FOR SUPPLIER” for the hotel's file. Choose the desk's printer and print. A hotel may choose one copy.
  • Offer an Issue tax invoice button: the bill shows Issue tax invoice where Check out was, and nothing is issued until it is clicked.
  • Nothing at check-out: the bill says “Not issued yet: this hotel issues the tax invoice on Invoices.”

At the desk, right after check-out (the hotel starts on this)

  1. Check the guest out (A5.1). The panel stays open on the bill and says “Issuing and printing the tax invoice…” for a moment.
  2. The print window opens with the invoice: the original and the duplicate, one after the other. Choose the desk's printer and click Print. It prints on the printer the computer already uses, on A4; nothing is set up for it in the PMS.
  3. Under Tax invoice the bill now shows Print or send with the invoice's number, for example Print or send 5EL/2026-27/00004, and the orange Print again. If the printer missed it, click Print again. To give it as a file, click Print or send and then Send invoice (A10.7), or choose Save as PDF in the print window.

A bill that still owes, or is overpaid, is not issued: the bill says why under Issue tax invoice; settle it and issue it there or on Invoices. A stay with two bills issues and prints each one, with who it is billed to (A4.8). Issuing is for reception, the hotel manager, the accountant, the GM, an Admin and the owner; anyone else sees “Not issued yet.” and who can. Cancelling an invoice with a credit note is only for the accountant, the GM, an Admin and the owner (A4.11).

Where the hotel offers the button

  1. After check-out, where Check out was, the bill says Tax invoice with the orange Issue tax invoice.
  2. Click it. The window Issue this tax invoice? says who it is billed to and the number it takes (Figure A5-13). Click Issue the invoice.
  3. The button becomes Print or send with the invoice's number. Click it: the tax invoice opens in a new tab, and its Print prints both copies.

Closing the panel without issuing leaves the bill on Invoices, ready to bill.

Later, on Invoices

A bill not invoiced at the desk waits on Invoices (Finance; or Find a screen). A4.11 has all of it, with the credit note. In short:

  1. Open Invoices. It lists the departed stays ready to bill, with how many.
  2. Click Issue on the stay. It is grey while the bill still owes (“The folio still carries a balance. Settle it, or transfer it to the city ledger, before invoicing.”) or is overpaid (“The folio is overpaid. Refund the difference before invoicing.”).
  3. The window Issue this tax invoice? says who it is billed to and the next number in the GST series. Click Issue the invoice. Keep it closes the window and changes nothing.

Watch out

  • An issued invoice is never edited: “An issued invoice is never edited: a wrong one needs a credit note.” Numbers come in sequence, so the series cannot gain a hole (A4.11).
  • The tax invoice goes by email by itself only when it is made out to the guest. One made out to a company, an agent or a booking site goes by the desk's Send invoice (A10.7).

Why it works this way

Asking "are you sure" before a legal number is taken, when it is asked for by hand: D27-117. A company's, an agent's or a booking site's invoice is not sent by itself: D27-284, D27-313. The tax invoice on the bill after check-out, with who may issue it unchanged: D27-341. Issued and printed at check-out by default, two copies, each hotel's choice: D27-344. GST's invoice for a service is in two copies, the original for the recipient and the duplicate for the supplier (CGST Rules, rule 48).

A5.10Before the night audit

A guest who has left must still be checked out, or the day cannot close. At the end of the day, Due out should be empty, or hold only guests who are really still here. When everyone has gone it says “Nobody departing today. Every room stays another night.” (A1.6).

  • In Night audit, the check Departures not yet checked out counts the guests due out today who are still checked in, and it stops the audit (A7.2).
  • The Run card then says “4 rooms are still showing due out. Check them out, or extend the stay - the audit will not decide which.” and Run night audit stays grey (A7.3).
  • For each guest: if they left, settle the bill and check them out. If they are staying on, use Change dates or guests (A2.8).

Why it works this way

A guest still due out at night either left and nobody checked them out, or is staying another night. The two lead to opposite charges, and only a person at the desk knows which, so the audit refuses and names the rooms: D27-305.
