Chapter A15

Rates, revenue and distribution

For the owner, the GM, the Revenue Manager and whoever reads prices. How to read the Rates calendar, change a price for a day or a run of days, set the add-ons every price is built from, close a date or set a minimum stay, read the Revenue outlook, set up the Channel manager to the booking sites through SU, and keep the companies and agents with their contracts.

A15.1The screens

Two parts of the Front office menu hold this chapter: Rooms and rates (what this hotel is selling) and Distribution (who sells it). You see the screens your role uses.

Two screens these depend on sit under Property setup: Booking sources, where each booking site gets its SU code, and Cancellation terms. The same company records also open from Companies and agents in Guests & CRM.

A15.2Reading the Rates calendar

Open Rates. It says “Each day's price, and how the day sold. The add-ons follow the base.” It opens on two weeks from the hotel's own business date.

  • The base is the grey line of each room type: what one guest pays for the room alone (room only, EP) that day. Under the price, “3 left” is the rooms of that type still to sell that day.
  • The rows under it are what the room really sells for that day: for two guests on room only, then CP, MAP, AP and AI. Where the room sleeps more, + per extra adult and + per extra child say what one more guest adds; where it sleeps no more, those rows are left out.
  • The arrows move two weeks at a time. Away from the business date, Today brings you back. Weekends and the hotel's holidays are shaded grey.

The key

On the calendarMeans
4,000 (bold)A base typed for this room type.
4,000 (grey)From the base room type: the base room type's base plus this type's difference (A15.4).
4,000 (grey italics)A price set before, covering two guests (A15.4).
-Not priced. Nothing that needs it is sold. Never read a dash as zero.
3 leftRooms of the type still to sell that day.

Paying rooms sold, ADR and Revenue are the paying rooms only, at the booked rate before tax: complimentary rooms and day use are left out. The occupancy under Paying rooms sold counts complimentary rooms, as the reports do, so it can be a little higher than the paying rooms alone. ADR is a dash on a day nothing was sold.

Under the calendar, links open the reports: Manager report, Monthly occupancy, Monthly statistics and Revenue outlook (Figure A15-05).

Watch out

  • On the demo hotel every price is still the old rack rate, so every base shows in grey italics and every meal plan shows the room-only figure. A hotel priced only by its old rack keeps selling every plan at that figure until a base and the add-ons are set (A15.4).
  • Everyone who reads prices sees the calendar; only the Owner, an Admin, the GM and the Revenue Manager can tap a price. The server asks the same question on every price it is sent.

Why it works this way

One screen for the price and how the day sold, in place of the old Pricing, Rate plans, Rate calendar and Rate performance: D27-188. The break-up rows under each room type and Set prices: D27-206; every room type always open: D27-237. Paying rooms sold beside one occupancy rule everywhere: D27-151.

A15.3Changing a price

Only the base is typed: what one guest pays for the room alone. Every plan and guest count on that day follows it, through the add-ons (A15.4). The line under the calendar says so: “Tap any price to change it, for that day or through a later day.”

One day, or from that day: tap the price

  1. Tap the room type's base on the day. The window is named after it: “Deluxe · base”.
  2. Type the Base per night in rupees. Or tick Free if it really is free.
  3. Leave Through on the same day for that day only, or choose a later day. The line under it says what it covers: “12 Oct 2026 only.” or “5 days, 12 Oct 2026 through 16 Oct 2026.”
  4. Tap Set base. The message says “Base set” with the room type and the days.

A run of days: Set prices

  1. Tap Set prices at the top of Rates.
  2. Choose the Room type.
  3. Choose From, the first day, and Through, the last day.
  4. Type the Base per night, or tick Free.
  5. Tap Set base. It stays grey until a price is typed or Free is ticked.

Watch out

  • No default price. A blank is not priced, and 0 is kept only when it is ticked Free: “0 is kept only when it is ticked Free. Leave it blank if nobody has priced it yet.”
  • A day that already has a base of its own also offers Clear, which takes that base away (“Base cleared”).
  • On a date that has a base, the old rack rate is no longer used there. Each plan is then the base plus its add-on, and a plan or a second guest whose add-on is a dash is not sold that day. Set the add-ons (A15.4) before the first base.
  • Where the rooms are derived from one base room type (A15.4), only that room type's base can be tapped; the others follow it.

A15.4How rates are built, and prices set before

Under the calendar, How rates are built shows the add-ons that ride on every base. Each is set once for the hotel, in rupees, with no dates:

  • Breakfast (CP), per guest, MAP, per guest, AP, per guest, All inclusive (AI), per guest: every guest pays the meal plan's add-on, children too.
  • Double occupancy (the second guest): what the second guest adds, adult or child.
  • Extra adult (from the third guest) and Extra child (from the third guest).

The card says: “A base covers one guest, room only. A dash is not set: nothing that needs it is sold.”

To set the add-ons, or derive the room types from one

  1. Tap Edit on How rates are built.
  2. Under Room types, choose Each room type has its own base, or Derived from one room type: “Derived: the others are the base room type plus a difference.”
  3. Derived: for each other room type, type how much it is more than the base, and choose ₹ or %. A percentage is rounded to the whole rupee.
  4. Type each add-on in rupees, or tick Free. Leave it blank where nobody has priced it.
  5. Tap Save. The message says “Every price follows the base and the add-ons.”

Prices set before

Prices typed before this way of pricing began are kept, under Prices set before: “Kept exactly as they were: each covers the guests it always did, and wins over the base on its dates.” A room type's old rack rate is listed there too, “Used only on dates no base covers.”

  1. Tap Remove beside the price.
  2. It asks first, naming the price. Tap Remove, or Cancel.

Why it works this way

A base plus add-ons set once, each room type its own base or derived from one by rupees or a percentage, and the calendar changing only the base: D27-188. The base covers one guest; the second guest is double occupancy; from the third, extra adult or child; meals per guest, children too; a percentage rounded to the rupee; a part nobody set is a dash and is not sold: D27-194. A hotel still on its old rack sells every plan at the room-only rack until a base and the meal add-on are set: D27-196. Zero only when ticked Free: D27-120.

A15.5Rooms left and the restrictions

Tap Rooms left at the top of Rates. Each room type, each day: the rooms still to sell in large figures, and under them “paying rooms sold / sellable”.

  • A day with nothing left shows yellow; a day on stop sell, red.
  • Complimentary and tentative rooms hold a room but are left out of the small figure; occupancy on the reports counts complimentary rooms.
  • A minimum or maximum stay shows on the day as its figures, such as “2–5”.

To close a date or set a minimum stay

  1. On Rooms left, tap the room type's day. The window is named “Deluxe · 17 Oct 2026”.
  2. Tap what applies: Stop sell (“Nothing may be booked at all”), Closed to arrival (“A stay may not begin here - protects a long weekend”), Closed to departure (“A stay may not end here”).
  3. Type Minimum nights or Maximum nights if needed.
  4. For a run of days, choose Through: “Apply the same rules to every day up to here.”
  5. Tap Apply. The message says “Applied” with the room type and the days.

Watch out

  • To take a rule off, clear everything and tap Apply: “Clearing everything removes the rule rather than storing a row that says nothing.”
  • Apply with Through sets the same rules on every day of the run, in place of what each day had.
  • A minimum above the maximum is refused: “A minimum of 3 nights and a maximum of 2 accepts no booking at all.”
  • A restriction is per room type, not per plan: a room not for sale is not for sale on any plan.
  • Restrictions go to the booking sites through SU (A15.8). A booking made at the desk is not checked against them: the desk decides.
  • Only the Owner, an Admin, the GM and the Revenue Manager can tap a day.

A15.6The Revenue outlook

Open Revenue outlook (under Rooms and rates, or the link under the Rates calendar). “What the coming ninety nights look like, and what they suggest. Nothing here changes a price - Rates does that.” A line at the foot says the same: “A suggestion never names a price, and nothing here sets one. Rates is where a rate is changed.”

What it suggests, and when

A night that isReads
14 days out or nearer, and 50% sold or less: Soft“Consider a lower rate for Sunday, 11 October 2026.”
85% sold or more: Filling well“Consider holding or raising the rate for …”
Anything else: On trackNothing.

Each suggestion's reason gives the figures, for example “2 days out, 47.06% sold, 9 of 17 rooms left.” The sold figures are the reports' own: complimentary rooms count, over the rooms that can be sold.

Nearby shows the lowest and middle price of the hotels nearby that night, and when they were checked, where Rivals' prices is switched on in Settings (Modules and connections). Tap it for the list. They are other hotels' public prices, never a price this hotel sells at.

  • Dates worth a look lists the soft nights, emptiest first, then the nearest. With none soft, it lists the coming nights.
  • New 1 day, New 7 days, New 30 days: how many of the rooms sold for that night were booked in that long, counted back from the property's own day. A dash where the records do not reach back that far, as on the demo.

Why it works this way

It shows and it suggests; a person sets every price: D17-56, D18-15. Soft is 14 days out and 50% sold or less, filling well 85% or more, as built, to be reviewed after a month of real bookings: D22-14. The nearby hotels' lowest public price per night, 30 nights ahead, checked twice a week: D27-270.

A15.7The Channel manager

Open Channel manager, under Distribution. “The connection between this system and the booking sites, through SU. Nothing is sent or taken until SU is set up and this hotel is switched on.” The GM sees what it does, what it still needs, and the bookings the sites sent; the Owner and an Admin also see the SU setup (A15.8).

What it doesIn the screen's words
Rooms left, sent outSell a room here and SU tells the booking sites, so the same room is not sold twice.
Prices and closures, sent outThe prices and closures on Rates go to SU as they change. A date with no price goes to SU closed.
Bookings, taken inA booking site's booking arrives as a booking, and its cancellation cancels it. Anything it cannot read waits for a person, with the reason.
  • What it still needs before it goes live, as the screen lists it: SU's test account and its keys in the server's settings; SU's certification; the room types and meal plans matched to SU's ids; each booking site's SU code, picked on Booking sources; this hotel switched on in Settings; and one rate everywhere or a rate per channel, still to be decided.
  • Booking sites and their SU codes: each booking site recorded on Booking sources, with its code. “No SU code: its bookings wait for a person” or “…not one of SU's codes: its bookings wait for a person” means the code is to be picked on Booking sources.

Bookings from the booking sites

Every booking SU has sent is listed as it arrived, with the guest, the site, the dates, the rooms, the total and what became of it: Booked, Changed, Cancelled, Refused: no room free, Needs somebody. Until the first arrives it says “SU has not sent a booking yet.”

  1. A booking Refused: no room free was for a room no longer free: it was not made here, and the site was told there is nothing left. Settle it with the site and the guest.
  2. A booking that Needs somebody says why underneath. Deal with it.
  3. Tap I have dealt with this. It is marked Dealt with.

Why it works this way

The channel manager is SU: D17-66, built D27-178. A booking for a room no longer free is not made, and listed for the GM to settle: D17-80. Each booking site's SU code is chosen on Booking sources, nothing hard-wired: D27-183. A date with no price goes to SU closed: D17-81.

A15.8Setting up SU (the Owner and an Admin)

The SU setup is on the Channel manager, in the order it is needed. Nothing is suggested or filled in from a name: each id is typed, each SU type is picked. Saving sends nothing: sending is its own button.

  1. SU login. Until the owner adds SU's keys to the server's settings, it reads Not set up. Then tap Check the login: “SU accepted the login.”
  2. This hotel on SU. The address, phone and email come from the hotel record (the Properties screen); the PIN code and map point from Settings, on the Property tab. Type the SU hotel id (up to 20 letters, digits and hyphens), the Country (its two-letter code), the contact's first and last name and the Description. Tap Save hotel details.
  3. Room types. For each room type, type its SU room id, pick its SU room type, type Children, at most, tick Send to SU, and tap Save with its name.
  4. Meal plans. The same for each meal plan: SU rate plan id, SU meal plan, Send to SU, Save.
  5. Send to SU. It lists what SU still needs first. When nothing is missing, tap Send to SU: it sends the hotel, its room types and meal plans marked Send to SU, and no prices or rooms left.
  6. Rooms left, prices and closures. Tap Send a year when the hotel first goes on SU. After that only what changed is sent, on its own every minute once the hotel is switched on; Send what changed sends it now.

Watch out

  • A room type with no rack rate cannot be listed on SU.
  • A wrong match oversells the wrong room type: check each SU id against SU's own.
  • Where a meal plan has no price of its own, the room type's rack rate goes to SU, so the room keeps selling; where there is no price at all, the date goes to SU closed. The card then says how many nights are selling at the rack rate, and from when.
  • On a server using SU's live account, nothing is sent for a hotel that is not switched on.

Why it works this way

The Owner and an Admin set SU up; the GM sees the status page: D17-85. The rack rate goes where a meal plan has no price of its own, instead of closing the date: D17-92; a date with no price at all goes closed: D17-81.

A15.9Contracts: companies and their rates

Open Contracts, under Distribution: “Companies and agents, and the rates each has agreed with this hotel.” Each row is a company: its GSTIN, city, credit, and its contract here. A travel agent shows Travel agent beside its name. Tap a row to open the company.

To add a company

  1. Tap Add company. “One record for the group: the same company at every hotel.”
  2. Type the Name, choose the Kind: Corporate or Travel agent.
  3. Type the GSTIN from the company's GST certificate (it prints on the company's tax invoices), the Billing address, City, State and PIN code.
  4. Type the Contact person, Phone and Email.
  5. Switch on Credit allowed only if the company may pay later: “A bill may be sent to the company to pay later (Bill to company).” Then type Credit days and Credit limit (₹), or leave them blank.
  6. Tap Add company. The company opens.

Reception adds a company with its name, GSTIN and address only, for the tax invoice: “Credit and contracts are set by the owner, an administrator or the GM.”

To add a contract (the Owner, an Admin or the GM)

  1. Open the company and tap Add contract. “The contract in force on the arrival date prices the booking.”
  2. Type a Name (the company and the year), and choose Valid from and Valid to.
  3. Under Priced by, choose:
    • Fixed rates: for each room type and meal plan, the Single (₹) and Double (₹) rate. “Per room per night, before tax. Single is one guest, double two; a third guest pays the extra-person price on Rates. Blank is not agreed: the booking takes the day's price.”
    • Or Discount off the day's rate: the Discount (%), taken off the price on Rates for the arrival date.
  4. For a travel agent, type the Commission (%). Add Notes if needed.
  5. Tap Add contract. “The next booking for this company is priced by it. Bookings already made keep their rate.”
  • A contract shows on the company with its dates, In force while it runs, and Edit.
  • When a booking names the company, its contract in force on the arrival date prices every night; the booking line says “Contract: …”. A rate typed on the booking stays as typed.

Watch out

  • A contract rate is an amount above 0: “A contract rate is an amount above 0. Leave it blank where none is agreed.” A discount is above 0 and at most 100.
  • A contract rate is always before GST: the booking's “Rate includes GST” tick does not apply to it (“Contract rates are before GST”).
  • Credit days and the credit limit are shown, not enforced, until the company's payments are recorded.

Why it works this way

Fixed rates per room type and meal plan, single and double, or a percentage off the day's rate; the contract in force on arrival prices every night; no contract figure is the day's price; the Owner, an Admin and the GM keep contracts: D27-184. A contract rate is never split for GST: D27-233.
