Chapter A2

Bookings

For reception and whoever takes bookings. How to find a booking, take one on the phone, book and check in a walk-in, book several rooms as a group, hold a booking while it waits for the hotel, change the dates or the room, and cancel or void a booking. The last sections are for the owner: the cancellation terms, the booking sources and CRS bookings.

A2.1The bookings screens

The Bookings part of the menu holds these screens. The owner, an Admin, the GM, the hotel manager and reception may make, change, cancel and void bookings; other roles may only look.

ScreenWho uses itWhat it is for
All bookingsReceptionEvery booking on the file, in any state: find one and open it.
GroupsReception, managersThe bookings of more than one room: their rooms, who pays, the lead guest (A2.6). Not in reception's menu: open it with Find a screen (A0.6).
CRS bookingsReception; the owner and an Admin for its connectionBookings the CRS sold for this hotel, and what became of each (A2.11).
New bookingWhoever may make a bookingOne page to take a booking, give it a room, or check the guest in (A2.2 to A2.5).

To find a booking

  1. Open All bookings. It says “Every booking on the file, in any state.” with the business date, and how many are on file.
  2. Type in Guest, reference or room: part of the guest's name, the reference, a room number, or three or more digits of the phone.
  3. Or tap a tile to see one state only: All, Due in, In house (guests due out today too), Checked out, Cancelled, void & no-show. Each tile shows its count; the one chosen is dark.
  4. Click a row. The booking opens in the stay panel (A1.4), where More options holds the changes in A2.8 and A2.9.

The list runs from the latest arrival down. Its columns: Room, Guest (with the reference under the name), Type, Plan, Source, Arrival, Departure, Nights, Status, Rate. The states are Due in (orange), In house (blue), Due out, Checked out, Cancelled, No show, Void, and Waiting for the hotel (A2.7).

Watch out

  • Nothing is ever deleted. A cancelled, voided or no-show booking stays on the file under its own tile.
  • A booking waiting for the hotel is under All, not under Due in: it is not confirmed yet.
  • Nothing found? Try a shorter search: a surname, or the last few digits of a reference.

A2.2A booking on the phone: the guest and the stay

New booking is one page: “One guest, one set of nights. Save it, give it a room, or check them in.” The guest, the dates, the room and its price, the ID and an advance are on the page; the rest folds away. On the right, The booking adds it up as you type.

To take a booking

  1. Open New booking. It opens on today's business date, one night, one room.
  2. Type the name in Guest or company. It is the only guest detail that must be typed.
  3. Type the Phone, and the Email if the guest gives one. If the system knows that phone or email, a line under them names the guest and how often they have stayed with the group, and asks “same person?”. Press Same person only if it is them (A10.6); nothing is joined until you do.
  4. Ask the guest if they agree to WhatsApp, and switch on Guest agreed to WhatsApp if they do.
  5. Set Arrival and Departure. The number of nights shows under Departure. The night of departure is not charged.
  6. Choose the Booking source: how the booking reached us. Nothing is chosen for you (except Walk-in for a walk-in, A2.5).
  7. Choose the Room type and the Meal plan, and set Rooms, Adults and Children.
  8. Leave Rate empty to use the price on Rates, or type the rate agreed with the guest.
  9. Pick a Room now if you want to; Assign later is fine.
  10. Read the price under the line and the total on the right, and tell the guest.
Meal plan, as the list says itMeans
EP · Room onlyThe room, no meals.
CP · Room + breakfastThe room and breakfast.
MAP · Room + breakfast + one mealThe room, breakfast and one more meal.
AP · Room + all mealsThe room and every meal.
AI · Everything charged to the roomAll inclusive.
  • The price line reads, for example, “₹3,699.00 a night + ₹184.95 tax (5%) · 2 nights”. A rate you typed adds “, agreed”.
  • “7 left” is how many rooms of that type are free on every night of the stay. All 7 left books all of them. Booking more than are left is allowed: the count then adds “this books more than that”. You decide.
  • Another room (top of the Stay card) adds a second line, for a second room type. The bin takes a line away. More than one room in all makes a group (A2.6).
  • Complimentary: no charge, nothing posted. Only the owner, an Admin or the GM can tick it; for others it reads Complimentary (owner, admin or GM only) and cannot be ticked.
  • Departure the same day as Arrival is a day use: type the day's price on the line. It is charged at check-in.
  • A booking site's source shows Prepaid on the booking site, ticked. Untick it when the guest pays at the hotel: the advance is then asked before check-in.

Watch out

  • No price, no total. If Rates has no price for this party, the line says “No price for …” and the total stays a dash: “Set it on Rates, or type a rate on the line.” The system never guesses a price.
  • The first night's price is kept for every night. If the calendar's price changes later in the stay, the line says so: “The first night's price is kept for every night; the calendar differs later.”
  • The booking source cannot be filled in later. Ask how the guest found you before you save.
  • WhatsApp unticked: messages go by email if there is one, else not at all.

Why it works this way

One page with three buttons, for booking, giving a room and checking in: D27-158. The WhatsApp tick stays in sight because the voucher and the check-in code need it; offers and “woman travelling alone” fold away: D27-176. Rate includes GST, a tick per line, off unless ticked: D27-187, D27-199. A day use's price is typed every time: D27-229. Prepaid on the booking site: D27-246. A returning guest is only suggested; a person confirms: D21-2.

A2.3The ID, the advance, the folds and the three buttons

Under the room line are the ID and the advance, then four folded sections, then the three buttons.

  • ID. For a booking still to come it says “The document is taken when they check in.”: only the Nationality is asked now. For an arrival today, Document and Number are asked too, for Check in now (A2.5). A nationality other than IN puts the stay on the Form III register (A3).
  • Advance. Money the guest pays now: the Amount and how it was Paid by. It is held as the guest's credit until the nights are charged, and the total on the right takes it off.
FoldWhat is in it
More about the guestGuest agreed to offers, Woman travelling alone (placed away from stairwells and service corridors; not announced at the desk), Title, Address, City, State, PIN code, Country.
Booking detailsThe booking: Confirmed or Waiting for the hotel (A2.7). Expected check-in and check-out, if they said. Business source (the account or agent behind it), Market segment, Sales person. Discount.
CompanyBill to: Guest or Company. For a company, its GSTIN is shown from its record; the tax invoice prints it.
NotesNotes on the booking.

Company and Notes can each be switched off for a hotel in Settings. A hotel can also make some fields required, such as the phone or the market segment; the reason under the buttons then names the missing one, for example “The phone number is needed for a booking at this hotel.”

  • Discount. Up to 10% by hand, with a reason. ₹ off a night, with a reason. More than 10% only through a named Promotion.

The three buttons

ButtonWhat it does
Save bookingSaves the booking. A room picked on the line is given to it too.
Save and assign roomThe same save, once a room is picked on the line. Until then it says “Pick a room.”
Check in nowSaves the booking, takes the advance if one is typed, then checks the guest in, through the same check-in as Arrivals (A3), so an advance the hotel asks before check-in (A3.6) is already paid. Only for an arrival today, one room, with a room picked.

The filled (orange) button is the job the page was opened for: Save booking on the phone, Check in now for a walk-in. A button that cannot be used says why under the buttons, for example “Check in now: Check in now is for an arrival today.” or “Type the guest's name.”

Watch out

  • “Not booked” with a reason means nothing was written: fix what it names and save again.
  • If the booking is saved but a later step is refused (the room, the check-in or the advance), the booking stands, and the next screen says which step and why (A2.4).
  • Several rooms? Check in now checks in one room at a time: “save the booking, then check each room in from Arrivals.”

Why it works this way

The three buttons, Company and Notes switchable in Settings: D27-158, D27-163. The advance held as the guest's credit: D27-4. Discounts, 10% by hand with a reason, more only by a named promotion: D27-36. The hotel's default guarantee and required fields: D27-260.

A2.4Booked: the reference, the guest's page and the voucher

After Save booking the page shows Booked with the guest's name: the reference of each room and its room type, then what to do next, in the order you do it on the phone.

  • Send voucher goes once. After that the button is replaced by “Voucher sent” with the day, the time and who sent it. It goes to the guest by WhatsApp or email, as the WhatsApp tick decides (A10.7).
  • Where nothing can be sent, the buttons are greyed and say why. On the demo hotel the sentence is “Sending is not set up yet. Bills and vouchers print and download as usual; nothing goes by WhatsApp or email until …”.
  • Check in now shows here too for a guest arriving today, one room, not yet checked in.
  • Open the booking opens it in All bookings (a group: the group's page, A2.6). Another booking clears the page for the next call.
  • A room picked but not given says “No room given: … Assign one from Arrivals.” An advance that did not go through says “Advance not taken: … Take it on the bill.”

Why it works this way

One voucher, recorded so it never goes twice: D27-35. View voucher sends nothing: D27-187. The check-in link beside the voucher: D27-105. Messages go to guests only as A10.7 describes.

A2.5A walk-in: booked and checked in at once

A guest at the desk with no booking. Start from the room, not from the menu.

  1. Open Rooms (A1.7) and tap a free room. New booking opens on that room: today, one night, source Walk-in. Its heading says “A walk-in into room 301. Check them in, or save the booking.”
  2. Type the guest's name and phone, as in A2.2. Check the room type, meal plan, adults and the price.
  3. Under ID, choose the Document and type its Number. Check in now records them; Save booking keeps only the nationality.
  4. If the room is being given before the hotel's check-in time, type Why the room is being given early.
  5. Take an advance if the guest pays now.
  6. Tap Check in now, the filled button.

Watch out

  • Check in now uses the same check-in as Arrivals, with all its checks: the room must be ready and free, the ID complete, a foreign guest's Form III filled (A3). If it refuses, the booking still stands, and the page says “Not checked in: … Check them in from Arrivals.”
  • An empty room opened from the Calendar for a later night is an ordinary booking of that room, not a walk-in: no Walk-in source, and Save booking is the filled button.

Why it works this way

The walk-in became this one page, opened from an empty room: room, today, one night, source Walk-in: D27-163.

A2.6A group: several rooms on one booking

A wedding, a conference, a family: one guest, one set of dates, several rooms. Book it once; more than one room becomes a group by itself.

  1. On New booking, set Rooms to the number of rooms (or add lines with Another room for other room types).
  2. Type a Group name, such as “Sharma Wedding, March”. Left empty, it is named after the guest.
  3. The booking on the right says “2 rooms are booked as one group.” Save it.
  4. Each room gets its own reference. Open the booking opens the group's page.

The Groups screen

Groups lists every group: its name, rooms, room-nights and dates, and two figures that are never added together: Rooms owe (the guests' own bills) and Host owes (the host's account; a dash when there is none). Here and coming shows the groups in the house or due; All adds the past ones. Tap a group to list its rooms, then Open the group.

One group: the host, the guests, the rooms

  • The host. “No host account. Every room settles its own.” until someone presses Open a host account. Then the rooms can be put on the host's account, and the page gets the card The host's account (below).
  • The guests. What the rooms owe on their own bills, across all of them.
  • Host pays. Tick a room to put its accommodation on the host's account; untick to give it back. It changes only the nights not yet charged.
  • Lead guest. The flag marks the guest who gets the group's one confirmation and its review request. It starts on the room booked first; tap another room's box to change it.

The host's account: taking the money, and the invoice

Once a host account is open, a card The host's account comes under the rooms. It says who the bill is made out to: “Billed to Sharma Wedding. Every line says which room it is for.” That is the company the group is booked for; with none, the guest who leads the group; with neither, the group's name. Each night routed to the host is a line, with its date and its room. Until there is one: “Nothing has been charged to the host yet. Route a room above, and tonight’s audit will post it here.”

  1. To take money: tap Settle on the card; it is grey while nothing is owed. The window is the one a guest's bill has: “Settle bill”, Method, Amount, Take payment, and any of the hotel's ways of paying will do. Bill to company moves the amount to the company's city ledger (A4). It is refused when the group is billed to no company: “… is not billed to a company, so its bill cannot go to the city ledger. Take the payment another way.” Over the company's credit limit it needs a reason from the owner, an Admin or the GM. More than the host owes is kept only if you tick Hold it as the host's credit.
  2. To invoice: tap Issue tax invoice. It stays grey, and its reason shows when you point at it, until every room has left and nothing is owed: “Every room checks out first: a night still to come would land on this bill.”, “The bill still carries a balance. Settle it, or send it to the city ledger, before invoicing.” or “The bill is overpaid. Refund the difference before invoicing.” The window asks “Issue this tax invoice?”, says who it is billed to and the number it will take. Tap Issue the invoice, or Keep it. The card then shows Tax invoice with its number, which opens it.

Both buttons are shown only to a role that may take payments or issue invoices. Nothing sends the host's invoice by itself, and its page has no Send invoice.

Watch out

  • A group's rooms are given later, from Arrivals or the Calendar's Assign room: the Room box is not on the line when it books more than one room.
  • Ticking Host pays before the host account is open is refused: “This group has no open master folio yet. Open one, and the room charges will go to it.”
  • Nights already charged stay on the bill they were posted to. Moving one is a correction on the bill, with a reason (A4).
  • Host pays moves the room's accommodation only. Food and drink stay on the bill of the guest who signed for them.

Why it works this way

Two accounts, never one total: the host and the guests are different people asked for different money, and a sum would be a number nobody can be asked to pay. Who leads a group: the room booked first that still stands, unless the desk picks another: D22-52. The host's account is settled and invoiced on the group's page, with the same Settle and the same question before an invoice number is taken as a guest's bill: D27-305.

A2.7Waiting for the hotel

A booking the hotel has not accepted yet: the room is held, and nothing goes to the guest. Bookings from the CRS arrive this way (A2.11). The desk can make one too.

  1. On New booking, open Booking details and choose Waiting for the hotel (Figure A2-05). It says “Waiting holds the room and sends the guest nothing until it is confirmed.”
  2. Save booking. The page says Held, waiting for the hotel.
  3. When the hotel accepts it, tap Confirm the booking: here, or later in the booking's More options. The message says “Confirmed” and “the voucher can go now.”
  • Confirming confirms every waiting room booked with it.
  • While it waits, New booking neither gives it a room nor checks it in: “A booking waiting for the hotel gets its room once it is confirmed.” There is no voucher for it either: “Only a confirmed booking still to arrive has a voucher.”
  • Not accepted? More options, Cancel this booking. The window says “It was waiting for the hotel and was never confirmed: no charge, and nothing goes to the guest.” Type Why and tap Cancel the booking. The room is free at once.

Why it works this way

A booking waits with its room held; the hotel confirms; then one voucher goes: D27-35. A waiting booking is cancelled directly, by the same people who may cancel a confirmed one: D27-64.

A2.8Changing a booking: More options

Open the booking (All bookings, Arrivals, a room tile) and tap More options. The choices are listed by when they happen; an option you cannot use is grey and says why.

To change the dates or the number of guests

  1. More options, Change dates or guests. The window is called “Amend A2 Test Sharma” and says the stay as it is.
  2. Change Arrival, Departure, Adults or Children. Once the guest has arrived, the arrival cannot change.
  3. Read the line under them: “2 → 3 nights · ₹11,097 of room charges at ₹3,699 a night”.
  4. Tap Save. The message says “Stay amended” and the change: “A2 Test Sharma: 2 nights becomes 3.”
  • The room held for the booking moves with it. If the room is taken for a night being added, nothing changes at all, and the message says which.
  • Added nights are charged at the rate already agreed. Changing the rate is separate (Change rate, or the bill).
  • A night already charged cannot be dropped here: correct it on the bill first (A4). A stay is at most 90 nights.

To move a guest in the house to another room

  1. More options, Move to another room. The window says where they are now: “Currently in 209.”
  2. Type To which room.
  3. Set From which night: the first night in the new room. It starts on the business date.
  4. Choose Why: Guest asked to move, Upgrade, Downgrade, Something wrong with the room, or Putting a group together.
  5. Type a New rate per night only if the price changes. Left empty, nothing about the money changes.
  6. Tap Move. The message names the new room and how the nights are split between the two.
  • Moving changes no charge on its own. Nights before tonight stay with the old room. A new rate applies from tonight; to change a night already billed, correct it on the bill, with its reason (A4).
  • Take them out of the room without assigning another leaves the booking standing with no room: “Out of the room”.

Why it works this way

Why a guest moved is kept, because “something wrong with the room” is the question asked when a complaint arrives a week later. A move is a change of room, not of price: the rate is a separate decision.

A2.9Cancel, void and no-show

Three ways a booking does not happen. None of them deletes it.

WordMeansWhat it costs
CancelledThe guest booked, then cancelled.What the booking's cancellation terms keep, on the guest's bill.
VoidIt should never have existed: entered twice, wrong property, a test.Nothing. Left out of revenue and out of cancellation figures.
No showA confirmed guest never came.Marked by the night audit (A7); the terms decide what is kept.

To cancel a confirmed booking

  1. Open the booking. More options, Cancel this booking. The window says “The guest cancelled. This room only; the terms it was booked under decide the charge.”
  2. Read the first line: what the terms charge, before you cancel. For example “This booking carries no cancellation terms, so nothing is charged.”
  3. Type Why, such as “Guest changed plans”.
  4. Tap Cancel the booking. Keep it closes the window and changes nothing.
  • Inside the terms' free window: “nothing is charged.” Otherwise the window says how much is kept and that it goes on the guest's bill; an advance already paid stays on the bill.
  • The owner, an Admin or the GM may switch on Waive the charge. Nothing then goes on the bill; the reason is kept with it.
  • It cancels this room only. For a group, cancel each room that is cancelled.

To void a booking

  1. More options, Void this booking. The window says “For one that never happened. Use Cancel if the guest cancelled.”
  2. Type Why: “Entered twice”, “wrong property”, “test booking”.
  3. Tap Void. The message says it “is on the record as never having happened.” It holds no room from then on.

Watch out

  • Cancel and Void are only for a booking that has not arrived. A guest in the house is checked out instead (A5): “They have arrived. Check them out instead.”
  • A booking with money on its bill cannot be voided: the system refuses and says why. Cancel it, so the terms apply.
  • A guest who has not arrived by the night audit becomes a no-show. Check in or cancel your arrivals before it runs.

Why it works this way

Cancelled and void mean opposite things to a report: a cancellation happened and measures demand; a void never happened, and counting it would inflate the figures the hotel sets its policy from. The terms are copied onto each booking when it is made, so a booking keeps the terms it was sold under even if the hotel changes them later. A waiting booking is cancelled with no charge: D27-64.

A2.10For the owner: cancellation terms and booking sources

Two screens in Property setup decide what a booking carries.

Cancellation terms

Cancellation terms: “What a cancellation or a no-show costs at this property.” A hotel starts with none: “Until a policy exists, the night audit charges nothing on a no-show.” The owner, an Admin, the GM or the revenue manager writes them.

  1. Tap New policy.
  2. Type its Name, what the front desk will call it (“Flexible”).
  3. Type Free cancellation until: hours before arrival. Empty means no free window.
  4. Choose what A cancellation retains and what A no-show retains: Nothing, The first night, The whole stay, A percentage of the booking, or A fixed amount.
  5. Tap Save policy. Use for new bookings makes it the one new bookings carry.

Bookings already taken keep the terms they were made under.

Booking sources

Booking sources is the list New booking's Booking source is chosen from. “The list is the group's”: a source added here appears at every hotel. “The commission is this property's”: what this hotel agreed, copied onto each booking as it is taken. Add a source adds one. Commission on a source sets this hotel's per cent; blank means no rate has been agreed, which is not the same as 0. The owner, an Admin or the GM sets it.

A2.11CRS bookings

CRS bookings: “Bookings the CRS sold for this hotel, and what became of each”. A booking a salesperson makes in the CRS for this hotel arrives within five minutes as a booking waiting for the hotel, with the room held (A2.7). The desk confirms it. A change made in the CRS afterwards shows here as a note for the desk, with Done; nothing is changed by itself.

The owner and an Admin also see Connection to the CRS: the source CRS bookings are filed under, each CRS room type's match here, and Read bookings from the CRS. On the demo hotel it says “The CRS login is not in the server's settings yet, so nothing is read.”
