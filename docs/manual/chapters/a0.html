Chapter A0

Getting started

For your first day. How to sign in, how the screen is laid out, how to find any screen, and what happens when the internet drops.

A0.1Signing in and out

Fidato PMS runs in a web browser, on a desk computer, a tablet or a phone. Nothing needs to be installed. Your property gives you an email and a password. Use only your own: everything you do is saved under your name.

To sign in

  1. Open the browser and go to pms.fidatohotels.com.
  2. Type your email in Email.
  3. Type your password in Password. To see what you typed, click the eye at the end of the box (Show password). Click it again to hide it. It starts hidden because the desk screen faces the lobby.
  4. Click Sign in. A small spinner turns on the button while it works. Then your first screen opens (A0.2).

When you open the system on a device where you are already signed in, the screen says “Checking your session…” for a moment, then opens where you were.

Watch out

  • “That email and password do not match.” The message is the same whether the email or the password is wrong, so check both. (A different message for each would tell a stranger which emails belong to staff.)
  • “No active staff record for this account.” Your password was right, but your account is switched off or not set up at any property. Ask your general manager.
  • Forgot your password? The page says it: “Trouble signing in? Your general manager can reset your password from the People screen.” (On a phone the line is under the form and stops at “reset your password”.)
  • “Set a longer password”. If your role was raised to owner, Admin or General Manager and your password is shorter than 10 characters, this box appears right after you sign in. It cannot be closed. Type a new password and click Set it. Everybody else needs at least 8 characters.
  • The first sign-in on a device needs the internet. After that, if the line drops, a reload keeps you signed in (A0.8).

To sign out

  1. Click your name at the top right. This is the account menu.
  2. Click Sign out. The sign-in page opens.

Closing the browser or the tab does not sign you out. On a desk computer that other people use, always sign out when you leave.

Watch out

  • Signing out clears the lists kept on the computer, so the next person does not open on your screens.
  • If you made changes with no connection and they are not sent yet, they stay on the computer, under your name. The sign-in page says “1 change is kept on this computer and will be sent when you sign in here again.” They go when you sign in on this computer again, not on another one.

Two-step sign-in: a code from your phone as well

With two-step sign-in on, signing in takes your password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, both free). Somebody who learns your password still cannot get in without your phone. Anybody may switch it on for themselves. The owner may also make it required for the owner, Admins, GMs and Accountants (A17.13); then the system asks you to set it up before anything else.

To set it up

  1. Put Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your phone, from the phone's app store.
  2. In the system, click your name at the top right, then Two-step sign-in, then Set it up.
  3. In the app, add an account and scan the square code on the screen. If the phone cannot scan it, choose “enter a setup key” in the app and type the key shown under the code.
  4. Type the 6 digits the app now shows in Code from the app and click Go on.
  5. Ten recovery codes appear. Each one lets you in once if your phone is lost or not with you. Write them down or print them, keep them away from the phone, and click I have kept them. They are never shown again.

To sign in with it

  1. Sign in with your email and password as always.
  2. The Two-step sign-in page opens. Open the app on your phone and type the 6 digits it shows for this account.
  3. Click Go on. Your first screen opens.

Without the phone, click Use a recovery code instead and type one of your ten. Each works once. The code is asked once per sign-in on each device, not on every screen.

Watch out

  • “That code is not right. The app shows a new one every 30 seconds: type the one showing now.” Type the code showing now. A code already used is not taken again; wait for the next one.
  • “Five wrong codes in a quarter of an hour...” Wait 15 minutes, then try again. Every code typed is kept on record.
  • Lost your phone and your recovery codes? The owner, or an Admin at your hotel, switches two-step sign-in off for you on the People screen (A16.6), with a reason. Then you set it up again on your new phone.
  • “Your role needs two-step sign-in. Set it up to go on.” The owner made it required for your role. The page shows the same three steps as above; nothing else opens until it is done.
  • Your account menu's Two-step sign-in says whether it is on for you and how many recovery codes are left.

Why it works this way

Longer passwords for the people who reach the most: D27-243 (10 characters for the owner, Admin and GM, 8 for others) and D27-245 (a person given one of those roles sets a 10-character password at their next sign-in). Clearing the computer at sign-out was one of the four security fixes the owner asked for before the release: D27-88. Unsent work stays under the name of the person who did it, because the bill and the ledger never forget who made an entry (the owner, 28 September). Two-step sign-in is the safety work the owner put before every feature, D27-384; built in our own system, because Firebase's needs a paid plan, and off until the owner requires it, so switching it on locks nobody out: D27-396.

A0.2Your first screen and the Start here card

After signing in you land on your first screen. For most people it is the Dashboard. It can also be Rooms or Calendar. Your general manager sets it for each person, and you can change your own.

To change where you start

  1. Click your name at the top right.
  2. Under Start on, click Dashboard, Rooms or Calendar. A tick shows the one chosen.
  3. The message “You will start on Rooms. From your next sign-in, on any device.” confirms it.

Some people have no Dashboard, because it shows the hotel's revenue and their day starts at their own work. Unless they chose Rooms or Calendar themselves, they land on their own work: housekeeping on Housekeeping, the chef and the kitchen manager on Kitchen, the waiter and the bar on Tables, the Inventory Manager on the Store.

The Start here card

Reception, housekeeping, the waiter and the chef see a card called Start here on their first screen. It lists the jobs they will do most. Each line opens the screen where the job is done and says which button finishes it. (Reception's card is in A1.9.)

  1. Click a line to go to that screen. A job done on the screen you are already on says “On this screen” instead.
  2. When you know your way, click Got it. The card goes away: “Put away. Bring it back any time from your account menu.”
  3. To see it again: account menu, Show Start here again.

Why it works this way

The first screen per person, set by the GM and changeable by the person: the owner, 14 September. No Dashboard for housekeeping, the kitchen, the restaurant floor and the store, because it carries revenue: the owner, 15 September, made firm in D27-141. A Start here card for reception, housekeeping, the waiter and the chef, none for the GM or the owner, gone when they tap Got it: the owner, 15 September.

  1. Find the switcher: at a desk or tablet it is in the top bar; on a phone it is at the top of the menu drawer (A0.7).
  2. Click the space's name. The highlighted name is the space you are in.
  3. In Settings, Back to front office at the top of the menu also takes you back.

Watch out

  • No switcher at all? You have one space only. Housekeeping, the Inventory Manager and the Revenue Manager work in the Front office alone; the waiter, the chef and the kitchen manager in the Restaurant alone.

  • The spaces change only how the menu is grouped. They do not change who may open a screen.

Why it works this way

Three spaces, each with its own menu, and who switches: D27-249. The details (↗ links in Settings, the Accountant and the Viewer getting Front office and Restaurant, the restaurant staff keeping one space with no switcher, the switcher at the top of the phone's menu drawer): D27-256.

A0.5Choosing the hotel

Every screen works on one property at a time. The property's short code is in the address, for example /h/5EL/arrivals for 5 Elements Akkalkot. Its name is under the heading of the Dashboard, Rooms and Calendar.

Most staff work at one property. The system opens on it, and the account menu shows it under Property (its name, then its code and city).

If you work at more than one property

  • Account menu. Under Property, each property you may work in is listed; a tick marks the chosen one. Click another and the same screen opens for that hotel: the address, the lists and the business date change together. On one record (a guest's bill, one equipment item) you land on that hotel's matching list instead (In house, Equipment). If you may not open that screen at the other hotel, its first screen opens. On the Dashboard and in Settings, the screen stays and the property changes.
  • Dashboard. A Property box at the top right picks which hotel the Dashboard's figures are for. It changes the Dashboard only; the rest of the system stays on the property it was on. The four tiles at the top then open that hotel's lists (A1.2).
  • The owner and Admin. All properties, in the Across properties section of the menu, shows every property on one page. Click a property's name to open its Rooms.

A test hotel

A test hotel is a practice copy of a real hotel, for learning the system without touching the real one, for example “Hotel Amrapali · TEST”. It starts with the real hotel's whole setup (rooms, rates, the menu, settings) and no guests, bookings or money.

  • The orange stripe. On every screen of a test hotel, an orange stripe across the top says “TEST HOTEL · practice only · nothing here is real”. It cannot be closed. While you see it, nothing you do counts.
  • TEST beside its name. In the account menu under Property, in the Dashboard's Property box and on People, a test hotel has TEST beside its name. It is never in All properties.
  • Its paper. Every bill, KOT, invoice, credit note, report and Form III printed there says “TEST · NOT VALID” in large letters and carries no GSTIN.
  • Nothing leaves it. Send voucher, Send check-in link, Send invoice and the review requests are off, with the line “This is a test hotel: nothing is sent from it.”
  • Who works in it. Only the people the owner or a group administrator puts in it. Somebody added on People with Test on works only in test hotels, never the real one. A member of staff may also be given the test hotel as a second place.

The owner and group administrators make a test hotel on Properties, under the real hotel: Make a test hotel. Once it is made, the same place has Open (go to it), Reset (deletes everything done in it, keeping its setup and its people; type RESET to go on) and Re-copy setup (brings the real hotel's rooms, rates, menu and settings again, deleting the test activity first; type RE-COPY).

Watch out

  • Each property has its own business date. Two hotels can be on different days (A1.1).
  • Your role can be different at each property, for example GM at one and Reception at another. The screens use your role at the property in the address, the same rule the server uses.

Why it works this way

The property is in the address so a booking can be linked to and a task survives switching. The role read for the property in the address: D26-13. All properties on one page for the owner, in place of the table that was at the Dashboard's foot: D27-243. Picking a property opens the same screen for it, address, lists and date together: D27-271; from a record, that hotel's matching list: D27-273. Test hotels inside the live system, one per real hotel, kept apart from everything real: D27-296.

A0.6Find a screen (Ctrl K)

The quickest way to anywhere. It finds every screen you may open, whether it is in your menu or not, and, if you work with bookings, guests and bookings too.

  1. Press Ctrl and K together (on a Mac, ⌘ K). Or click Find a screen… at the top. On a phone, tap the magnifying glass.
  2. Type a word: the screen's name, or the word you would say. Old and trade words work too: folio finds Folios, day end finds Close restaurant day, floor finds Tables.
  3. Move with the arrow keys ↑ ↓ and press Enter, or click the line.

Each line shows the screen's section on the right (not on a phone). A screen that is yours to open but not in your menu says “Not in your menu”. A service not built yet says “Not built yet”.

To find a guest or a booking

  1. Open Find a screen. If you work with bookings, the box says “Find a screen, a guest or a booking”.
  2. Type at least three letters of the guest's name or of the booking reference, or at least four digits of their phone number.
  3. Up to eight bookings appear under Bookings, below the screens: guests in the house first, then those coming, then those gone. Each line shows the guest, the reference, the dates and the room, and its status on the right (Due in, In house …).
  4. Click one. It opens in All bookings with its panel open.

If nothing matches: “Nothing called that. Try a shorter word, or what a guest would call it.” (For somebody who does not work with bookings it says “No screen called that”.)

Watch out

  • It lists only what your role may open. A screen you may not open is not listed at all.
  • Bookings are found only by people who work with bookings. A phone number finds a guest only for people who may see guests' details; for others the phone is blank.

Why it works this way

“Off the menu, still findable by search” was the owner's condition for shorter menus (15 September). Finding guests and bookings from the same box: D27-239. Guests' phone and email kept from roles that read bookings but not guests: D27-143.

A0.8When the connection drops: the offline bar

Hotels lose the internet. The system keeps working wherever that is safe, and a bar at the top of the page says what is happening. It decides “no connection” from whether its last request reached the server, not from the Wi-Fi sign: the Wi-Fi can be up while the line behind it is down.

The bar saysWhat it means, and what to do
“No connection. Working offline. Anything you can still do will be held.”The line is down. Nothing is waiting yet. Carry on.
“No connection. 1 change held: Housekeeping. They go when the line is back.”Your work is safe on this device and is sent by itself when the line returns.
“2 changes held.” with Send nowThe line is back and the changes are going. Send now sends them at once.
“One change was refused by the server.” with the reason and DiscardSomebody must look. Example: the room was given to another guest by another desk while you were offline. Read the reason, put it right, then Discard the line.
“1 change was made by someone else signed in on this device. They will send when that person signs in again.”Not your work, and not an error. It waits for that person.
“This browser will not keep a queue - a private window, or site data turned off…”Nothing can be held here. Work in a normal browser window.

What can be done offline, and what cannot

 Examples
Held, sent laterMarking rooms clean or inspected, housekeeping assignments, work orders, marking a guest VIP, restaurant orders and the kitchen, a dish on or off the menu, a function enquiry.
Held, with a warningA check-in or a room given to a booking. You are told: “Check-in is held and will be sent when the connection is back. It is not confirmed until then - the room could be taken by another desk in the meantime.” A restaurant bill charged to a room is held the same way.
Refused, with a reasonAn invoice, a payment, the cash drawer, settling a restaurant bill in cash, card or UPI, printing a restaurant bill, adding or changing add-ons on a dish (Customise), closing the restaurant day, the night audit, a charge or a correction on a bill, check-out, moving a guest, Form III, confirming a banquet function. Anything not named above is refused too. Each refusal says why and what to do instead, for example: “Take it on paper and enter it when the connection is back.”

What you can still see

The screens below keep a copy on the device for a week, so they open with no connection: the properties and rooms, Arrivals, In house, Due out, Rooms and Calendar, Housekeeping, Work orders, the banquet diary, the restaurant's Tables, its menu and numbered add-ons, and the rooms a bill can be charged to. Guests' documents, bills and reports are not kept: a shared desk computer is no place for a passport scan. A reload with no connection keeps you signed in, for up to a week.

Rooms says how old its copy is, in a yellow line above the rooms: “No connection - these rooms are as they were at 6:38 PM. They refresh by themselves when the connection is back.”

The screen you were on says it too: Housekeeping shows “1 change waiting to sync” under its heading and Offline at the top right, and the room's card says “Queued: mark inspected”.

Watch out

  • A room that reads Free on a copy from an hour ago may have been sold since. Check it again when the line is back, before you promise it to a guest.
  • Bills are not kept on the device. The In house and Due out lists do keep their last copy, balances included: with no connection, treat a balance there as possibly old. Online, every money figure is read again each time a screen opens.
  • A charge to a room held offline can reach the server after the guest has checked out. The server then refuses it, and the bar keeps the refusal on screen so somebody follows it up.

Why it works this way

A tax invoice number comes from a legal series; two desks offline could give two guests the same number, and it cannot be undone, so invoices are refused offline. Cash is counted against what the server knows, so payments are refused too. A waiter must be able to charge a meal to a room on a bad line: the owner, 8 September. Rooms kept on the desk computer for a reload with the server down: D22-23. Money figures re-checked every time a screen opens: D17-58.
