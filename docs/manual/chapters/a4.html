Chapter A4

The guest's bill

For reception, the general manager and the accountant. How to read a guest's bill, post a charge, take a wrong one off, correct a charge or the rate, take a payment, split the bill with a company, print the interim bill, run your cash drawer, and what happens after check-out: the tax invoice and the credit note.

A4.1Where the money is: the Finance screens

At the desk, a guest's money is the bill: the Bill tab of the guest's panel (A4.2). In Finance the same thing is called a folio. The Finance part of the menu holds these screens. You see the ones your role uses; reception's menu shows Cashiering, and reaches the others with Find a screen (Ctrl K, A0).

Why it works this way

“Bill” at the desk and “folio” in Finance are the owner's words (15 September). A balance in either direction is real: a guest overpaid is money the hotel owes, and it is shown, never as zero.

A4.2Reading the guest's bill

To open a guest's bill

  1. Open In house and click the guest. Their panel opens on the right.
  2. Click the Bill tab. (The orange Settle and check out at the top of the panel opens the same tab.) From Folios, a guest opens straight on the bill.
  • The first night is posted at check-in, each later night by the night audit. Until then, the nights to come are shown in the dashed box, at the agreed rate with their GST, and counted in Due. Any advance and anything paid are counted too.
  • If no GST rule covers the room's rate, the box shows a dash, never a figure the system guessed.
  • When no nights are to come, the top shows Balance, marked Outstanding, Overpaid or Settled.
  • The bill is in sections: Room, Restaurant, Bar (alcohol, under VAT), then Other charges. Each line shows its amount before tax; each section has its subtotal and its tax. The early check-in and late check-out fees charged when the guest checks in early or leaves late sit under Room.
  • Under the sections: Total charges, then Payments received, then the last row: what is due.

The bill's one orange button is the next step: Settle while money is owed, then Check out once nothing is (chapter A5). A button you may not use is not offered; one that cannot be used yet is greyed, and says why when you point at it.

Why it works this way

The headline counts the nights still to come, so the desk sees what is owed from check-in: D27-187, D27-199. The bill in sections, the same on the screen and on paper: D27-200; the early and late fees under Room: D27-223. Roles that do not handle money do not see the Bill tab: D27-143.

A4.3Posting a charge

To post a charge

  1. On the Bill tab, click Add charge. The window Post a charge names the room and the guest.
  2. If the hotel keeps a priced list of extra charges, From the hotel's list comes first: choosing one fills the words, the price and the category. Each can still be changed.
  3. Choose the Category: Room posting, Food, Beverages, Late check-out fee, Early check-in fee, Extra person fee, Laundry or Miscellaneous.
  4. Type the Description: the words the guest reads on the bill (“Laundry, 3 shirts”).
  5. Type the Amount, before tax.
  6. Leave Taxable ticked. It shows the GST rate this amount carries, and the box under it the tax, split CGST and SGST, and the total. Untick it only for a charge that bears no GST.
  7. Click Post charge. The message “Charge posted” gives the amount with tax and the room.

Watch out

  • No GST rule, no charge. If the hotel has not set the GST rule, Taxable shows “GST -” and the charge is refused with the reason. A missing rate is not zero; somebody sets it first (Finance, Invoices, or Settings).
  • Post charge stays off until there is a description and an amount above zero. To take money off a bill, reverse the charge it corrects (A4.4); a charge is never a minus figure.
  • One press is one charge: the button waits while the charge is sent.
  • A company over its credit limit: the window says so in a sentence. The owner, an administrator or the GM can post it anyway with a reason (Allow, with reason); anybody else asks one of them.
  • The bill has no discount button. A lower room price is a manager's Change rate (A4.6).

Why it works this way

Everything posted at the desk carries the room's GST rule for that amount, and the window shows that rate before you post, so the bill never has a rate nobody set. Each line names the person signed in when it was posted. Over a company's credit limit only with a manager's reason: D27-275.

A4.4Taking a wrong charge off: Reverse

A charge is never edited or deleted at the desk. A wrong one is reversed: it stays on the bill, struck through, and the reversal is its own line.

To reverse a charge

  1. Click the curved arrow at the end of the line (Figure A4-06, arrow 4).
  2. The window Reverse this charge? names the line and its amount with tax.
  3. Type Why. It is required. If the hotel has set ready reasons, they show under the box: click one to use it.
  4. Click Reverse (Keep it leaves the line as it is). The message: “Posting reversed - The reversal is its own line - nothing was edited.”

Watch out

  • A line is reversed once, and a reversal cannot itself be reversed. Post the charge again if it was right after all.
  • A line from a day the night audit has closed: “This day is closed - correct it on Closed days.” (chapter A7).
  • Once a tax invoice stands on the bill: “This bill has tax invoice … Only a credit note corrects it now.” (A4.11).
  • A payment is reversed only by the owner, an administrator, the GM or the accountant. Its window, Reverse this payment?, says the receipt stays on the bill, struck through, and the money is owed again.
  • A greyed arrow cannot be used: point at it to read why.

Why it works this way

The bill only ever grows: what was posted stays, so anyone can see what happened and who did it. Reversing asks first, with a reason: D27-117. Reception reverses charges; a payment only the four above: D27-125.

A4.5A manager corrects a charge

For the owner, an administrator and the GM. Their bill has a pencil on each charge: change its price, its words, or both, with a reason, in one step.

To correct a charge

  1. Click the pencil on the line. The window Edit this charge names it and its amount with tax.
  2. Change the Words, the Price before tax, or both. The tax stays at the rate it was charged.
  3. Type Why. It is required, and kept on the reversed line; the guest's bill never shows it.
  4. Click Correct the charge. The message: “Charge corrected - The old line is reversed with the reason, and the corrected one posted.”

Watch out

  • No pencil on a payment, a reversal, or a line already reversed: there is nothing to edit.
  • The pencil is greyed on a day the night audit closed (“This day is closed - correct it on Closed days.”) and once a tax invoice stands on the bill (a credit note corrects it then).
  • A room night: only its price changes. “A room night's words count its nights, so only its price changes.” Its tax follows the room's slab on the new price.
  • Drinks that already have a VAT bill are refused until that VAT bill is cancelled.

Why it works this way

The owner, an administrator and the GM may correct a charge; the old line stays, reversed with the reason, and the corrected one is posted in the same step: D27-157, D27-162.

A4.6Changing the rate

Also for the owner, an administrator and the GM: Change rate, after the other buttons, gives the whole stay a new rate a night.

To change the rate

  1. Click Change rate. The window Change the rate names the guest and the rate now.
  2. Type the New rate a night, before tax. The room's GST slab follows it.
  3. If the figure you typed already includes GST, tick Includes GST. The window then shows how it splits, for example “₹… a night + ₹… GST (5%).”
  4. Type Why. It is kept with every night it corrects.
  5. Click Change the rate. Nights already charged are corrected to it, with this reason (a closed day's on Closed days); nights still to come are charged at it. The message “Rate changed” says how many charged lines were corrected.

Watch out

  • All or nothing. If one charged night cannot be corrected, nothing changes and the message names that night.
  • Only a booking still to come or a guest in the house changes rate. After check-out, a bill is corrected on Closed days or by a credit note.
  • Before check-in, the same window opens from the guest panel's More options, Change rate: it changes only the agreed rate, since nothing is charged yet.

Why it works this way

Change rate for the same three, nights already posted corrected through the Closed days correction where the day is closed: D27-157, D27-162. A rate that includes GST is split by the room's slab before it is kept: D27-199.

A4.7Taking a payment: Settle

To take a payment

  1. On the Bill tab, click Settle. The window Settle bill says what is outstanding, and whose bill it is.
  2. On a stay with two bills, choose Which bill (A4.8). One bill is settled at a time.
  3. Choose the Method. The list is the hotel's own ways of paying (Settings); the hotel's default is chosen first. Every hotel started with Cash, UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card and Bill to company.
  4. The Amount is filled in with what is owed. Change it for a part payment.
  5. Check Outstanding, Paying and Remaining, and click Take payment. The message names the amount received and the method.

The payment shows under Payments received: “Received - Cash”, or for Bill to company “Transferred to city ledger”. Cash goes into your drawer, which opens by itself with your first cash (A4.10). When nothing is owed, Check out opens (chapter A5).

Watch out

  • More than the bill: the window says “This is ₹… more than the bill; it will be held as the guest's credit.” Take payment stays off until you tick Hold it as the guest's credit.
  • Before arrival, Settle opens for an advance the hotel asks before check-in, filled in with what is still owed and held as the guest's credit without a tick (A3.6). With no advance asked and nothing posted, it opens for a deposit: “A deposit before arrival”, the Amount empty for you to type, held as the guest's credit the same way.
  • Cash above ₹1,00,000 at once asks for the Guest's PAN, or the tick No PAN: Form 97 taken. Cash of ₹2,00,000 or more from one guest is refused; take the rest another way.
  • A company's bill (A4.8) goes Bill to company only if the company is allowed credit. Otherwise the message says it is not: take the payment another way, or ask the owner, an administrator or the GM to allow credit on its record.
  • One press is one payment. A wrong payment is reversed only by the owner, an administrator, the GM or the accountant (A4.4).

Why it works this way

More than the bill is held as the guest's credit, once the desk ticks it: D27-4, D27-11. The PAN or Form 97 above ₹1,00,000 in cash (Income-tax Rule 159): D25-40. Cash opens your drawer by itself: D27-200.

A4.8Two bills: when a company pays

A company pays the room; the guest pays their own extras. Split the bill: the stay gets a second bill in the company's name, and each line goes on the bill that pays it.

To split the bill

  1. On the Bill tab, click Split the bill.
  2. Under Who is paying the second bill? choose the company, and click Open the second bill. The message: “Second bill opened - Move charges onto it from the ledger above.”
  3. Above the lines, the bills now show side by side: Everything, Guest, and the company, each with what it owes. Click one to see only its lines.
  4. To move a line, click the two-arrow button at its end, then under Move to the bill it belongs on. The message: “Moved - Reversed on one bill and posted on the other - nothing was edited.”
  5. Settle each bill on its own (A4.7). For the company's, choose it under Which bill and the Method Bill to company: “Transfers to the city ledger”. The amount goes to the company's account.

When the company pays, record it on Company accounts: Record payment, then Who paid, Amount received, Received on, How (bank transfer, cheque, UPI or card, never cash), Reference (the bank's UTR or the cheque's number), and an optional Note.

Watch out

  • Only a company on file can be chosen. With none, the window says to add one under Companies first: a company's bill carries its GSTIN and address.
  • A payment stays on the bill it was taken on, and a reversed line cannot be moved: neither has the move button.
  • Nights a group's company pays for are on the group's own bill, not on the guest's.

Why it works this way

A move is a reversal on one bill and a posting on the other, so nothing is edited. A guest's figures count only the guest's own bill: D27-226. A company's payment is never cash: D27-242.

A4.9The interim bill

The guest asks to see the bill before paying, or a company wants a copy.

  1. On the Bill tab, click Interim bill. The window Interim bill is the print preview.
  2. Click Print. The bill opens on a page of its own; print it from there.

It shows the guest, the room and the stay, the same sections as the Bill tab, and at the foot what was paid and what is outstanding. A reversed line and its reversal are left off it. It is not a tax invoice and carries no invoice number: the numbered tax invoice is issued after check-out (A4.11).

Why it works this way

The desk's print says “Interim bill”, before check-out and after, so nobody takes it for the numbered tax invoice: D22-7; the button says the same: D24-25.

A4.10Your cash drawer: Cashiering

Every rupee of cash taken at the desk belongs to somebody's drawer. Open Cashiering (Finance, in reception's menu) to see yours.

Opening

  • There is no step to open. Your drawer opens by itself the first time you take cash.
  • If the last person handed you a float by name, the line says so (“₹… was handed to you: your drawer opens on it.”) and your drawer opens on it. Otherwise it opens at ₹0.
  • Only when a float is given to you by hand, count it here: type how many of each note. The button becomes Open the drawer - ₹…; click it.

During the shift

  • Taken this shift lists what you took, by method. Only cash reaches the drawer: a card payment, or a bill sent to a company, is not in the tin.
  • Money that was not a payment: under In and out of the drawer, click Record. Choose What happened (Paid out, Cash in, To the safe), type How much and What was it for, and click Record.
  • A Paid out waits for the GM's or the owner's yes. Until then it reads “Waiting for a yes - not taken off the drawer yet”.
  • The drawer, on the right, adds it up: Opening float, Cash taken, money in and out, Expected, and Counted.

At the end of your shift

  1. Under Count the drawer, type how many of each note and coin, and click Save the count.
  2. The drawer says “The drawer balances”, or “Over by …” / “Short by …”. A difference does not stop the hand-over, but it has to be written down.
  3. Click Close and hand over. If the count differs, type Why.
  4. Hand the drawer to: the next person, or Nobody.
  5. Float kept for the next shift: always typed, from ₹0 up to what you counted. Above ₹0 it needs the next person's name. The rest is to deposit.
  6. Click Close the shift.

The cash to deposit is banked from Cash deposits: the GM records each day's deposit (amount, date, bank, slip number and the slip's photo), and the owner or the accountant verifies it.

Why it works this way

Each cash point counts in and counts out, and the deposits are logged and verified: D27-190. The drawer opens by itself with the first cash: D27-200. A float kept waits for the person named: D27-201; it is always typed, and the rest goes to deposit: D27-202. A paid-out counts only once the GM or the owner says yes: D27-242.

A4.11After check-out: the tax invoice and the credit note

The interim bill is not a tax invoice. The numbered GST tax invoice is issued on Invoices, after the guest has checked out and the bill is settled.

To issue a tax invoice

  1. Open Invoices. Its list is the departed stays ready to bill.
  2. Click Issue on the stay. It is greyed while the bill still owes or is overpaid; point at it to read why.
  3. The window Issue this tax invoice? says who it is billed to and the next number in the GST series. Click Issue the invoice.
  • A stay with two bills gets an invoice for each bill.
  • Alcohol is under VAT: its own VAT bill is issued in the same step, from its own series.
  • The stay's row then shows the invoice's number. Click it to open the invoice on its own page, with Print and Send invoice (by email, with the PDF; the test hotel sends nothing).
  • The hotel's Tax invoice switch (A10.7, A17.12) sends an invoice by itself only when it is made out to the guest. One made out to a company, an agent or a booking site, for example Expedia, is not sent by itself: click Send invoice on its page.
  • It can also be issued on the guest's bill right after check-out (A5.9).

What the printed invoice shows

Print prints it on the desk's own printer, on one A4 page for an ordinary stay, without the menu. The invoice e-mailed to the guest is the same document, as a PDF. It carries everything the hotel's old folio invoice did, in Fidato's document style:

  • At the top: the hotel's own logo and the Fidato Hotels logo side by side (each when uploaded: A17.3, A17.4), the hotel's name and "by" the group, and on the right TAX INVOICE with its number and time, over the orange rule. Under it the hotel's address, phone, e-mail and website, and its GSTIN and PAN.
  • Two columns: the booking reference, invoice number, guest, who it is billed to (with their GSTIN or "not registered"), the source, the place of supply; the date of the invoice, the business date, the room type and number, the persons (adults and children), the rate type, the nights, arrival and departure.
  • The lines: each charge with its SAC, quantity, rate, total, discount, taxable value, SGST, CGST, IGST and CESS (IGST and CESS are always 0.00: a stay is supplied in the hotel's own state), and the totals under them.
  • Below: the amount in words, the payments received, the tax by rate; on the right the totals, what was paid and the balance.
  • At the foot: who checked the guest in (Reception (C/I)) and out (Cashier (C/O)) and who issued it, in a grey strip; the guest's signature and the hotel's authorised signatory; then the group's name and office (address, phone, e-mail, website, Instagram) from Settings, Group (A17.4), or the hotel's own address until those are typed.
  • A long bill runs onto a second page, with the columns' names again at its top.
  • The e-mailed PDF can show a logo only if it was uploaded as a PNG picture. An SVG logo shows on the printed invoice only.

To correct an issued invoice: the credit note

  1. Open the invoice from the list under Issued, and click Cancel this invoice.
  2. Type Why the invoice was wrong.
  3. Click Issue credit note. The invoice is not edited and not deleted: a credit note with its own number is issued against it, and both stay on the record.

Watch out

  • A credit note is for the owner, an administrator, the GM or the accountant. For anyone else the button is greyed, with that sentence.
  • Once a tax invoice stands on a bill, its charges cannot be reversed or edited at the desk: the credit note is the correction.
  • E-invoicing (an IRN and a signed QR) is not required: the group is under ₹5 crore of turnover. The Invoices screen says so.

Why it works this way

An issued GST document never changes, and its number is never lost from the series; a mistake is corrected by a document of its own. Who issues a credit note: D22-33. Alcohol's own VAT bill: D22-44. The printed invoice laid out as the hotel's old one, keeping "Tax Invoice" and both signatures, with no registration card number: D27-342. Fidato's document style, both logos, the group's office at the foot, and the e-mailed PDF the same document on A4: D27-343.
