Chapter A14

Sales, marketing and loyalty

For the owner, managers and reception. The leads app's enquiries inside the PMS and how a lead becomes a booking, the month's marketing figures, and the loyalty programme: switching it on, its rules, joining a guest, points earned at check-out and taken off the room charges.

A14.1The Sales and marketing screens

In the Front office menu, the part called Sales and marketing sits under Guests & CRM: both are about people. It holds three screens.

ScreenWho sees itWhat it is for
Leads and WhatsAppEveryone who sees guests: Owner, Admin, General Manager, Hotel Manager, Reception, Accountant, ViewerThe leads app's enquiries for this hotel, read as you open the page; Book this lead (A14.2).
MarketingOwner, Admin, General ManagerOne month: each campaign's spend beside the bookings and revenue it brought, and every booking source's (A14.3).
LoyaltyEveryone who sees guests (Points by hotel and The rules: owner, Admin, GM)Who is in house and their points: join, use points, leave; the programme's rules; points by hotel (A14.4).

Where each one works

None of the three is hidden by a hotel's module switch: the menu shows them wherever your role allows. What each needs before it does its work:

  • Leads and WhatsApp and Marketing's spend: the leads app connected on the server (the owner's read-only login), and this hotel matched to one of the leads app's hotels. Without them the screen says so; Marketing still shows its bookings and revenue.
  • Loyalty: the hotel's own Loyalty switch (A14.4), plus a programme name and an earn rate for the group. Without the switch the screen says “Loyalty is off at this hotel: nobody joins, earns or uses points here. The owner or an administrator turns it on in Settings, under Modules.”

Reception's own menu has none of the three. Reception opens Leads and WhatsApp and Loyalty with Find a screen (Ctrl K): type “loyalty” or “leads”, and tap the screen, marked “Not in your menu”.

Why it works this way

A Sales and marketing part of the menu, for Leads and WhatsApp, Marketing and Loyalty: D27-87. Everyone who sees guests sees leads; marketing's numbers only the owner, an Admin and the GM: D27-170. A shorter menu for each role, with every other screen they may open one search away: the owner, 15 September.

A14.2Leads and WhatsApp

The hotel's enquiries - from WhatsApp, the website and ads - live in the leads app, where staff reply to them. Leads and WhatsApp shows them inside the PMS: “Enquiries from the leads app, read as you open this page. Staff reply in the leads app.” The PMS sends nothing to a guest from here and writes nothing into the leads app. The one thing done here is Book this lead.

What the screen can say

It saysMeans
“Not connected yet” (Figure A14-01)“The leads app is read with a read-only login the owner sets up on the server. Until then nothing is read and nothing is shown here.”
“This property is not in the leads app”“The leads app has no hotel matched to this property, so it has no enquiries to show here.”
“The leads app was not read”With the reason: “Today's reads of the leads app are used up. They come back after midnight Pacific time.”, or “The leads app could not be reached. Nothing was changed there. Try again in a few minutes.”
“No enquiries yet”“Enquiries arrive in the leads app from WhatsApp, the website and ads, and show here.”

When it is connected

  • Along the top, one button per status with its count: All, Open (New, Assigned, Claimed, Overdue, Contacted, Follow-up and Quoted together), then each status there is - New, Assigned, Claimed, Contacted, Follow-up, Quoted, Booked, Lost, Long term, Dead, Overdue, Closed. These are the leads app's own words.
  • Enquiries, “Newest message first”: the name (or the phone), the status, the last message, the channel - “Meta Ads”, “Google Ads”, “Website organic”, “Direct WhatsApp”, “Referral”, “Walk-in or phone”, “Manual entry”, “Other” - and when. Fifty at a time; Show older enquiries for more.
  • Tap an enquiry to read it beside the list: the name, phone, channel and status; Came in; for a booked one, Booking in the leads app and Value typed there; then the conversation, the guest's messages on the left and the hotel's on the right. On a phone the conversation takes the screen; Back to the enquiries returns.
  • Reply in the leads app opens the enquiry in the leads app, where you answer.

To book a lead

  1. Open the enquiry and tap Book this lead.
  2. New booking opens with the guest's name and phone filled in, the booking source by the lead's channel - Meta Ads, Google Ads, WhatsApp or Website, when this hotel has a booking source by that name; any other channel, you choose - and the lead's campaign kept on the booking.
  3. Finish the booking as usual (chapter A2). Its campaign is what Marketing counts (A14.3).

An enquiry opened too long ago, or erased in the leads app: “This enquiry is no longer in the leads app, or its link has expired. Open it again from Leads and WhatsApp.”

Watch out

  • Every look costs. The leads app is read live and its free allowance is shared with the app itself, so the PMS reads at most 5,000 times a day. The list comes 50 at a time; a conversation is read only when you open it; a screen opened twice within a minute is read once.
  • Nothing is copied into the PMS. A guest erased in the leads app is gone from here the next time the list loads. Their stays and bills stay.
  • Staff's internal notes in the leads app are not shown here.
  • “The guest asked not to be messaged. The leads app refuses replies to them.” on an enquiry means exactly that.

Why it works this way

The leads app keeps running; the PMS shows its leads, conversations and spend read-only, with a link to reply there: D27-100; its conversations are linked, never copied: D21-5. Read live every time, no copy kept, a cap of 5,000 reads a day, staff notes not shown, a lead's booking taking its source by channel and keeping its campaign: D27-170. A guest erased there is erased from this view too, and their stays and bills stay as tax law requires: D27-108. The app is kept separate, and the PMS only shows its leads: D27-186.

A14.3Marketing

For the owner, an Admin and the GM. Marketing shows one month, by the stays' arrival date: what each campaign cost, beside the bookings and revenue it brought here, and the same for every booking source.

To read a month

  1. Open Marketing. It opens on this month.
  2. Choose another in Month.
  3. Read Campaigns: Campaign, Where (“Meta Ads”, “Google Ads”, “Other ads”), Spend, Bookings, Revenue.
  4. Read By booking source: Booking source, Bookings, Revenue.
  • Spend comes from the leads app; bookings and revenue from this system's own stays. A booking carries its campaign only when it was made with Book this lead (“A booking carries its campaign when it is made from a lead.”).
  • Revenue is the stays' room charges before tax: the nights already charged, plus the nights still to come at the booking's rate. Cancelled and no-show stays are left out.
  • A dash under Spend means nobody has the figure - never a zero. The line under the campaigns says why: “Spend is read from the leads app, which is not connected yet.”, “This property is not one of the leads app's hotels, so it has no spend.”, or the leads app's own reason.
  • Nothing that month: “No campaigns this month”, “No spend was recorded in the leads app, and no stay arriving this month came from a campaign.”; “No stays arrive this month”.

Watch out

  • The month is by arrival: a stay booked in September for October counts in October.
  • Opening Marketing reads the leads app twice; it counts against the same daily allowance as Leads and WhatsApp.
  • The guest lists for sending offers (stayed twice, not back in a year, came from which site) have no screen yet. Offers will go only to guests who said yes (A10.5).

Why it works this way

Spend beside the bookings and revenue of this system's own stays, never a figure typed in the leads app; marketing numbers for the owner, an Admin and the GM only: D27-170. Revenue is booked value - nights charged plus nights to come at the booked rate, before tax, cancelled and no-shows out, by arrival date: D27-173. The first version of marketing asked for guest lists that can be acted on: D18-17, for the owner, an Admin and the GM: D22-17; offers only to a guest who said yes: D25-39.

A14.4Loyalty

A member earns points on their stays at every hotel of the group, and uses them on their room charges: 1 point is ₹1 off. The heading line says the programme's name and its earn rate, for example “… · 5 points per ₹100 before GST; 1 point is ₹1 off room charges” - or, until they are set, “No programme name yet · no earn rate set: stays earn nothing”.

Once: switch it on and set the rules

  1. The owner or an Admin opens Settings, Modules and connections, Modules, and switches on Loyalty for the hotel: “Members earn points on their stays here and use them on their room charges. Off means nobody joins, earns or uses points at this property.”
  2. The owner, an Admin or the GM opens Loyalty. Under The rules (“For every hotel of the group.”) they type the Programme name - “The words a guest agrees to use it.” - and Points per ₹100 - “Before GST. Empty: stays earn nothing.”
  3. Tap Save the rules. The message says “Rules saved”.

What stops the rules: “The name is 40 letters at most.”; “Points per ₹100 is a number above 0 and at most 100, or empty for none.”

To join a guest

In house lists every guest checked in: Room, Guest, Points (or “Not a member”).

  1. Ask the guest. Tap Join on their line.
  2. The window says “Read this to the guest. Joining is not a yes to offers.” Read them the words shown: “Join programme name: we keep your stays and points to give you rewards. I am 18 or over.”
  3. If they agree, tick The guest agrees.
  4. Tap Join. The message says “… joined”.

What stops a join: “Loyalty is off at this hotel. The owner or an administrator turns it on in Settings.”; “The programme has no name yet. Set it on the Loyalty screen first.”; “A member needs a mobile number. Add it to the guest first.” (Edit guest, A10.3); “This guest is already a member.”; “Tick that the guest agrees, in their own words, before they join.”

Earning: by itself, at check-out

When a member checks out, the stay earns points by itself, usable at once, at the earn rate: everything on the stay's bills before GST and after discounts - room, restaurant charged to the room, and the rest - except GST, alcohol, refunds and payments. On a booking from a booking site or an agent, the room earns nothing; the extras do. Whole points, rounded down, once per stay.

To use points

  1. On the member's line, tap Use points (shown on direct bookings only).
  2. The window, Use points for …, says their balance: “They come off the room charges as a discount line, from 500 at a time.”
  3. Type the Points - “1 point is ₹1 off. The guest signs the bill.” The button reads Use ₹500.
  4. Tap it. The message says “₹500 off the room charges”. The bill shows a discount line under the nights; each night keeps its price.

What stops it: “Points are used while the guest is checked in.”; “Points are used on direct bookings only, not on a booking from a booking site or an agent.”; “Points are used from 500 at a time.”; “The guest has 320 points.”; “Points come off room charges only, and this bill has … of them left.”; “This stay has a tax invoice. Points cannot be taken off it now.”

To leave

  1. On the member's line, tap Leave.
  2. The window asks … leaves loyalty?: “Their 320 points lapse. Their history stays with the bills.” and “They can join again later, with no points.”
  3. Tap Leave. The message says “… left”.

The member card on Guest 360 (owner, Admin, GM)

Guest 360 (A10.5) shows a Loyalty card: “Member since …, joined at …”, the points, and every line of the statement at every hotel - “Earned”, “Used”, “Adjusted”, “Lapsed” - with the booking, the hotel and any reason. A guest who is not a member reads “Not a member. The desk joins a guest on the Loyalty screen, with their yes.” (Figure A10-08).

  1. To add or take points by hand: type Points (“Minus to take”) and the Reason (“Stays on the statement”); tap Adjust. The message says “Points adjusted”.
  2. To take a member out: Leave, then Leave loyalty: “Left the programme”.

What stops an adjustment: “Say why. The reason stays on the statement.”; “The guest has 320 points; a balance cannot go below 0.”

Points by hotel

For the owner, an Admin and the GM (Figure A14-05): for the month chosen, each hotel's Points earned and Points used, in rupees, so the hotels can settle between themselves. Points earned at one hotel may be used at another; the hotel that gives the discount carries it on its own invoice.

Watch out

  • Joining is not a yes to offers. Offers are a separate question (A10.5).
  • A member is one person across the group. Two guest records joined as one (A10.6) share one balance; an undo splits it back.
  • A discount line of points taken off the bill again (reversed) gives the points back by themselves.
  • No tiers. Points do not lapse yet: the 24-month expiry is not built, and no point could lapse before October 2028.
  • Reception, the Hotel Manager, the GM, an Admin and the owner join, use points and take guests out. The Accountant may use points, but not join a guest or take one out; a Viewer only looks.

Why it works this way

Build loyalty now: D27-178. The earn rate is a setting with no default - unset, nothing earns; no tiers at the start; a per-hotel switch; 18 or over with a mobile; its own consent tick; one membership per person; booking-site and agent rooms earn on extras only; points used from 500 on the member's own room charges as a discount line; the giving hotel carries the discount: D27-179, with the rules L1 to L18 in the loyalty service's notes.
