Chapter A7

Night audit

For the manager or the desk who closes the hotel's day. What the night audit does, the checks it shows first, what stops it and how to clear it, running it, the closed days and how one is corrected, and the reports it can send by email.

A7.1What the night audit does

The night audit closes the hotel's day. Until it runs, the business date stays where it is, whatever the clock says (A1.1). A person runs it, late at night, from the Night audit screen. It is never run by itself: a hotel that forgets simply stays on yesterday, and every screen says so.

When it runs, in one go, it:

  • charges one night's room, with its tax, to every guest in house (not a complimentary room, a day-use stay, or a night already charged at check-in);
  • marks every arrival who never came as a no-show, and gives the room back. A no-show is charged only the retention its booking's terms set; a booking with no terms is charged nothing;
  • moves the business date on to the next day.

It never checks anybody out, and it never decides for a person. If something only a person can answer is still open, it refuses, and says what (A7.3).

Watch out

  • Yellow is not the same as stopped. Of the six checks, only Departures not yet checked out stops the audit. The other things that stop it are not checks at all: they are written on the Run card (A7.3).
  • An arrival is counted here only on the day it was due. The audit marks as no-shows every booking still waiting whose arrival day is today or earlier.

A7.3What stops the audit, and how to clear it

Below the checks, the Run card: Run the audit and roll to the next day. While anything stops the audit, the reason is written on the card and Run night audit stays grey. The Revenue Manager and the Viewer see the card and its reasons and no button: they may not run the audit (A7.1).

Four things stop it. Each has its own sentence on the card.

The card saysWhat to do
“The night audit for 16 Oct opens at 16:00, by the hotel's clock.”Wait until that time. Before it, nobody can close today's day. A day already behind (the business date is earlier than today) is never locked. The time is set in Settings → Times and rules, Night audit opens at, by the owner or an administrator (A17).
“4 rooms are still showing due out. Check them out, or extend the stay - the audit will not decide which.”Open the check Departures not yet checked out. For each guest: if they left, settle the bill and check them out (A1.6, A5); if they are staying on, open the guest and use Change dates or guests.
“A front-desk drawer is still open. Count the cash and close the shift …”On Cashiering, count the desk's cash and tap Close the shift.
“The till has not been closed for today. Run the day end on it first …”Close the restaurant's day: Close restaurant day (A8.9). Only a restaurant with orders today needs it.
“This property has no GST rule for rooms …”The card goes on: “Set it in the GST card, under Settings → Taxes and billing or Finance → Invoices. A missing rate is not zero - the audit will not invent one.” Ask whoever sets the hotel's taxes.

To clear the guests still due out

  1. On Night audit, tap Open on Departures not yet checked out. Due out opens (Figure A7-03).
  2. The red box says how many still owe, and how much in all. Tap a guest: the panel opens on the Bill tab.
  3. If the guest has left: take the payment, then check them out. Check-out unlocks when the bill reaches zero.
  4. If the guest is staying another night: Change dates or guests, and move the departure.
  5. Go back to Night audit. When nobody is left due out, the sentence goes from the card.

Why it works this way

Today's day closes only from the hotel's opening time, 16:00 to begin, so nobody closes it in the morning by mistake; catching up a day behind is never held back: D27-338. A guest still due out at night either left and nobody checked them out, or is staying another night. The two lead to opposite charges, and only a person at the desk knows which, so the audit refuses and names the rooms. The two drawers are counted first because the audit states the day's takings, and money not yet counted is not final. The button is shown grey, with its reason, to everybody who may run the audit, and not shown at all to the two roles who may never run it. The screen and the server ask the same question, so they cannot disagree: D27-305.

A7.4Running the audit

  1. Read the checks (A7.2) and clear what stops it (A7.3).
  2. When the card shows no reason, tap Run night audit.
  3. It asks first: “Close Friday, 9 October 2026?” with “The property moves to Saturday, 10 October 2026. Tonight's room charges are posted to every guest in house and arrivals who did not come become no-shows. A closed day cannot be edited, only corrected afterwards.”
  4. Tap Close 9 Oct 2026 (the day being closed), or Cancel. While it works, the button reads Closing the day….
  5. A green card says “Friday, 9 October 2026 is closed. The property is now operating in Saturday, 10 October 2026.”, with what it did: rooms posted and their revenue, rooms given free (“complimentary, not charged”), and no-shows.

When the restaurant sold something that day, the card also shows its day: orders, covers, the total with its tax, and each way it was paid. Under it, the two drawers side by side, never added together: Front desk (its shifts) and Till (its outlets), each with what it opened on, what was counted, and how many differences were explained. These are read from what the till and the desk already wrote; the audit adds none of it to a bill.

If the hotel has switched it on (Settings, Front desk rules, The night audit sends every room with a guest staying on for its daily clean), the audit also marks those rooms dirty for the new day. It starts off.

Watch out

  • A closed day cannot be opened again. It can only be corrected, line by line (A7.5).
  • Pressed twice, on two desks, or on a screen left open after the day was closed elsewhere? The day closes once. The second press changes nothing and says, for example, “9 Oct was already closed. Reload to see today.” and “Nothing was run again.”, with the record that was made: who ran it, when, rooms posted, room revenue, no-shows.
  • No connection, no audit. It closes the day for the whole property and needs the server. Wait for the line.
  • After the audit, other desks see the new business date the next time they open a screen (A1.1).

Why it works this way

The audit asks first, because a closed day cannot be edited: D27-117. A second press changes nothing and shows the record that was made: D17-12. The first night is charged at check-in, so the audit skips a night already charged: D27-204. The daily clean at the audit, off unless the hotel says so: D27-260. The audit is run by a person and never scheduled: the owner's rule.

A7.5Closed days, and correcting one

Under the Run card, Closed days lists every night audit run at this property, newest first: Day, Ran by, Closed, Rooms posted, Room revenue (the room charges the audit posted, before tax) and No-shows. A figure the record does not hold is a dash. Until the first audit, it says “No day closed yet” (Figure A7-02, arrow 4).

To correct a closed day (owner, Admin, GM)

  1. Under Closed days, tap Correct on the day. Days from the 1st of last month on can be corrected; older ones stay as they are, and their Correct is grey.
  2. Under Charge to correct, choose the charge that was wrong: a room charge, a no-show charge, a restaurant bill charged to a room, or another charge of that day.
  3. Type The right figure, before tax, or tick Take the charge off entirely.
  4. Type Why it was wrong. It is kept with your name and the time.
  5. Tap Save correction. “Correction saved”.
  • Nothing is rewritten: the wrong charge stays on the bill, and the reversal and the right charge sit beside it, on the same day. That day's reports show the corrected figure.
  • A corrected day is marked Corrected, and its Room revenue shows old → new. Anybody else who reads Closed days sees Corrections on that day: what was changed, why, by whom.
  • A payment, a refund or a discount is never corrected here. A bill with a tax invoice says “Issue a credit note instead.”; drinks with a VAT bill say “Cancel the VAT bill instead.”
  • A room charge's tax is worked out again, as the audit does; any other charge keeps the rate it was charged at.

Why it works this way

The history of closed days: D17-13. A closed day is corrected with a reason, the old and new figures both kept: D22-18. The owner, an Admin and the GM correct: D24-3; this month and the month before: D24-5. Every charge may be corrected, never money: D25-5.

A7.6The reports it sends by email

Right after the night audit closes the day, reports can go by email. Each address gets one email with every report switched on for it: the subject is the hotel's name, "daily reports" and the date ("5 Elements Akkalkot daily reports - 26 Oct 2026"); each report's key figures are in the email under its name, and each report is attached as its own PDF. If one of an address's reports cannot be made that night, its whole email waits and goes, complete, once every report is ready. The owner, an Admin or the GM sets them, for each hotel, in Settings, Messages, Daily report emails.

To send a report

  1. Switch the report on.
  2. Under Send to, type each address on its own line. At most 20 for one report.
  3. Tap Save at the foot.

Watch out

  • Nobody gets anything until an address is typed. A report switched on with no address says “On, but nobody gets it until an address is typed.”
  • A yellow box “Nothing is sent yet” means sending is not set up on the server. Nothing goes by email until it is.
  • A report switched on after an audit starts from the next audit.

Why it works this way

Daily report emails, each report on or off with its own addresses, the night audit report on to start, nobody sent anything until an address is typed: D27-280. The key figures, at most 20 addresses, and a report switched on later starting from the next audit: D27-282. One email per address with a PDF for each report: D27-371.
