Chapter A16

Across properties

For the owner, an Admin and the general manager. How to see every property on one page, read and edit a property's details, add a property, and run the people who work in each one: adding them, giving them a property and a role, setting their password, and switching them off.

A16.1The screens across properties

The Across properties section of the menu holds the screens that sit above any one hotel. The property switcher at the top does not change them. You see the ones your role uses.

ScreenWho uses itWhat it is for
All propertiesThe owner, AdminThe group today: every property added up, what needs you at each one, and a row per property.
PropertiesThe owner and Admin; the GM, hotel manager, revenue manager, Reception, accountant and viewer see their ownA card per property: its rooms and tax. For the owner also Edit, Close, adding a property, and each hotel's test hotel.
PeopleThe owner, Admin, the GM; the hotel manager can lookEverybody who can sign in, where they work and as what. Add somebody, give a property, set a password, switch somebody off.

One more screen is not in the menu: one property's page, /properties/5EL, with its figures and the people who work there (A16.5). It opens from Open on a property's card: the owner's, and for a GM or an Admin the card of a property they run.

A16.2All properties: the group today

Open All properties. The line under the title says how many properties there are, “each on its own business date”: a property that has run its night audit and one that has not are each shown on their own day.

  • The group today shows Occupancy, Average rate (ARR), RevPAR and Room revenue for today, with the Month so far under each, and the same month last year (Month last year; for room revenue, vs last year, the change in percent).
  • The rates are worked out from the group's totals, never averaged from each property's rate.
  • Last year counts only the properties that sold a room in that month a year back. “Last year: 0 of 1 properties” means none did yet, so the last-year figures are a dash. A new property never pulls the comparison down.
  • The (i) beside it says the same in one line.

Needs you

A property whose business date is behind comes first: “business date 9 Oct, 1 day behind today (10 Oct)”, with “Close the days in Night audit”. Then everything each property's bell asks somebody to act on: an arrival not yet checked in, money outstanding on a room due out, money to refund, a store never counted. Each line starts with the property's name. A line with › opens the screen that deals with it. When there is nothing, it says “Nothing waiting at any property”.

A row per property

Watch out

  • A figure that did not come back is a dash, never a zero. If the figures fail to load, the page says so and offers to try again.
  • Test hotels are never counted or shown here, and never in Needs you.

Why it works this way

All properties replaced the table at the foot of the Dashboard; last year compares only the properties that had one, and says how many: D27-243. Test hotels kept out of everything real: D27-296.

A16.3Properties: a card per property

Open Properties. What you see depends on who you are.

The owner and a group administrator

The line under the title counts them: “1 open”, or “2 open · 1 closed”. Each open property has a card; closed ones are under Closed, faded.

  • The card shows All rooms (every active room, blocked or not), room Types, the GSTIN, and the GST in use: GST on rooms, GST on restaurant, bar, banquet, spa, laundry and everything else. A dash means none is set.
  • Calendar switches to that property and opens its Calendar.
  • Close takes the property out of every picker. Nothing is deleted: its bookings, bills and invoices stay. It is off while guests are checked in, and the card says why: “15 bookings checked in - it cannot be closed until they check out.” Reopen, on a closed card, brings it back.

The test hotel

Under each open property's card, the owner and a group administrator see Test hotel: “A practice copy of this hotel's whole setup, with no guests or money.”

  • Make a test hotel makes it, once per hotel. Then the card shows its name with TEST, and three buttons.
  • Open switches to the test hotel and opens its Rooms. Every screen there carries an orange stripe: “TEST HOTEL · practice only · nothing here is real”.
  • Reset deletes every booking, bill, invoice, restaurant order, guest, company, cash entry, stock move and night audit made in the test hotel. Its setup and the people testing in it stay. Type RESET to confirm.
  • Re-copy setup deletes the test activity, then brings the real hotel's rooms, rates, menu and settings again. Type RE-COPY to confirm.

Everyone else

A GM, Reception or an accountant sees only the properties they work at, and cannot change anything here. A test hotel they also work in shows TEST beside its name. Their card says Rooms sellable today: the rooms less the ones blocked today, so it can differ from the owner's All rooms. A GM or an Admin of a property also gets Open on its card, to its own page (A16.5), beside Open the Calendar.

Watch out

  • Close does not ask first. The property leaves the pickers at once. Reopen undoes it.
  • Reset and Re-copy setup cannot be undone. They touch the test hotel only, never the real one.

Why it works this way

A property that has taken a booking must never disappear: its folios and invoices may have to be produced years later, so it is closed, not deleted, and never with a guest still in it. “Stay view” became “Calendar”: D27-82. Test hotels, one per real hotel, made, reset and re-copied by the owner and group administrators only: D27-296. The card's Calendar opens the Calendar, not Rooms; a GM or an Admin gets Open on a property they run; the two room counts have two labels: D27-308.

A16.4Editing and adding a property

To edit a property's details

  1. On the property's card, click Edit. The window says “These print on this property's invoices.”
  2. Change the Name, City, State, Address, GSTIN, Phone or Email.
  3. Click Save. If something is wrong (a GSTIN that is not fifteen characters, for example), it says “Not saved” and why.

The rest of a property's profile (PIN code, PAN, website, property type, stars, the map point) is in Settings. There a GM or Admin edits their own hotel's details; the name is a group administrator's to change (A17.2).

To add a property

  1. Under the cards, find Add a property.
  2. Type its Code: 2 to 8 letters or digits, such as NSK. Think twice: “It appears in every URL and every invoice number, and it cannot be changed later.”
  3. Type its Name and City; its State and GSTIN if you have them.
  4. Type the Provisioning key, the key for this system. The box says “Creating a property makes the hotel and its invoice series; its room types and rooms are added in Set up this hotel. Being signed in as an administrator is not enough on its own.” Your browser is told not to fill a saved password into it.
  5. Click Create the property. It stays off until the code, name, city and key are typed.

The message names the new code and says what comes next: “No room types yet - add them in Set up this hotel.” The new property starts with its own invoice series and no room types or rooms (D27-263, D27-322). The key box is emptied after each property. Under the cards a grey line says the same: “Creating a property makes the hotel and its invoice series; add its room types and rooms in Set up this hotel. The code appears in every URL and every invoice number and cannot be changed afterwards.” To go on, choose the new property in the top bar and walk through Set up this hotel (A17.2).

Why it works this way

Creating a property makes a whole new hotel in the system, so it takes the provisioning key as well as the owner's sign-in. The code is in every address and every invoice number ever issued, so it is fixed. The hotel's code, currency and time zone are the group owner's, and its name a group administrator's to change: D27-214, D27-308.

A16.5One property and its team

On a property's card, click Open (the owner's card; a GM or an Admin has it on the card of a property they run). The page shows that property's own figures, on its own business date, not the date of the hotel chosen at the top, and the people who work there. A group administrator can open it for any property, and a GM or Admin for their own; anybody else reads “This property is not yours to manage”.

  • A figure not measured yet is a dash. A property in its first week often has no month figures yet, and Average rate and RevPAR are a dash while nothing is sold, never ₹0.
  • FSSAI licence number: the owner, an Admin or the GM clicks Change, types the 14 digits from the licence (blank for none) and clicks Save. It prints on food bills from then on.
  • Starts on is Dashboard, Rooms or Calendar. Choosing one saves it at once, from the person's next sign-in. They can change it themselves.
  • A person with no password yet shows “No password yet” in red: they cannot sign in until one is set (the key).
  • Remove asks first, as on People (A16.6).

To add somebody at this property

  1. Click Add somebody here. The window says “They can sign in straight away, and they see this property only.”
  2. Type Their name and The email they sign in with. “A typo here means they cannot sign in at all.”
  3. Type A password for them: at least 8 characters (10 for an Owner, Admin or GM). It is hidden as you type; Show lets you read it back to them.
  4. Choose What they do here. It starts on Reception, as on People.
  5. Click Add. Tell them the password and the address yourself: nothing is emailed.

Why it works this way

The person, their password and their role are one act: split into three, somebody stopped halfway can sign in and see nothing. The page answers only a property you run, because it carries the property's GSTIN and contact details.

A16.6People: everyone, where they work, and as what

Open People. The line under the title counts them: “14 active · 3 can reach every property”. The owner and the other group administrators see everybody; anybody else sees themselves and the people they share a property with.

  • Every property: “A group administrator reaches every property, including ones added later. Property grants do not apply to them.” This screen cannot make one.
  • Each property chip reads the code and the role there: “5EL · Reception”. A test hotel's chip carries TEST.
  • “No password yet” in red: “No sign-in account yet - they cannot get in at all. Set a password and they can.”
  • You see a button only where you may use it. A GM leaves the hotel's Owner and Admin alone, and nobody takes away their own access or switches themselves off.

To take a property away from somebody

  1. Click the ✕ on the chip.
  2. It asks “Remove Agra Clerk from 5EL?”: “They can no longer work at 5EL. Their account stays, and their name stays on everything they did there.”
  3. Click Remove, or Cancel.

To switch somebody off

Click Deactivate on their card. They can no longer sign in, and their name stays on everything they did. Their card fades and reads Switched off; Bring them back lets them sign in again. Once somebody is switched off, Everybody / Switched off at the top shows them on their own.

Watch out

  • Deactivate does not ask first. It works at once. Bring them back undoes it.
  • Nobody is ever deleted. A person who has left is switched off.
  • Somebody added with no property signs in to an empty system. The yellow note at the top names them until they have one.

Why it works this way

The activity log names people, so deleting one would leave entries nobody can read: they are switched off instead. Removing a person's access asks first: D27-117. Nobody switches their own access off: D24-19. Nobody runs somebody above them: the owner, 24 September. The Switched off filter: D27-243.

A16.7Adding somebody, and giving them a property

Access is per property. That is what stops one hotel seeing another's guests.

The owner: add, then give a property

  1. On People, click Add somebody.
  2. Type Their name and The email they will sign in with. “This is the link between the person and the account. A typo here means they cannot sign in at all.”
  3. Type A password for them, at least 8 characters. It is hidden; Show lets you read it back to them.
  4. Click Add. The message says “They can sign in now. Give them a property next - until then the app opens empty for them.”
  5. On their card, click Give a property. Choose Which property and Doing what. The line under it says what that role does.
  6. Click Give access. The message says “Pune Clerk can now work at 5EL”, and as what.
  • Only properties they do not already have are offered. Somebody can work at more than one property, with a role at each.
  • Test (the owner and a group administrator): in Add somebody, switch on Test, “For practising in a test hotel, never a real one.” The window then asks where they work, from the test hotels only, and as what. They work only in the test hotel, never the real one. With no test hotel yet, it says “There is no test hotel yet. Make one on Properties first.” (A16.3).

The GM, or a hotel's Admin: add somebody at your hotel

  1. On People, click Add somebody.
  2. Type their name, email and password, as above. The window says “Set them a password, where they work and what they do, and they can sign in straight away.”
  3. Choose Where they work (your own hotel) and Doing what.
  4. Click Add. They can sign in at once, at that property, in that role.

Watch out

  • The email is the whole sign-in. Type the address they actually use. It cannot be changed afterwards on this screen.
  • Nothing is emailed. Tell them the password and the address yourself.
  • A password for an Owner, Admin or GM role is at least 10 characters; for anybody else, 8.
  • A GM's picker never holds Owner or Admin. Only the owner makes somebody an Admin.

Why it works this way

People works for the GM, for their own hotel only: D22-3. Only the owner makes an Admin; the GM and a hotel's Admin make none: D22-2, D27-140. Somebody who reaches every property is a group administrator, and making one takes the provisioning key. The Test switch: D27-296. Password lengths: D27-243.

A16.8A new password, and a person's details

To set somebody's password

  1. On their card, click Password (Set a password if they have none yet).
  2. The window is “New password for Agra Clerk”: “Their old one stops working immediately.”
  3. Type the Password: at least 8 characters, 10 for the owner, an Admin or a GM. They type it at a terminal in front of guests, so choose something they can remember. Show lets you read it back.
  4. Click Set it. Then tell them: “Nothing is emailed - tell them yourself.”

The owner and a group administrator can set anybody's password; a GM or a hotel's Admin, the people at their hotel, never a group administrator's. If it is refused, the message says why.

To change a person's name, phone or first screen

  1. On their card, click Edit.
  2. Change the Name or Phone.
  3. Choose where they Starts on: Dashboard, Rooms or Calendar, the first screen after they sign in. They can change it themselves.
  4. Click Save.
  • “Their name is on everything they have done. The email is how they sign in and cannot be changed here.” The window shows it as “Signs in as agra@fidato.in.”
  • A group administrator's Edit has no Starts on: they set their own from their account menu.

Why it works this way

The password is typed, not emailed, because many of a hotel's staff have no address they read. A misspelt name is on every folio and every line of the activity log, so it is corrected here, not left.
