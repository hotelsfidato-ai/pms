Chapter A10

Guests and guest messages

For reception, managers and the owner. Who has stayed, the companies and agents bookings come through, correcting a guest's record, one guest across the whole group (Guest 360) and the same person entered twice, what the system sends to guests by itself, the Guest messages screen and what to do when a message did not go, and asking a guest for a review.

A10.1The Guests & CRM screens

In the Front office menu, the part called Guests & CRM holds everything the system knows about a person rather than a booking. You see the screens your role may open.

Why it works this way

One part of the menu for what is known about a person - the guest list, companies and agents, what was sent to them, the reviews asked for - and the CRM's screens joining it as they were built: D21-79. Guest 360 and the same-person list are for the owner, an Admin and the GM only, because a person's whole history across the group is more than any one desk needs: D21-4.

A10.2Guests

Open Guests. Every guest booked at this hotel is one line, put together from their bookings by the name on the booking. Repeat guests come first. The pill beside the heading counts them.

ColumnWhat it shows

Watch out

  • The list goes by the name typed on the booking. Two people called “Rohan Mehta” are one line; one person typed two ways is two lines. A name with “overlapping stays” is either two people sharing a name or something entered twice: “Shown, not merged - a wrong merge is far harder to undo than a visible duplicate.” To tell one person from another the system uses their phone and email instead (Guest 360 and Possible same person, A10.5 and A10.6).
  • The search finds names only. Nothing found: “No guest by that name”, “Try part of the surname.”
  • A guest appears here the first time they are booked. A guest record is never deleted.

A10.3A guest's record: the marks on a booking, and Edit guest

A guest's own record - name, phone, email, address, nationality, VIP - belongs to the person, not to one booking. It is changed from a booking's panel (open the booking from Arrivals, In house or All bookings), on the line under the guest's name.

  • Mark VIP. Tap it. Where the group has VIP levels, choose which. The message says “Marked VIP”, “… - this holds for their next visit too.” Remove VIP takes it off.
  • Stayed before. A guest who has stayed with the group before carries a blue mark, “Stayed with the group 3 times”. That is all the desk is shown: no hotels, dates or amounts.
  • Agreed to WhatsApp. The voucher goes on WhatsApp only when this is ticked and the booking has a phone number (A10.7). You may tick or untick it at any time; the audit log keeps who did.

To correct a guest

  1. On the booking's panel, tap Edit guest. (The owner, an Admin and the GM also have it on Guest 360, A10.5.)
  2. The window says “The guest's own record: it is the same on every booking of theirs, at every hotel.”
  3. Change what is wrong: Salutation, Full name, Phone, Email, Address, City, State, PIN code, Country, Nationality.
  4. On the booking's panel, under them, are this booking's Company name, GSTIN and Company address (A2.2): “This booking's company. With a GSTIN, its tax invoice is made out to the company; the guest still pays.” Change them here before the tax invoice is issued. They are not on Guest 360: they belong to the booking, not the guest. Only the people who may change a booking see them, and nobody does when the hotel has switched off the Company section in Settings.
  5. Tap Save. It stays grey until something has changed and everything typed is right. The message says “Guest updated”, “A10 Test Ravi Kumar: the change is on every booking of theirs.”

What stops Save, in red under the form: “A guest has a name.”; “"12345" is not a phone number. Type 10 to 15 digits, e.g. +91 98765 43210.”; “"ravi@" is not an email address, e.g. name@example.com.”; “Nationality is a two-letter country code, e.g. IN.”; “"XX" is not a country code. Type the two letters, e.g. IN, GB or US.”; “That GSTIN is not right - a character is mistyped. Check it against the company's GST certificate.”

The booking's panel shows the company under Booked through as Guest's company, with its GSTIN. An invoice already issued keeps the name it printed.

Watch out

  • You are changing the person, not this booking. The new phone or email is on every booking of theirs, at every hotel of the group, and messages go to it from then on. Every change is kept in the audit log.
  • A box emptied is cleared on the record - except Name and Nationality, which a guest always has.
  • A nationality other than IN puts the guest on the Form III register (chapter A3).
  • Only the people who may edit guests see Edit guest, Mark VIP and the WhatsApp tick: the owner, an Admin, the GM, the Hotel Manager and Reception.

Why it works this way

No screen could correct a guest's record until the owner asked for this one, from the booking panel and Guest 360: the owner, 28 September. WhatsApp goes only to a guest who agreed, ticked on each booking: D21-72; the desk may change the tick at any time: the owner, 21 September. The desk sees “stayed with the group before” and nothing more: D21-13.

A10.4Companies and agents

Companies and agents answers “where did the business come from, and what was each worth?”. Three tiles at the top, then one table for each kind of source.

KindWhat it says
Travel agents“Booked through a channel, and charged a commission for it.” Agoda, Booking.com, Expedia and go-MMT.
Direct“Straight to the property. No commission on any of it.” Walk-in and Fidato Hotels.
Corporate accounts“A firm booking for its own people, settled on the city ledger.” Every other source is listed here too - a travel agent added with Add company among them, and a company with no booking at this hotel yet, with 0 bookings and a dash for its revenue.

Each table: Source, Bookings, Room nights, Revenue, Commission, Owed, and Edit on a company for the owner, an Admin and the GM. The heading line of each kind adds up its revenue and bookings. Under the tables: “Commission is charged outside this system today and prints as a dash rather than a zero. It fills once a channel manager is connected.”

To add a company

  1. Tap Add company. The window says “One record for the group: the same company at every hotel.”
  2. Type its Name. Choose its Kind: Corporate or Travel agent.
  3. Type its GSTIN from the company's GST certificate - it prints on the company's tax invoices - and its Billing address, City, State, PIN code.
  4. Optionally the Contact person, Phone, Email.
  5. If the company may pay later, switch on Credit allowed (“A bill may be sent to the company to pay later (Bill to company).”) and type Credit days and Credit limit (₹).
  6. Tap Add company at the foot. The message says “Company added”.

Reception gets a shorter window: Name, GSTIN, Billing address, City, State and PIN code, and the line “Its name, GSTIN and address, for the tax invoice. Credit and contracts are set by the owner, an administrator or the GM.”

To change a company

For the owner, an Admin and the GM; nobody else sees the button. The same window opens from Edit company on a company's Contracts page (A15).

  1. Find the company in its table and tap Edit at the end of its row. A booking site or a source that is not a company, such as Walk-in, has no Edit: those are on Booking sources.
  2. The window is headed Edit company, with the company's details in it. Change what is wrong.
  3. Tap Save. The message says “Company updated”.

A company is one record for the group, so the change is at every hotel at once, on every booking of that company. A tax invoice already issued keeps the name, GSTIN and address it printed, and a credit note against it repeats them; the next invoice takes the new details.

What stops it: “A company has a name.”; “A GSTIN is 15 characters: 2 digits, the 10-character PAN, then 3 more, e.g. 27AAPFU0939F1ZV.”; “That GSTIN is not right - a character is mistyped. Check it against the company's GST certificate.”; “A PIN code is 6 digits, e.g. 413216.”; “Credit days is a whole number from 1 to 365.”; “A credit limit is an amount above 0, in rupees.”

Watch out

  • A company is one record for the whole group. The same name or GSTIN twice is refused: “… is already on the list. Edit that company instead.”, “… already has GSTIN …. Edit that company instead.”
  • Credit days and the limit are “Shown, not enforced, until the company's payments are recorded. Blank is not set.”
  • The Hotel Manager, the Accountant and a Viewer see the list but not Add company. Reception adds a company but does not see Edit.
  • This is not Booking sources under Property setup, which is the list a booking's source is picked from, with each one's commission.

Why it works this way

The heading says what the menu says, “Companies and agents”, because it once said “Booking sources” - the name of a different screen: D17-22. Add company here as well as on Contracts, with Reception limited to the name, GSTIN and address and never credit: D27-184. Edit on every company here, the existing window, and who may stays the owner, an Admin and the GM because a company is one record for the group: D27-382.

A10.5Guest 360: one guest, every hotel

For the owner, an Admin and the GM. Guest 360 shows one person across every hotel of the group.

To find a guest

  1. Open Guest 360.
  2. Type two letters of a name, or a whole phone number or email, in Name, phone or email.
  3. Each guest record found is a row: the name, “Blacklisted” if so, the phone and email, and how many times they stayed. Tap the row.

Nobody found: “Nobody found”, “No guest record has that name, phone or email.” The booking panel's Guest 360 link (Figure A10-02) opens the guest's page directly.

To blacklist a guest

  1. On the guest's Guest 360, under Blacklist, type the Reason. “The desk is shown this when they book again. It warns; it never refuses.”
  2. Tap Blacklist. The message says “Blacklisted”, and the guest carries the red “Blacklisted” mark.
  3. To take it off: Clear the blacklist. The message says “Blacklist cleared”.

From then on, typing this guest's phone or email on New booking or a walk-in shows a red line: “Ravi Kumar is blacklisted. Reason: …. The booking is not refused - you decide.” A booking site's guest whose phone or email is a blacklisted guest's is named on Arrivals and in the check-in the same way (chapter A1).

Offers

The Offers card says either “Agreed to offers. They may be sent to this guest's phone and email, at every hotel.” or “No offers are sent: this guest has not said yes.”

  • Record their yes: the guest has said yes to offers.
  • Record "no offers": the guest does not want them. It is listed as “No offers to phone 98765 43210” (or email) with Clear.
  • Both buttons show only when the guest's record has a phone or an email. Rekha Gaikwad's has neither, so Figure A10-08 shows none.
  • The desk asks too, with Guest agreed to offers on New booking (the walk-in too) and at check-in. A later answer replaces an earlier one.

Watch out

  • An ID document is never shown here - only “an ID is on file” or “no ID on file”.
  • The blacklist warns; it never stops a booking or a check-in. A person decides.
  • “Offers” and “no offers” are kept against the phone number and the email themselves, for every hotel of the group - not against one booking.
  • Visits counts the times they checked in, at any hotel; several rooms booked together count once; cancelled, no-show and future bookings do not count. Charged is worked out as Total spend on Guests is, over every hotel.
  • A guest who was joined from two records shows “Joined into this record: …” with Undo a join (A10.6).

Why it works this way

Guest 360 now, on the PMS's own data: D21-3; for the owner, an Admin and the GM: D21-4. The blacklist is set with a reason by those three and warns the clerk, never blocks: D21-21; a booking site's guest who matches is warned of on Arrivals and at check-in: D22-8. “No offers” is recorded against the phone and email for the whole group: D21-22. Offers only to a guest who said yes: D25-39, D26-6; asked at booking, the walk-in, check-in, the guest's own page and on Guest 360: D26-7.

A10.6The same person twice

A guest is one record for the whole group. The system recognises a returning guest by an exact phone number or email, never by a name, and never joins anyone by itself: a person always confirms.

At booking: “same person?”

On New booking (and the walk-in), as soon as a phone or email matches a guest on file, a grey box appears under them: “A10 Test Ravi Kumar · Has not stayed with the group yet - same person?” (or “Stayed with the group 3 times”). Nothing is offered when something typed disagrees with the guest on file - a different email, say; the two then wait on Possible same person for a manager.

  1. Ask the guest. If it is them, tap Same person. The box then says “- this booking is theirs.” and the booking goes on their record.
  2. Not them? Do nothing, or tap Not them to undo your choice: the booking makes a new guest record, as before.
  3. Typing the phone or email again clears the choice.

The desk sees a name and how many stays, nothing more. A blacklisted match also shows the red line of A10.5.

Possible same person: the old duplicates

Bookings made before this existed made a new record each time, so a returning guest may be two or ten records. Possible same person (owner, Admin, GM) lists every two records that share a phone number or an email, across every hotel. The pill says how many are “to look at”.

To join two records

  1. Open Possible same person. Each card is two records: the name (it opens their Guest 360), VIP or Blacklisted, phone and email, how many stays, the last stay, and “ID on file” or “no ID on file”.
  2. Decide whether they are one person. A family on one mobile, or an agent's number, is two people: see below.
  3. On the record whose name, phone and email should stand, tap Keep this record. It now reads This record stands. If only one of the two has an ID on file, it is chosen already: “Only … has an ID on file, so that record stands and the ID stays with the guest.”
  4. Read the strip: “A10 Test R. Kumar's stays move to A10 Test Ravi Kumar. Nothing is deleted, and it can be undone.” Tap Join into one.
  5. The message says “Joined”, “A10 Test R. Kumar's stays are now A10 Test Ravi Kumar's. It can be undone below.” The join is listed under Joined: “A10 Test R. Kumar joined into A10 Test Ravi Kumar · 1 stay moved · by … · 9 Oct 2026”.

Two people, not one

  1. On the card, tap Not the same person.
  2. The message says “Kept apart”, “… will not be listed again. It can be undone below.” The pair is listed under Not the same person: “A10 Test Ravi Kumar and A10 Test R. Kumar are two people · by … · 9 Oct 2026”.

To undo

Under Joined or Not the same person, tap Undo on the line. The message says “Undone” with “Every stay is back on the record it came from.” or “The two records are listed again.”

Watch out

  • Nothing reads a name. Two records with the same name and different phones are never listed; two different names on one phone are.
  • A join moves the stays; nothing is deleted. A blacklist or VIP on either record stays on the joined guest, and comes off again on undo.
  • An ID document never moves. When both records have one, the one on the record that does not stand stays where it is, for an undo.
  • When unsure, do not join. A wrong join is worse than a missed one.

Why it works this way

Suggested on an exact phone or exact email, a person confirms, every join can be undone, never automatic: D21-2. The same rule at booking - “stayed 3 times - same person?” - and a list of the old duplicates for a manager: D21-20. “Not the same person”, undoable like a join: D22-8.

The system sends a guest only a few messages, each through one sending service. Today:

MessageWhenHow

A booking waiting for the hotel sends nothing until it is confirmed.By email where the guest has an email, with the voucher PDF; and on WhatsApp where the booking is ticked Agreed to WhatsApp and has a phone, with the same PDF. Each way is its own message.

The voucher is the booking's confirmation. Its PDF shows the guest and who booked; under the stay, the guest's arrival time, their special request (A2.3) and the room promotion, each where there is one; each room with its guests, nights and rate; the booking's extras, each priced or “Included”; the room charges, GST, the extras with their GST and the grand total (a dash where no GST rule is set), and under it Paid and Due at check-in; what was paid in advance and what is left to pay at the hotel (or “Pay at the hotel”, or “Bill to company”), the booking's cancellation terms, the check-in and check-out times, the hotel's house policies, the bank details where they are set, and “Confirmed by the hotel” with the reference and the clerk. While online check-in is on, it also prints the guest's own check-in link and a QR code, “Check in online”.

  • A group of rooms gets one voucher, about its lead room, to that room's guest. It covers every room of the group.
  • Bookings made before sending was first switched on are confirmed only between 09:00 and 21:00, the hotel's time. A booking made after that goes at once, any hour.
  • There is no day-before reminder and no separate confirmation.
  • The booking's Notes are never on the voucher: they are the hotel's own. The guest's own wish is its Special request (D27-347). The lines the hotel's old confirmation printed: D27-344.

The desk's buttons

After a booking is made, its Booked card (chapter A2) has:

  • Send voucher: sends the voucher now, instead of waiting for it to go by itself. Once the voucher has gone by email, the button is replaced by “Voucher sent 9 Oct 2026, 14:05 by …”. It goes once: “The voucher for this booking has gone already, or three tries to send it failed. It is sent once, and not again from here.”
  • View voucher: opens the PDF to look at, print or save. It sends nothing. It is also under More options on a booking's panel, for a confirmed booking still to arrive.
  • Send check-in link: sends the voucher again, with the guest's check-in link, every way the guest can be reached: “Voucher sent again, with the check-in link”. Hidden where the hotel has switched online check-in off.

While sending is not set up, both send buttons are grey and the sentence under them says why (A10.8). Other refusals: “This booking is waiting for the hotel, so nothing is sent to the guest. Confirm it first.”; “There is nowhere to send it: no email address, and no telephone number the guest agreed to WhatsApp on.”; “No voucher could be made for this booking, so nothing went on WhatsApp.” (tell the manager).

Which messages are switched on

Settings, Messages, Guest messages on/off (the owner, an Admin and the GM). One switch per message, for this hotel only. Nothing here sends anything. The Tax invoice row says when it goes: “When the invoice is issued, by email, if it is made out to the guest. Only invoices issued after it is switched on; one made out to a company, an agent or a booking site goes by Send invoice.”

Watch out

  • The voucher goes on WhatsApp only with the guest's yes. New booking says it: “Ask them. Unticked, messages go by email if there is one, else not at all.” Bookings from the central reservation system arrive ticked; bookings from booking sites do not.
  • Nothing goes until sending is set up on the server: the sending workflow's address, its secret and the WhatsApp number. The vouchers that go by themselves also wait for the owner's pace, how many a minute. Until then, every screen that could send says so, and nothing is recorded.
  • The wording a guest reads is kept in the sending workflow, not in the PMS. Messages are in English.
  • A message goes to the phone and email on the guest's record when it is sent. Correct them first (Edit guest, A10.3).

Why it works this way

Fewer messages: the voucher is the confirmation, sent once, with the check-in link printed in its PDF; no day-before reminder; the desk's button stays to send it again: D27-264. The voucher both ways, email and WhatsApp: D27-235. WhatsApp through the central system's already-approved voucher template for now: D27-266; online check-in on for every hotel, a hotel may switch it off: D27-281. The tax invoice by email only: D27-284; by itself only when it is made out to the guest, never when it is made out to a booking site, an agent or a company: D27-313. One voucher per group, to its lead guest: D22-41, D22-52. Every future booking confirmed on switch-on day, soonest arrival first, at the owner's pace: D21-33, D21-34; the catch-up only from 09:00 to 21:00: D22-42. English only: D21-73.

  1. Messages from guests, when there are any (see below).
  2. A yellow notice while sending is not set up.
  3. The bookings no message can reach, with what to add to each.
  4. The record of every message sent at this hotel, newest first (the last 200).

One booking's messages

The same screen for one booking reads “What this system sent about this booking”. For a room of a group it shows the group's messages, because the group's one voucher covers every room. It is opened from Reply on a guest's message (below).

Messages from guests

A guest can write to the desk from their own page (Message the front desk). For the owner, an Admin, the GM, the Hotel Manager and Reception, Guest messages then starts with a card, Messages from guests, and a yellow “2 unread”.

  1. Read the message: the guest's words, their name, the booking and when.
  2. To answer, tap Reply. The booking's own Guest messages opens, with Reply to the guest: “They see it on their own page as “Front desk”. It is not sent on WhatsApp.” Type and tap Send reply. A reply marks the booking's unread messages handled.
  3. Done with it without a reply? Tap Mark handled. It then reads “Handled by …” with the time.

Once the guest has checked out: “This guest has checked out. Replies stop at check-out.”

Watch out

  • “Delivered to the sender” is not “read by the guest”. It means the sending service accepted the message for WhatsApp or email.
  • A booking listed as one no message can reach is not recorded anywhere else: nothing was attempted, so nothing failed. Fix it on the booking (A10.9).
  • The booking panel has no button for one booking's messages. Look for its reference in the Booking column.
  • The Revenue Manager, Housekeeping, the kitchen and the till do not see Guest messages: “What was sent to a guest is part of their guest record, and your role cannot open guests at this property.”
  • No connection: “This device is offline, so what was sent cannot load. It loads by itself as soon as the connection is back.”

Why it works this way

The system sends the voucher by itself, and the screen records every attempt so the desk can answer the guest's question the next day: D18-12, as changed by D27-264. Guest messages follow the Guests permission, so the Revenue Manager, Housekeeping, the kitchen manager and the till no longer read them: D27-144. One “Message the front desk” box on the guest's page, landing here with an unread count: D23-14; the desk replies, the guest sees “Front desk”, no WhatsApp, no replies after check-out: D27-224.

A10.9When a message did not go

A message that did not go shows “Did not go” in red, with the reason under What came back. The system tries the voucher - and a tax invoice - again by itself, a minute later, up to three tries for each way (email and WhatsApp count apart). After three, it stops.

What came backMeansWhat to do
“The receiver took it (200).”Gone to the sending service.Nothing.
“Could not reach the receiver. …”The sending service could not be reached.Nothing at once: it tries again. If it says this three times, tell the manager - the sending service needs looking at. Give the guest the voucher yourself meanwhile (View voucher, print or save it).
“The receiver did not answer within twenty seconds.”It was slow. It may in fact have gone.As above. A try again after a slow answer is never sent twice: the sending service remembers what it sent.
“The receiver refused it (400). …” (or another number), with its own wordsThe sending service said no.Check the guest's phone and email (Edit guest, A10.3). Tell the manager, with the words shown.

A booking no message can reach

The reason saysWhat to do
“No telephone number and no email address”Ask the guest. Add their email with Edit guest; or add their phone and, if they agree, tick Agreed to WhatsApp on the booking's panel.
“Not agreed to WhatsApp, and no email address”Ask the guest whether WhatsApp is all right. If yes, tick Agreed to WhatsApp; if not, add an email.

Once the booking can be reached, the voucher goes by itself and the booking leaves the list. The list does not depend on whether sending is set up: a booking fixed now is a guest reached on the day sending begins.

Watch out

  • After three failed tries, Send voucher refuses too. Hand the guest the PDF from View voucher, and tell the manager.
  • Never tick Agreed to WhatsApp to “make it go”. The tick is the guest's yes.

A10.10Reviews

Reviews asks a guest who has left for a review, and shows who was asked. Nothing is read in from Google, TripAdvisor or the booking sites: “Open the site itself to read them.”

To set the hotel's review link (owner or Admin)

  1. Under The review link, paste the hotel's own Google review link in Paste the link. It must start with https://.
  2. Tap Save the link. The message says “The review link is saved.”, and under it: “Set by … on …”.
  3. A wrong link: correct it, or tap Clear it: “The review link is cleared. Nothing will be sent until there is one.”

Not a web address: “A review link has to be a full web address beginning with https://. Paste the one the guest should open.” Anyone else reads “Setting this is for the owner or an administrator. Ask one of them.”

To ask a guest

  1. Find the guest under Departures: Guest (with the reference), Left, Stay, Review asked.
  2. Tap WhatsApp or Email on their row. WhatsApp is grey when the guest has no phone, Email when there is no email address.
  3. The message says “Ravi Kumar has been asked for a review.” The row then reads “Asked” with the time; or “Did not go” with the reason.

The buttons appear only when the hotel has a review link, sending is set up, and your role may ask (the owner, an Admin, the GM, the Hotel Manager, Reception). A dash under Review asked means not asked yet.

Watch out

  • After check-out only: “A guest is asked for a review after they have left, never before. This stay has not been checked out.”
  • A group is asked once, at its lead guest's room. The other rooms read “The group is asked once, at FH-….”
  • The hotel's Review request switch (A10.7) is off until switched on. While it is off, asking is refused: “Review requests are switched off for this hotel (Settings, Messages).”
  • No link: “This property has no review link yet, so there is nowhere to send the guest. The owner or an administrator can add it on this screen. The group's own review link has not been given yet.”
  • The Accountant and a Viewer read the list and send nothing.

Why it works this way

Ask after check-out and record who was asked; reviews are not pulled in from anywhere: D18-14. Each hotel's own Google link: D21-30. A group asked once, at its lead guest's room: D22-10. The review request was left for later, so its switch starts off: D27-266. No link is invented: a link nobody has set is a dash, and nothing is sent without one.
