Chapter B4

Rooms and rates

For the owner. What a hotel sells and how its price is built: room types and rooms, the base price and its add-ons, rate plans and meal plans, the restrictions, and how the CRS's and the booking sites' room types are matched to the hotel's own. Why it was built this way, and what was left out.

B4.1Room types and rooms

A room type is what is sold: Deluxe, Superior. A room is where the guest sleeps: 201, 202. A booking is for a room type; the room is given at check-in or before.

  • A room type says who it sleeps: the adults and children its price covers, the most it sleeps, and the most adults and children; and its beds, view, size, colour on the Calendar, amenities and photos (Settings, Rooms, Room types). New bookings are held to its limits D27-258.
  • A new hotel starts with no room types: the owner or GM adds their own, as with prices. A type nothing has used can be removed; one that has been used is switched Off, hidden from new bookings, its history kept D27-263.
  • The GM sets up their own hotel: room types, rooms, their imports D21-47 D22-30.
  • Out of service is a dated block, from a date with a reason; the Rooms screen, the room count and every occupancy figure follow it D17-11. A room lived in by staff is blocked with the reason house use and no end date, which the owner calls "NC": at 5 Elements the GM's room, so 18 rooms, 17 sellable (the owner, 16 September; D21-64).
  • Occupancy has one rule everywhere, the Manager report's: rooms sold on the night (complimentary in; no-shows, waiting bookings and day use out) over the sellable rooms D27-151.
  • The desk may overbook where the hotel allows it (a switch, on by default) D27-243; a machine never may: a booking from the CRS or a booking site for a room no longer free is refused D17-43 D17-80.

B4.2How a price is built: a base and add-ons

Since 30 September a room's price is a base plus add-ons D27-188 D27-194. The base is what one guest pays for the room alone (room only, EP) on a date. Everything else is added to it, and the add-ons are set once for the hotel. So the daily work is only the base: change it on a date, and every plan on that date moves with it.

  • Two ways per hotel: each room type carries its own base; or one room type is the base and the others are derived from it by a difference, in rupees or a percentage (rounded to the whole rupee) D27-188 D27-194.
  • Counting guests: the base covers one guest; two guests (adult or child) add the double occupancy figure; from the third, each adds the extra adult or extra child figure, adults first. Every guest, children too, pays the meal plan's add-on D27-194.
  • A part nobody set is a dash, and anything that needs it is not sold, and goes to the booking sites as closed D27-194.
  • Nothing already typed moved. A price typed before 30 September is kept exactly as it was, and prices exactly as it did, with a Remove beside it D27-194. A hotel still priced only by the older rack rate keeps selling every meal plan at its room-only rack until a base and the meal add-on are set on Rates D27-196. (The local demo is priced that older way, which is why every meal plan in Figure B4-01 shows the room-only figure.)
  • Prices are typed as rupees, never invented: everything on Rates starts as a dash (B3.1).

The Rates screen

  • Everyone who may read prices sees Rates; only the owner, an Admin, the GM and the Revenue Manager can change anything on it, and the server asks the same question on every price it is sent (B2.2).
  • Each day's price sits above how that day sold: rooms sold, occupancy, average rate, revenue D27-188 D27-151. Every room type is always open with all its rows D27-237 (5.1).
  • The Calendar shows each night's base price, else the standard price, else a dash D27-274.

Why it works this way

  • One figure moves everything: the owner, 16 September, wanted the cheapest room to be the base and everything else "connected by a difference", so a change reflects all through the system. The first build (16 September, migration 090) took that literally: one base for the hotel and dated differences in rupees. On 30 September the owner simplified it to a base per day and add-ons set once: the calendar changes only the base D27-188.
  • Rupees, not percentages, was the owner's rule of 16 September for every difference. Since 30 September a room type derived from the base may also be a percentage; the add-ons stay rupees (migration 162) D27-188.
  • The base is not the default. The base is priced for one guest; how many guests a room is sold for by default is a separate setting on the room type, which the pricing does not change (the owner, 16 September, HANDOVER.md § 4).

B4.3Rate plans and meal plans

A rate plan is a room type on a meal plan, named by itself: "Deluxe · Room only (EP)". It is not a second price list: it is a view over the one pricing, drawn as a tree D27-251.

  • Master, Derived, Independent: the master is the base room type on room only; a derived plan follows it by a difference or a meal add-on; an independent room type has its own base D27-258.
  • Who sells a plan: each plan names the booking sources that sell it, none named meaning all; New booking offers only the plans the chosen source sells. The booking sites keep their own mapping in SU D27-258.
  • Meal plans: EP (room only), CP (with breakfast), MAP (one more meal), AP (all meals) and AI (all inclusive, everything charged to the room) D17-50 D17-68. RO is no longer offered; every picker shows the code with its words, "CP · Room + breakfast" D27-164. Each plan says on which days of the stay its meals are served, and the voucher says so too D27-258.
  • On reports, the "Rate Type" is the booking's meal plan; no separate plan names to keep up D17-95.

Prices that are not the day's rate

  • A typed rate on a booking stays as typed; the 10% discount rule binds only the Discount box D27-62. A rate may be typed "Includes GST" and is split exactly (B3.6) D27-187.
  • Company contracts: fixed rates per room type and meal plan, or a percentage off the day's rate; the contract in force on the day of arrival prices every night; a contract rate is always before GST D27-184 D27-233.
  • Day use: its price is typed on every day-use booking, never taken from the night's price, and a day use never turns into a night stay or back D27-229 D27-230.
  • Changing a confirmed booking's rate before check-in: the owner, an Admin or the GM, with a reason kept in the booking's notes D27-187 D27-195.

B4.4Restrictions

Beside the price, a rate setter can say a room type may not be sold, or not sold that way, on a date: stop sell, closed to arrival, closed to departure, and minimum and maximum nights (migration 044). They are set per room type per date, not per plan: a room not for sale is not for sale on any plan. They show on Rates' Rooms left, and go to the booking sites through SU.

Why it works this way

The owner's answer of 16 September: bookings from SU and every outside system are held to stop sell and minimum nights, and a price of 0 is refused unless complimentary; the desk keeps its own judgement, because a person at the desk can see the whole picture (HANDOVER.md § 4, "D10").

Not found in the code

The booking sites are held to the restrictions by SU, which receives them. The CRS bridge, the other outside system, refuses a booking at ₹0 as that answer says, but the code reviewed for this chapter does not check a CRS booking against stop sell or minimum nights. Worth confirming before the CRS sells into a restricted date.

B4.5Matching the CRS's and the booking sites' room types

The CRS and SU each have their own names and ids for the hotel's rooms. Nothing is matched by guessing: a person matches each one, once.

The CRS

  • On CRS bookings, the card Connection to the CRS (owner and Admin): the hotel's id in the CRS, the source CRS bookings are filed under ("Fidato CRS", made once in Settings D27-286), and each CRS room type's id with the room type it is here (Figure B5-02). The CRS room type ids come from the CRS's Firebase console D27-275 (1).
  • A CRS room type with no match is never booked by itself: the booking waits under "Needs attention" with the reason, as does one with no rate, or whose price per room does not split exactly D27-171.
  • The price is the CRS's quote, before tax, per room per night D27-171; the PMS adds the tax and bills it D17-46.

The booking sites, through SU

  • In the channel manager's SU setup (owner and Admin) D17-85: the hotel's SU id, each room type's SU room id and type, each meal plan's SU rate plan id. Nothing is suggested or filled in from a name.
  • On Booking sources, each booking site carries its SU channel code, picked from SU's list; nothing is hard-wired D27-183.
  • What goes to SU: rooms left, each price for each number of guests, and the restrictions, for a year ahead, then only what changed. A date with no price goes closed D17-81.

Two answers, depending on how the hotel is priced

A meal plan with no price of its own: on 18 September the owner chose to send the room-only rack rate rather than close the date D17-92; on 30 September, for a hotel priced the new way, an unset plan is not sold and is closed on SU D27-194, while a hotel priced only by the old rack keeps selling every plan at the room-only rack D27-196. The SU setup page says how many nights are selling at the rack rate, and from when.

B4.6Revenue management: suggestions only

  • Revenue outlook shows and suggests; it never changes a price by itself D17-56 D18-15. A night 14 or fewer days out and 50% sold or less is "Soft"; 85% or more is "Filling well" D22-14.
  • Rivals' prices: where the hotel switches it on, each nearby hotel's lowest public price per night, 30 nights ahead, checked twice a week, beside each suggestion D27-269 D27-270 D27-272. It needs the owner's data key in the server's settings.

Left out, and why

  • A price that changes by itself: never; a person sets every price D18-15.
  • Room categories (Deluxe and Deluxe valley view grouped under Deluxe), asked for on 16 September: a field for it exists in the database (migration 090), but no screen sets it.
  • Contract allotments: later D27-184.
  • Our own booking engine: SU's is used instead D27-180.
