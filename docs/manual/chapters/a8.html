Chapter A8

Restaurant

For waiters, cashiers, the kitchen and the restaurant manager. How an order goes from the table to the kitchen and onto the bill, how add-ons and custom add-ons are put on a dish, how the bill is printed and paid, how the cash drawer is opened, counted and closed, and how the restaurant and its KOT printers are set up.

A8.1The restaurant's screens

The restaurant has its own space. Click Restaurant in the switcher at the top (A0.4). Its menu, Point of sale, holds these screens. You see the ones your role uses.

A8.2Taking an order at a table

Open Tables. Each section of the room (A/C, Non A/C, the bar) shows its tables. A table's colour says where it stands.

ColourMeans
Free (white)Nobody is at it. Tap it to start an order.
Running (blue)An order is open; nothing has gone to the kitchen yet.
In the kitchen (orange)Dishes have gone to the kitchen.
Bill printed (green)The guest has the bill in front of them.
Paid - clear it (yellow)Paid. Tap Clear when the guests leave, so the table is free for the next ones.
Closed unpaid - clear it (grey)Closed without payment, with a reason (A8.7). Tap Clear too.

To take an order

  1. Tap a free table. The order pad opens over the room (Figure A8-02).
  2. Tap a dish to add it. Tap it again for two. To find a dish, type in Search the menu, or tap a section button (Starters, Main course…); tap it again to see all.
  3. A dish with a choice to make (a size, a crust, Sides) opens a small window first: choose, add a note for the kitchen if needed (“no chilli”), and tap Add.
  4. Check This round on the right. Use − and + for the number, the bin to take a dish off, Customise for add-ons (A8.3).
  5. Tap Send. The message “N4 sent · Kitchen docket 14.” means the kitchen has it and the KOTs are printing (A8.4).

To add a round to a table that already has an order, tap the table again. The pad says “Another round on this table's bill.” and lists the dishes Already sent at the top (A8.3), and under them the KOTs sent, each with Reprint (A8.4). The + beside a dish already sent puts one more of it on this round, with the same choices (size, sides, add-ons and note); it goes with Send on a new KOT, and the first is untouched (D27-349).

At the bar: the Bar role

Somebody given the Bar role works as a waiter does, on the same screens: Tables, the Till, the Terminal, Close restaurant day and Guest requests, with the waiter's Start here card, and has Bar stock too: the bar's sheet, its count and its bills (A9.11). Their pad, Till and Terminal hold only the bar's dishes: the dishes on the menu's Bar tab, the sections sold under Bar or Alcohol (A8.11), and the pad opens straight on them. Their KOTs print at the bar; if a manager has sent a Bar section's KOT to the kitchen (A8.10), its dishes are still the bar's and print where the manager chose. A dish from any other section is refused even if somebody tries it another way, and the message names it: “Paneer Tikka is not on the bar's menu. A waiter orders it.” They take payment, print the bill and close the restaurant day, as a waiter does; a discount is the GM's, the owner's or an Admin's (D27-352). The Bar role: D27-324; the Bar tab, Start here and Guest requests: D27-329; Bar stock: D27-356.

A parcel

A parcel is an order with no table, named by its customer. New parcel is on Tables, the Till and the Terminal.

  1. Tap New parcel.
  2. Type the Customer's name (it must be given, so two parcels can be told apart) and, if you like, a Phone to call when it is ready. Tap Open the parcel.
  3. On Tables the pad opens on it, titled “Parcel · Rahul”: add the dishes and Send, as for a table. On the Till and the Terminal the parcel opens there.

The KOT prints Parcel · Rahul in place of a table, and so does the bill. Tables shows each open parcel under Parcels, above the room: the customer's name, how long it has been open and what it comes to. Tap it to add a round; print its bill or open it on the Till from its buttons, as on a table. A parcel leaves the list once it is settled.

When the restaurant has a packing charge (Restaurant setup, A8.10), it goes on the parcel's bill once, with its first KOT, taxed as the food. It is on the bill, never on a KOT. Struck by a manager, with a reason, it does not come back.

Watch out

  • Nothing reaches the kitchen until you tap Send. The round is kept on your device while you build it. A round left on a pad shows on the table's tile as a dashed border with “not sent”.
  • No connection? Send says “N4 is held. The kitchen does not have it yet. It goes as soon as the connection is back.” The kitchen is not cooking it yet: tell them, or wait for the line.
  • A dish refused (off the menu today, a choice not made, the restaurant closed): nothing at all is sent, the round stays on the pad, and the message names the dish. Fix that dish and Send again.
  • The total at the foot of the pad is what the dishes cost before tax. The bill (A8.6) has the tax.
  • The same dish again is one more of it, but only until the kitchen has it. After that, more of it is a new line, so the kitchen gets a new KOT for it.

Why it works this way

The pad works with no network until Send, so a waiter at the table never waits on the internet; the whole round is sent at once, all of it or none, so the kitchen never cooks half an order. The same dish is one more of it: D27-189. The note after each tap: D27-290. Parcels, named by the customer, with a packing charge once per parcel taxed as the food: D27-359.

A8.3Add-ons: numbered, and custom

A guest wants something extra on a dish. There are two ways, both under Customise:

  • A numbered add-on, from the restaurant's list: “2 is Kebab, ₹120”. Staff remember the number.
  • A custom add-on, typed on the spot: something not on the list, with a name and a price, or Free.

A numbered add-on, before the dish is sent

  1. Tap Customise beside the dish (on the pad, the Till or the Terminal).
  2. Tap the add-on's number. Tap it again for two of it; × takes one off. An add-on that is off today shows, but cannot be tapped.
  3. The top of the window shows what the line will read, for example “Veg Biryani with add-on Kebab”, and the price moves as you tap.
  4. On a line of several of the dish, choose All 3 (the add-on on each) or Just 1 (one of them, as its own line). Done waits until you choose.
  5. Tap Done.

The dish stays one line, taxed at its own rate. The bill shows the breakdown under it (“Veg Biryani 310 · Kebab +120”), and the KOT prints “+ Kebab” under the dish so the cook does not miss it.

A custom add-on

  1. In Customise, tap Custom add-on.
  2. Type its Name, as the kitchen should read it: “Extra papad”.
  3. Type its Price in rupees (40, or 40.50), or tick Free. There is no default: a blank price, or 0 without Free, is not accepted.
  4. Set How many with − and +. It starts at 1 and is its own number: one papad for two thalis is one.
  5. Tap Add. It is listed under the add-ons as “+ Extra papad ×1” with its price. Add more if you need to; × takes one away.
  6. Tap Done.

A custom add-on is always its own line under its dish: “Paneer Tikka ₹320 / + Extra papad ₹40” on the bill. It is taxed at the dish's rate, and it goes to the kitchen with its dish.

On a dish the kitchen already has

The thali has reached the table, and the guest wants one more thing with it.

  1. On Tables: tap the table. The pad lists the dishes Already sent; tap Customise beside the dish.
    On the Till or the Terminal: tap Customise beside the dish.
  2. The window is called “Add to Paneer Tikka”. Tap numbered add-ons, or add a custom one, as above.
  3. Tap Done. Each add-on goes on as its own line under the dish.
  4. Send it, like any new dish: the pad's Send, or the Till's Send to the kitchen. Its KOT names the dish it is for: “+ Extra papad · for: Paneer Tikka (KOT 12)”.

Watch out

  • An add-on's number is set when it goes on. To change it, strike it (× on the Till) and add it again.
  • Taking off an add-on the kitchen already has is like taking off a dish: only the owner, an Admin, the GM, the hotel manager or the kitchen manager can, and they must say why (A8.7). Striking a dish strikes its add-ons, with the same reason.
  • Customise needs the connection on the Till and the Terminal: a change held offline could reach the dish after the kitchen has it.
  • Whoever takes orders can add a custom add-on. Each one records who added it and when.

Why it works this way

The numbered list, one line per dish, the breakdown on the bill and “+ Kebab” on the KOT: D27-289. “All 3 / Just 1”: D27-290. Custom add-ons, add-ons on a dish already sent, Free never a default, the dish's own tax, their own number: D27-291. The common custom add-ons can be given a number in the Menu (A8.11).

A8.4Sending to the kitchen: the KOTs

When you tap Send, the round gets one KOT number. The kitchen's dishes print at the kitchen's printer and the drinks at the bar's: one slip for each kind of KOT, each saying in big letters what it is.

  • What a KOT shows: the time, what it is (KITCHEN KOT 14), dine in or take away, the table, each dish with its number, its add-ons and Sides under it, and the kitchen note. Never a price.
  • Which kind a dish is depends on its menu section: Bar and Alcohol sections print at the bar, the rest in the kitchen, unless the manager chose otherwise (A8.10). It is decided when the dish is sent, so a reprint goes where the first slip went.
  • A dish taken off after it was sent prints, on a reprint of its KOT, under “STOP - TAKEN OFF”, with the reason.
  • A lost or torn KOT: the order's KOTs sent list one line for each KOT and kind (“KOT 3 · Bar”), each with Reprint: on Tables in the table's pad, and on the Till and the Terminal with the order open. Tap Reprint: it prints at the computer that printed that KOT, the kitchen's or the bar's, not on the device you are using, and the screen says where: “KOT 3 · Bar sent to Bar PC”. If that computer is off, the screen says “Bar PC is offline”: the reprint waits there and prints when it is back, or, if a manager moves that kind to another computer first (A8.10), it goes with the kind and prints there. If no computer prints that kind, it says so and nothing prints (a manager chooses one, A8.10). On the computer that printed it you can also open Restaurant setup, the KOT section, This computer's last 20 KOTs, and tap Reprint. Either way the slip says REPRINT. Waiters and the bar reprint: D27-324; at the station, and from the Till and the Terminal: D27-329; a waiting reprint follows its kind: D27-330.

The printing lines

The line saysWhat to do
“Kitchen KOTs: Kitchen PC - online” (grey)Nothing. It is printing.
“Bar KOTs wait: Bar PC not seen since 8:42” (red)The bar's computer is off, asleep, or its screen is closed. Its KOTs wait for it and print when it is back, oldest first, the same day. Turn it on and open a restaurant screen on it.
“No computer prints Bar KOTs - they are not printing” (red)Nobody has chosen a computer for that kind. A manager taps Set up on the line (A8.10).

Why it works this way

One computer prints each kind and no other device prints a KOT: D27-159, D27-161. A missed KOT prints when its computer is back, the same business day only: D27-167. Kinds, one number with a slip per kind, the heading in big letters, REPRINT, the red line only for a kind something is sent to: D27-292. A reprint prints at its kind's computer too, wherever Reprint is pressed: D27-329. The website cannot reach a printer by its address on the hotel's network, so a printer is always reached through the computer it is connected to.

A8.5The Kitchen screen

The Kitchen screen stays open on a screen in the kitchen (and one at the bar). Every dish the kitchen holds is a ticket, oldest first.

  1. When you begin a dish, tap Start. It moves to On the fire.
  2. When it goes out, tap Ready. It leaves the screen and is the waiters': the table's tile on Tables says “2 ready”.
  3. The waiter carries it out and taps “2 ready” on the tile (or Taken on the Terminal). It is served.
  • A ticket's edge turns yellow after 5 minutes and red after 10. Under the wait, “12 min est” is how long the dish should take; “- est” means nobody has timed it.
  • A dish taken off after it was sent turns into a red CANCELLED ticket with “stop cooking this” and the manager's reason.
  • By table shows one card per table, its dishes grouped by course, so you can see whether a whole table is ready.
  • All / Kitchen / Bar shows one kind's tickets. Each screen remembers its choice: set the bar's screen to Bar once.
  • LIVE in the header means new dishes arrive at once; RECONNECTING means it is checking every few seconds instead.

Why it works this way

Two buttons, Start and Ready; a Ready dish is the waiter's to carry out: D27-221, D27-222. The All / Kitchen / Bar filter in place of the old KOT printer button, and the two-row header on a phone: D27-292, D27-293.

A8.6Printing the bill

Each printed bill says at the top what it is:

HeadingWhen
BILLPrinted before payment, for the guest to check and pay; and the first print after payment, which also says how it was paid.
BILL · DUPLICATEA paid bill printed again.

Before payment, from Tables

  1. Tap the printer under the table.
  2. The message says where it went: “Bill sent to Bar PC”. The table turns green, Bill printed.

Where the bill prints

  • A bill printer is chosen (A8.10): it prints there by itself, and the screen says where it went.
  • The bill printer is off: the screen says “Bar PC is offline” and offers Print here. Print here opens the bill on your device; tap Print here on it. A bill printed here does not print again at the bill printer later. If you choose to wait instead, it prints there when it is back.
  • No bill printer is chosen: the bill opens on your device, as it always did. Tap Print here.

It is a plain restaurant bill. It shows the hotel and the restaurant, its GSTIN, FSSAI licence and HSN/SAC (the code of each kind its lines sell under, Settings › Taxes, A17; a dash until it is set: D27-344), the bill number and date, the table, the covers and the steward, and every line with its quantity, rate and amount (add-ons under their dish). The same dish ordered in two rounds - a beer, food, then a second beer - is one line, 2× (D27-353). Then the sub total, the discount, CGST and SGST for each rate (alcohol's VAT on its own line, and on its own VAT bill), any round off, and the GRAND TOTAL: the amount the Till asks for. A paid bill also says how it was paid. It ends “Thank you. Visit again.”

Why it works this way

The bill printer, Print here, and the bill opening as before when no bill printer is chosen: D27-292. A plain restaurant bill, BILL before payment, with the discount and the round off on it: D27-334. Alcohol's VAT apart from GST: D21-91.

A8.7The Till: the cash register

The Till is where a bill is checked and paid. The menu is on the left: tapping a dish adds it to the open order at once. The bill is on the right. Under it are Open orders, Settled today and An earlier day's bill.

To open an order on the Till

  1. From Tables, tap the eye under the table. Or on the Till, tap the order under Open orders.
  2. For a new order without Tables, type the table in Start an order and open it.

To take payment

  1. Check the lines and the Total with the guest. Print the bill if they want to see it (A8.6).
  2. Tap Cash, Card or UPI; or type the room and tap Charge to room; or Part - pay two ways.
  3. The bill closes and moves under Settled today. To print it, tap it there, then Print the bill.
  4. When the guests leave, tap Clear on the table in Tables.

Printing a bill from an earlier day

Settled today lists today's bills only. To print an earlier paid bill again, for a guest or for the accounts:

  1. On the Till, under An earlier day's bill, pick the Day, or type the Bill number (a part of it is enough).
  2. Tap the bill in the list. Each says its day, its items and its total.
  3. Tap Print the bill. It prints at the bill printer (or here, as in A8.6), headed BILL · DUPLICATE, so it cannot pass as a new sale.

Only paid, voided and NC bills are listed: a bill closed unpaid has nothing to print. An earlier day's bill can only be printed; it is voided only on the day it was paid.

Why it works this way

The team asked to reprint a bill of an earlier day (18 October); the owner asked for it on 20 October. A bill printed again says DUPLICATE: D27-292. Who may print it is unchanged: whoever prints today's bills.

Voiding a paid bill

A bill paid by mistake (the wrong table, paid twice) can be voided by the owner, an Admin or the GM, on the day it was paid and before Close restaurant day. Anyone else sees Ask the GM to void this bill and sends it (below).

  1. On the Till, tap the bill under Settled today.
  2. Tap Void this paid bill and type why.
  3. Paid by card or UPI: refund it on the card machine or the UPI app first, tap Next, and type the refund's reference (the UPI reference or the card approval).
  4. Tap Void the bill. Give the cash back to the guest out of the restaurant's tin.

The bill keeps its number and reads VOID, with the reason, who and when; Print the bill prints it headed Bill · VOID. The kitchen's KOTs stay as they were: the food was made. The day's sales, the reports and the Day book leave a voided bill out, and its alcohol's VAT bill is cancelled with it. Close restaurant day and the night audit list it under Paid bills voided. Its cash comes out of the restaurant's tin, so the tin's Expected leaves it out (A8.9); nobody's front-desk drawer is touched.

Giving a bill free: No charge (NC)

A bill the hotel gives free - the owner's guests, a staff meal - is closed as NC by the owner, an Admin or the GM. Their name on it is the approval. Anyone else sees Ask the GM for NC and sends it (below).

  1. On the Till (or the Terminal), open the bill. It must still be open.
  2. Tap No charge (NC) and type why.
  3. Tap Give NC. The bill prints at once at the bill printer (or here, as in A8.6).

The bill reads NC · No charge in place of a total, with the reason, who approved it and when; on the screen, as on paper, each dish shows how many and no price. The paper lists the dishes and how many, with no prices, no tax and no total, headed NC · No charge. The kitchen's KOTs stay as they were: the food was made. An NC bill is no sale and carries no value: the day's sales, the tax and the reports leave it out. Close restaurant day and the night audit list it under No charge (NC), with who approved it, when and why, and Close restaurant day's Complimentary tile counts the NC bills. Its table is freed at once.

Sent to the GM: what a waiter or cashier may not do

Four things are the owner's, an Admin's or the GM's: taking off a dish the kitchen already has, a discount, giving a bill free (NC), and voiding a paid bill. A waiter or a cashier does not wait for the GM to come to their screen: they send it to the GM, with a reason, and carry on.

  1. On the Till (or the Terminal), tap what you need: the dish's X, Ask for a discount, Ask the GM for NC, or, on a paid bill, Ask the GM to void this bill.
  2. Type why. A discount: the percentage or amount, and why. A void paid by card or UPI: refund it on the card machine or the UPI app first, and type the refund's reference.
  3. Tap Send to the GM. The bill says “Sent to the GM, waiting: Strike 2 x Paneer Tikka (KOT 51) - Table 4 · bill …”.

The GM's bell counts it and Approvals, at the top of their menu, shows it (A12.3). When they Approve, it is done at once, as if they had pressed the button themselves: the dish comes off, the discount goes on, the bill is NC or void. The waiter's bill then says “Approved by …”. When they Refuse, with a reason, it says “Not approved by … (the reason)” and nothing changes.

Changing how a paid bill was paid

A bill paid by card but keyed as cash, or the other way round, is put right by the owner, an Admin or the GM, before the night audit closes its day. The amounts stay the same; only cash, card and UPI change into one another.

  1. On the Till, tap the bill under Settled today, then Change how it was paid.
  2. For each part of the payment (a split bill has more than one), pick Cash, Card or UPI.
  3. Type why, and tap Change it.

The bill then reads, under “Paid by card”, “Payment mode changed: UPI to Card, ₹336.00, by … at 18:50 - the reason.” Close restaurant day lists it under Payment mode changed, marked “after the drawer was counted” where it was.

The owner's allowance

The hotel's owner takes up to a set amount a month from the restaurant free; above it, a set % off. Both are this hotel's settings, typed by the owner or an Admin in Restaurant setup, Owner's allowance: Free each month, ₹ and % off above it. Nothing is filled in to start with: until the amount is typed, no bill can go on the allowance. The month is the hotel's business month and starts again on the 1st; what is not used is not carried over. The card also lists this month's bills on the allowance: what was free, the % off given and what was paid.

  1. On the Till, open the owner's bill and tap Owner's allowance (the GM, the owner or an Admin; nobody else sees it).
  2. The window says this month's allowance, what is left, the bill, what is free, and, if the bill is more than what is left, what is to pay after the % off above it.
  3. Tap how the rest is paid: Cash, Card or UPI; or Settle it free when nothing is left to pay.

The bill then reads, for example, “₹164.00 free on the owner's allowance, ₹406.40 by UPI”, and its discount line names the % off: “Owner's allowance: 20% off above the ₹164.00 left of this month's ₹500.00”. GST is charged on the menu price, the free part included, as for any sale. Close restaurant day lists the free part as Owner's allowance in how the money arrived; it is not cash in the drawer, and the night audit report counts it with the room and function postings, the Manager report as Payment - Owner's allowance. A void gives the allowance back.

Watch out

  • Food the kitchen already has comes off only for the owner, an Admin, the GM, the hotel manager or the kitchen manager, and they type why: “The kitchen already has this. Why is it coming off?”. The reason prints on the KOT, shows on the Kitchen screen and in the day's list of dishes struck after the kitchen had them. The same goes for one fewer of a sent dish, and for closing unpaid a bill any of which was sent.
  • Cash before the first float is refused: “Count this outlet's first float once (Close restaurant day, Open the day): cash is taken after it.” Card, UPI and room charges go on. See A8.9.
  • A bill paid wholly in cash is rounded to the rupee.
  • Cash over ₹1,00,000 on one bill asks for the guest's PAN or another identity.
  • No connection: cash, card and UPI are refused until the line is back. A room charge can be made with no connection: it is held and sent when the line is back.
  • Once the day is closed (A8.9), new bills and payments wait for the night audit.
  • An order on a banquet function is paid on the function's bill only; cash, card, UPI and rooms are switched off for it, with the reason.
  • A bill charged to a room is never voided here: “This bill went to room 201 - reverse it on the guest's bill.” A bill on a banquet function is reversed on the function's bill.
  • The same day only: a void on a later day, or once the day is closed, is refused: “The restaurant's day is closed and its drawer counted. A bill of a closed day cannot be voided.”
  • NC is for an open bill, and cannot be undone. A paid bill is voided instead; a bill on a banquet function is paid on the function's bill and is never NC. An NC bill always says why: “Say why this bill is no charge. The reason prints on the bill.”
  • Sent to the GM, asked again when they approve: if the dish was struck meanwhile, or the bill paid, the GM is told why and it stays waiting: “That line is already struck, or is not here.” The same thing twice is refused: “This is already with the GM.” Nobody approves their own.
  • A payment mode changes only on a paid bill of the day the night audit has not closed, and never to or from a room or a function, which are changed on the guest's or the function's bill. Cash of ₹2,00,000 or more on one bill is refused; more than ₹1,00,000 in cash needs the PAN the bill has: void it and settle it again with the PAN.

Why it works this way

A manager and a reason for food the kitchen has: D27-119. Charge to room asks first, naming the guest: D27-117; two open bills in a room, one button each: D27-162. The Till sends to the kitchen like the Terminal: D27-166. Close unpaid frees its table with Clear: D27-288. Orders on a banquet function: D17-21. Voiding a paid bill: D27-324; its cash out of the tin: D27-327. No charge (NC), the GM's, owner's or Admin's approval with a reason, no prices on the paper and no value anywhere: D27-351. A waiter or cashier sends the GM a strike of a sent dish, a discount, an NC or a void, approving does it; a paid bill's cash, card or UPI changed before the night audit by the GM, the owner or an Admin, with a reason: D27-363. An NC bill's screen without prices, and Complimentary counting the NC bills: D27-365. The owner's allowance, its two figures the owner's or an Admin's to type, the rest paid at the till, GST as a sale, settled by the GM, the owner or an Admin: D27-355, D27-366. The taxes in Restaurant setup too, the same card as Settings: D27-368.

A8.8The Terminal

The Terminal is for a fixed tablet or screen in one outlet. The first time it is opened on a device it asks which outlet it is in, and remembers that on the device.

  • Open an order, tap dishes to add them (each with Customise, the sliders icon, and − and +), and send them to the kitchen.
  • A dish the kitchen made Ready shows Ready with Taken: tap it when you carry it out.
  • Under the dishes, the open order's KOTs sent, each with Reprint: it prints again at the computer that printed it (A8.4).
  • Room-service orders waiting: a checked-in guest's order from their own page. Accept makes it a room-service order for their room and sends it to the kitchen; Decline asks a reason, which the guest reads. It is charged to the room at delivery.
  • Payment and printing work as on the Till, No charge (NC) included.

A8.9The cash drawer: Close restaurant day

Close restaurant day is where the cashier opens the drawer with a float, records money that was not a sale, counts the drawer, and closes the day.

Once, before the first cash: open the day

  1. Open Close restaurant day. The card Open the day shows a row for each note and coin.
  2. Count the float in the tin and type how many of each: ₹500 × 4, ₹100 × 10. The total shows on the button.
  3. Tap Open the day - ₹3,000.

From then on each day opens by itself, on the float the last close kept.

During the day: money that was not a sale

  1. Open the Cash step. Under In and out of the drawer, tap Record.
  2. Choose What happened: Expense (paid out, such as milk), Cash in (a top-up), To the safe, or Due paid (money owed, collected).
  3. Type How much and What was it for. This line is what somebody asks about tomorrow, so say it plainly.
  4. For an Expense, choose its Category: Kitchen, Housekeeping, Maintenance, Office or Other. Record stays grey until one is chosen.
  5. For an Expense, add the Photo of the bill if you have it: take it with the phone's camera, or pick a file. It can be added later.
  6. Tap Record.

Each expense in the list shows its category after the word Expense; an expense recorded before 27 October 2026 shows a dash. Under it, Photo of the bill opens the photo, or Add photo adds one now - also after the day is closed. Every expense, with its category and photo, is in the Petty cash report (A11.6).

An expense may wait for a manager's yes: it shows “Waiting for a yes - not taken off the tin yet” and is not taken off what the drawer should hold until it is approved (A12).

At the end of the shift: count the drawer

  1. Open the Cash step. Under Count the drawer, type how many of each note and coin come out of the tin.
  2. Look at The sum: the opening float, plus the cash taken, plus or minus the money in and out, is what the drawer is Expected to hold. Under it, Counted. A paid bill voided today is not in it: its cash went back to the guest out of the tin (A8.7).
  3. If they differ, a yellow box says “Short by ₹217” or over, and how much of it is a paid-out waiting for a yes.
  4. Tap Save the count - ₹3,140.

Close the day

  1. Open the Finalize step. Before it closes lists what a person must decide first: bills still open (settle them, or close them unpaid with a reason), tables never cleared after payment.
  2. Under The drawer, if the count is short or over, type Why. It does not stop the close; it has to be written down.
  3. Type the Float kept for the next day. The rest is deposited.
  4. Tap Close with the date. “A closed day cannot be edited.”

Watch out

  • Close restaurant day refuses while any bill is still open. It will not decide for you whether a bill is paid or closed unpaid.
  • After the close, new bills and payments wait for the night audit (A7), which starts the next business day.
  • Close restaurant day needs the connection.

Why it works this way

The first float is counted once; after that each day opens on the float the last close kept, so nobody types it again every morning. A closed day is closed: the owner, 28 September. A paid-out is not taken off the drawer until somebody says yes: D27-242. A difference never stops the close but must have a sentence, because a manager needs the sentence, not the number.

A8.10Setting up the restaurant and its printers

Restaurant setup is for the owner, an Admin and the GM. The kitchen manager sees its KOT section only.

  • Sections and tables: add a section (A/C, Non A/C, the bar), then its tables, one or a numbered run, four to a row. Taking one away switches it off; it is refused while a bill sits on it. Nothing is deleted.
  • Table booking hours: when hotel guests may ask for a table from their own page.
  • Owner's allowance: the free amount a month and the % off above it (A8.7).
  • Taxes: the Serves alcohol (VAT) switch, then the same tax card as Settings, Taxes (A17): the GST rates, the VAT rate when alcohol is served, each menu section's tax and the HSN/SAC codes. Changed here or there, they are one setting. The owner or an Admin switches alcohol on or off; the rates, each section's tax and the codes are changed by whoever may change them in Settings, Taxes.

A parcel's packing charge

Under Parcels, type the Packing charge, ₹ and tap Save. It goes on each parcel's bill once, with its first KOT, taxed as the food (A8.2). Left blank, a parcel has no packing charge; nothing is put in for you. The owner, an Admin and the GM set it.

The KOT section

To make a computer print: the Fidato Printer app

Fidato Printer is a small Windows app that runs this website and prints each KOT and bill by itself: no window opens, no print screen flashes up. It sits by the clock and starts with Windows.

  1. On the Windows computer the printer is plugged into, open the website, sign in, open Restaurant setup, the KOT section, and under Set up this computer for printing tap Download Fidato Printer for Windows.
  2. Open the file (Fidato-Printer-Setup.exe). If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, tap More info, then Run anyway. It installs and opens by itself, and puts Fidato Printer on the desktop.
  3. In its window, sign in and open Restaurant setup, the KOT section. Under This computer, type its name (“Bar PC”), tick what it prints, and tap Save.
  4. Click the Fidato Printer icon by the clock: Printer, then the receipt printer. (Left as Windows' default printer, it prints on whichever printer Windows uses.)
  5. Go to the Kitchen screen in that window and close the window: it keeps printing from the clock. The printing line on every till now reads “Bar KOTs: Bar PC - online”.
  • A slip the printer did not take (paper out, printer off) shows a Windows message, “Fidato Printer: not printed”; print it again from Reprint below.
  • It updates itself. When it starts and once a day it looks for a new version on the Fidato server; when there is one, it downloads it and installs it straight away, by itself. It is gone for a few seconds and comes back by the clock with no window; KOTs and bills sent meanwhile wait and print when it is back. If it cannot reach the server it keeps printing and looks again the next day.
  • Which version: rest the mouse on the icon: it says “Fidato Printer 1.1.0”, so you can see which computers have updated.
  • The icon's menu: Open Fidato Printer, Printer, Start with Windows, and Quit (stops printing).
  • The other way, with Chrome: download the setup file (KOT-station-setup-<code>.cmd). It lists the printers (type the receipt printer's number), makes it the default printer, puts a Fidato Printer shortcut that opens Chrome on the desktop and in Startup, stops the computer sleeping, and opens this section. Chrome prints without asking but shows its print screen for a moment.

By hand instead, step by step: src/modules/pos/KOT-STATION-SETUP.md. If slips come out too wide, set the printer's paper to 80 mm once in its Printing preferences.

Printing on this computer: the shortest slip, and a test

On a computer that prints, the KOT section shows Printing on and its name. Each slip is as long as it needs, and never shorter than its Shortest slip: 160 mm unless you change it. A receipt printer turns a slip it takes for a landscape page, so a short KOT can come out sideways while the longer bills come out upright.

  1. On the printing computer, open Restaurant setup, the KOT section.
  2. Tap Print a test KOT, and Print a test bill. They print exactly as the real ones do, marked as tests.
  3. If the test KOT comes out sideways, type a longer Shortest slip, mm (180, 200), tap Save, and print the test KOT again. Blank puts it back to 160 mm.
  • One computer per kind. Ticking a kind on another computer moves it there; the old one stops printing it by itself. A reprint still waiting for the old one moves with the kind and prints on the new one, marked REPRINT (D27-330); a new KOT sent before the move does not print there.
  • One computer can print everything: tick every kind and the bills on it. Each slip still says what it is.
  • KOT kinds: Kitchen and Bar can be renamed (“Restaurant”) but never switched off; add a kind (Tandoor, Desserts) with Add a KOT. Its name heads its slip: TANDOOR KOT 14.
  • Which section prints where: each menu section starts with its tax heading's KOT (Bar and Alcohol to the Bar, the rest to the Kitchen); change the odd one, such as Mocktails to the Bar. This moves only the printing: who may order a dish follows its tax heading, so the Bar role orders Mocktails only if the section is sold under Bar (A8.2, D27-329).
  • A computer replaced: tick its kinds on the new one. The old one then prints nothing and can be Removed from the list.
  • Every computer that prints keeps its last 20 KOTs here, with Reprint.

The KOT's design

How every KOT at this hotel prints: one design for the hotel, set by the GM, the owner or an Admin. Anyone else who opens the KOT section sees it but cannot change it. The KOT beside the choices is the preview, drawn as it will print.

  1. Open Restaurant setup, the KOT section, The KOT's design.
  2. Choose the size of The dishes, and of The KOT number and table: Small, Normal or Large.
  3. Under What it prints, tick each line the kitchen needs: Waiter, Covers, Time sent, The KOT kind (KITCHEN, BAR), Notes, The order number.
  4. Type a heading line if you want one (“Fidato Kitchen”), at most 40 letters. Blank prints none.
  5. Choose The paper: 80 mm or 58 mm, the width of the roll in the printer.
  6. Tap Save the design. The next KOT prints this way on every computer that prints KOTs. Print a test KOT shows it on paper.
  • As it was puts back the KOT every hotel printed before: Normal sizes; the time, the kind, the notes and the order number; no waiter, covers or heading; 80 mm. Tap Save the design to keep it.
  • With the KOT kind off, the slip is headed KOT - 14 instead of KITCHEN KOT 14. Each computer still prints only its own kinds.
  • If a 58 mm slip comes out too wide, set the printer's paper to 58 mm once in its Printing preferences.
  • The design is for KOTs only. The bill does not change.

Why it works this way

Restaurant setup without a developer: D22-6. The KOT section, its kinds, one computer per kind, the routing by section and the bill printer: D27-292. The kitchen manager may open the KOT section: D27-292. The one-click setup file, and signing in coming back to the page asked for: D27-299. The Fidato Printer app, printing with no window, and the shortcut's new name: D27-360. The app updating itself, straight away: D27-365, D27-366. The KOT's design, one for each hotel, with a preview: D27-368.

A8.11The menu and the add-ons list

  • Prices are yours. Nothing was shipped with the system. A price changed today applies to the next order; bills already printed keep the price they were sold at.
  • Each section has a tax heading (Restaurant, Bar, Alcohol, Banquet). A dish is taxed by its section's rule unless it has its own rate.
  • Variations (a size, a crust): the first type's prices are the dish's; a further type's add to it, or are Free.
  • Sides: groups of options linked dish by dish, with how many a guest may choose.
  • Take off a dish until a time (“out of paneer”): the pad shows it, and refuses it, until then.

The Add-ons tab

  • The numbered list: number, name, what it is, price or Free. Change (never the number), Off for a while, Switch off / Bring back. A number is never given to a second add-on while the first is in use.
  • Custom add-ons used: what staff typed in the last 30 days, most used first, with who. Number it opens a new numbered add-on with its name and price filled in.
