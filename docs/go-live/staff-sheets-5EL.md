# 5 Elements Akkalkot — one page per role (DRAFT)

**21 September 2026 (D21-71). Drafts, for the owner.** One page each for
reception, the restaurant and the GM, to print and keep at the desk.

**Every button named here was checked on 21 September** — on the running
system signed in as that role, or, where no test login exists (the waiter), in
the screen's code. A few things are marked **[when built]**: they do not exist
yet, and each is written into its page, and checked against the screen, the day
it arrives. **Before 5 Elements' first real day, all three pages are checked
again against the new-receptionist test (D21-57).** Not included: spa and
laundry, which 5 Elements does not have (D21-63).

## RECEPTION

**Your login is your own.** Your GM makes it. Forgot the password? Your GM
resets it on People. Never use anybody else's, not even for a minute: every
bill, payment and check-in carries the name of whoever is signed in.

**Can't find a screen?** Press **Ctrl K** (or "Find a screen", bottom left)
and type what you want: "folio", "day end", "walk in".

| Your job | Where | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Start your shift** | Finance → **Cashiering** | Enter the **Opening float**, then **Open the drawer**. Close the shift when you hand over. |
| **Take a booking** | Bookings → **New booking** | Fill in the guest, phone, email, booking source and the nights, **Add** the room, then **Confirm the booking**. |
| **A guest walks in** | Front desk → **Walk-ins** → **Open Stay view** | Click any empty cell on the chart: the room and the night are already chosen. |
| **Check a guest in** | Front desk → **Arrivals** | Pick the guest from today's list, then **Check in**. |
| **Take a payment** | Front desk → **In house** | Open the guest, go to **Bill**, **Settle**, then **Take payment**. |
| **Check a guest out** | Front desk → **In house**, due out | Open the guest, go to **Bill**. **Check out** opens once nothing is owed. |
| **A restaurant bill to a room** | Point of sale | Reception can use the restaurant screens at 5 Elements. |

**When the system says no, read the sentence.** It says why, and what to
do. A **dash (-)** means nobody has set that figure. It is never a zero.

**[when built] New before your first day**
- **"Same person?"** When you type a phone or email a guest has used before,
  the system shows who it is. Tap yes only if it really is them.
- **A warning on a guest** means a manager has flagged them, with the reason.
  It does not stop the booking. Tell your GM.
- **"Guest agreed to WhatsApp"**: ask the guest, and tick it only if they say
  yes.
- **Online check-in**: a guest may have filled in their details before
  arriving. At check-in, compare them with their ID in your hand, then confirm.
- **Room cards**: if a room's QR card is lost or damaged, reprint it; the old
  one stops working.

## RESTAURANT

**Your login is your own**, made by your GM. The kitchen and the waiters see
different screens.

**Waiters: Tables, Till, Terminal, Close restaurant day**

| Your job | Where | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Open a table** | **Tables** | Tap the table. Its order pad opens right there. |
| **Send an order to the kitchen** | the table's pad | Add the dishes, then **Send**. Nothing reaches the kitchen until you press Send. |
| **Settle a bill** | **Till** | Open the order and tap how they pay: **Cash**, **Card**, **UPI**, or **Charge to room**. |
| **End of service** | **Close restaurant day** | Three steps: what the shift did, the cash in the tin, then close the day. Only cash is in the tin; card and room charges are not. |

**Kitchen: Kitchen**

| Your job | How |
|---|---|
| **See what is waiting** | Everything ordered is on the Kitchen screen. |
| **Cook it** | **Start** when you begin a dish (it moves to "On the fire"), **Ready** when it goes out ("At the pass"). **Picked up** when it has left the pass. |

**Kitchen manager:** also **Menu**, to change dishes, prices and what is off
today.

**[when built] New before your first day**
- **Sizes**: a dish with sizes (Half / Full) asks which one before it goes on
  the pad.
- **Add-ons and preferences**: extra cheese, choice of bread, and "no onion",
  "less spicy", "Jain". Tap them on the dish; they print on the kitchen slip
  exactly as tapped. Use the note only for something unusual.
- **Menu** (kitchen manager): **Add variation** on a dish, and add-on groups
  made once and ticked onto every dish that offers them.

## GENERAL MANAGER

You see every screen at 5 Elements. The jobs that are **yours**:

| Your job | Where | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Give each person their login** | Across properties → **People** | **Add somebody**: their name, email and role here. **Password** resets a forgotten one. **Deactivate** when somebody leaves; never delete. |
| **Room rates** | Rooms and rates → **Rate calendar** | The real rates, before the first booking (D21-38). |
| **GST rates** | Rooms and rates → **Room types**, the **GST** card | With your accountant, before the first bill (D21-65). *(Staff may be told "Settings → Tax" when a rate is missing. Settings only shows the rates; this card is where they are set.)* |
| **Banquet** | Banquet → **Halls and packages**, then **Diary** | Real halls and packages before the first event. |
| **Approvals** | System → **Approvals** | Purchases and anything else waiting for your decision. |
| **The day's figures** | **Dashboard**; Reports → **Manager report** | Occupancy, rates, revenue, what is owed. |
| **The night audit** | Reports → **Night audit** | **Run night audit** closes the day. *[Who runs it each night: the owner's to decide.]* |

**Not yours:** the Google review link. On **Reviews** it is set by the owner or
an administrator. Switching services on or off is theirs too.

**[when built] New before your first day**
- **Menu import**: the real menu, with sizes and add-ons, in one spreadsheet.
- **Blacklist a guest**, with a reason. Reception is warned, never blocked.
- **"Possible same person"**: old duplicate guests to join, one pair at a
  time. Every join can be undone.
- **Guest 360**: one page per guest, every stay at every hotel. Owner, admin
  and GM only.
- **Room cards**: print the sheet for all 18 rooms.
