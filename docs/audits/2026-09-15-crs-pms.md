## 2 · Incompatibilities

Severity: **blocks** = nothing arrives until fixed · **must decide** = the owner picks a side · **fix on the way** = a known small change · **note** = worth knowing.

### A · Identity — what each side calls a hotel, a room type, a guest, a company, a booking

**CRS-1 · must decide · No field on either side identifies the other side's property.**
- PMS: `hotels.code` `^[A-Z0-9]{2,10}$` ("5EL", in URLs) and a uuid `hotels.id` (`db/migrations/002_tenancy.sql:22-38`). No external-id column.
- CRS: `hotels/{id}` Firestore document; the `Hotel` type has name, city, contacts — no PMS code (`src/data/types.ts:47-60`). The reference ids are slugs (`scripts/hotels.reference.ts:89`).
- Meaning: a booking for "5 Elements" in the CRS cannot say which PMS property it is for without a map somebody keeps.
- Resolution: keep the map in n8n's configuration for the first version (no schema change on either side); later a `pms_code` on the CRS hotel document.

**CRS-2 · blocks · The same hotel has different rooms on the two sides.**
- CRS reference: 17 rooms — Deluxe 3, Superior 10, Executive 3, Family 1 (`scripts/hotels.reference.ts:101-104`). PMS production: 18 rooms — Superior 10 (201–210), Deluxe 8 (301–308). Superior agrees at 10; Deluxe is 3 vs 8; "Executive Rooms" and "Family Room" do not exist in the PMS.
- A booking names a room type on both sides (`types.ts:360 roomTypeId`; `api/src/modules/reservations/groups.ts:250, 362-364` — the PMS refuses "One of those room types is not here").
- Meaning: a CRS booking for an Executive room has nowhere to land; the CRS sells a room count the PMS does not have.
- Resolution: the PMS is the record for owned hotels (it assigns the physical rooms); the CRS's room types for 5EL should be re-imported from `GET /:code/room-setup` (`api/src/modules/inventory/inventorySetup.ts`). The owner must say which count is true.

**CRS-3 · must decide · Room types have no shared key.** CRS `roomTypes/{id}` with an optional `code` (`types.ts:121-137`; import `descriptors.ts:1044-1104`); PMS `room_types.id` uuid, name, `max_occupancy`, `base_occupancy` (`057_extra_persons.sql`). Same fix as CRS-1: a per-property map.

**CRS-4 · fix on the way · Every CRS booking would create a new PMS guest, even a repeat one.**
- CRS: `customers/{id}`, email and phone unique — but "validated client-side" only (`types.ts:297-303`; `src/lib/rules.ts:171-194`).
- PMS: `guests` is group-level with no uniqueness (`004_guests.sql:16-51`, indexes only), never deleted (`015`); `POST /:code/bookings` inserts a fresh guest every time (`groups.ts:317-322`) — no lookup by phone or email.
- Meaning: the PMS's "possible duplicates" list grows with every feed; merge rules are "not decided" (`src/modules/guests/MODULE.md:166-168`).
- Resolution: accept for v1 (shown, not merged), or a lookup-by-phone in the booking route — a PMS change, owner's yes.

**CRS-5 · fix on the way · The company does not travel.**
- CRS: `Reservation.companyId/companyName` (`types.ts:404-405`); `Company` carries legalName, GSTIN, credit, `negotiatedDiscountPercent`, `paymentTermDays` (`types.ts:237-285`).
- PMS: `reservations.company_id` (`005_reservations.sql:18`) and `reservation_groups.company_id` (`011:69`) exist, and `companies` has name/GSTIN/credit (`004:70-86`) — but the booking body has no company field (`groups.ts:254-271`) and neither insert writes one (`groups.ts:327-328, 404-410`); "no endpoint writes to `companies`" (`guests/MODULE.md:113`); `listCompanies` is keyed by a name slug, not an id (`:139-140`).
- Resolution: v1 — the company name into `businessSource` (free text, `groups.ts:422`); properly — extend the route with a company (PMS change).

**CRS-6 · must decide · Three booking-number formats share one prefix, and neither side stores the other's number.**
- CRS: `FH-YYYY-nnnnn` from `counters/reservations` (`firestore/index.ts:1225-1235`).
- PMS: `FH-DDMM-nnnn` per hotel for a booking (`groups.ts:398-401`) and `FH-YYMM-nnnn` for a walk-in (`front-desk/operations.ts:940`) — e.g. `FH-2609-5001` (`PENDING.md:78`) next to `FH-2026-00002` on the same desk.
- The PMS has `reservations.ota_reference` (`005:38`) — nothing writes it (grep of `api/src`). The booking response returns references and rates, not ids (`groups.ts:446-458`). The CRS has `hotelConfirmationNumber` (`types.ts:445`) and `recordHotelConfirmation` (`index.ts:1204-1222`) — one string per booking, while a CRS line with `quantity` 3 becomes three PMS references (`groups.ts:396`).
- Resolution: PMS reference(s) written back into `hotelConfirmationNumber` (joined by "/"); CRS reference stored in `ota_reference` — the route must accept it (PMS change).

### B · The booking shape

**CRS-7 · blocks · The CRS refuses to save a booking without the hotel's confirmation — which, for a PMS hotel, only exists after the booking is sent.**
- `firestore/index.ts:882-886` throws unless a confirmation number, a rep's name or a time is given; `confirmedAt` then defaults to "now" (`:952`).
- Meaning: the salesperson would type a name or time to get past the gate, and the real PMS reference arrives later — the field the desk quotes back is invented first.
- Resolution: a CRS change — for hotels flagged as PMS-run, a "confirmation pending from the PMS" state; the write-back fills it.

**CRS-8 · must decide · `ALL_INCLUSIVE` has no PMS meal plan.**
- CRS `MealPlan` = EP, CP, MAP, AP, **ALL_INCLUSIVE** (`types.ts:149`). PMS `meal_plans` seeds EP, CP, MAP, AP (`011_model_additions.sql:47-52`) and `reservations.meal_plan references meal_plans(code)` (`:185`).
- Meaning: an AI booking fails the foreign key → a 500 "Something went wrong" (`api/src/server.ts:222-223`) after the guest was already inserted (CRS-21).
- Resolution: refuse AI for PMS hotels in the bridge, or add an AI row (a PMS migration, owner's yes).

**CRS-9 · fix on the way · Status words differ, and nothing in the PMS API can cancel a booking from outside.**
- CRS: draft, confirmed, checked_in, completed, cancelled, no_show (`types.ts:333-339`); it emits `reservation.cancelled / checked_in / checked_out / confirmed` (`index.ts:1190-1193`).
- PMS: tentative, confirmed, checked_in, checked_out, no_show, cancelled (`005:49-56`); `POST /bookings` always writes `confirmed` (`groups.ts:409`). No API statement sets a reservation to `cancelled` (grep `api/src`: only banquet events, `banquet.ts:397`); `/:code/stays/:id/void` is explicitly "not cancel" (`operations.ts:1355-1369`); check-in/out are desk acts with a room.
- Resolution: v1 is one-way, create-only; a CRS cancellation raises a notification at the desk; a cancel route later.

**CRS-10 · fix on the way · Four of the six CRS channels have no PMS booking-source row, and a row is compulsory.**
- `bookingSourceId` required (`groups.ts:294-299`) and must be an active row (`:343-354`). Production rows cover direct, walk-in and four OTAs; CRS `corporate`, `travel_agent`, `website`, `phone` have none. Adding a row needs owner/GM (`082:263-265`; `app.is_manager()` `013_policies.sql:132-146`).
- Resolution: one new row **"Fidato CRS"**, kind `direct`, `channel_code` `CRS` — the column was built "for what a channel manager will send" (`082:111-112, 139-141`); the CRS channel goes into `marketSegment`/`businessSource`.

**CRS-11 · fix on the way · Payment term DP / RA / BTC has no PMS field.** (`types.ts:351-357`.) BTC implies the bill goes to a company — the PMS does that at the folio (`finance/folios.ts` split billing, `/:code/billing-companies`), not at booking. v1: text in notes; the owner decides whether BTC should auto-open a company folio.

**CRS-12 · fix on the way · Rooms, people and extra beds are counted differently.**
- CRS: `quantity`, `adults`, `children` per line, `extraBeds` **per line, not per room** (`types.ts:365-372`); `totalAdults = adults × quantity`, `totalChildren = children` (not multiplied) (`index.ts:919-921`).
- PMS: `rooms` per line, `adults`/`children` **per room**, checked against `max_occupancy` per room (`groups.ts:366-372, 396`); extra persons derived from `base_occupancy` by `app.tariff_for` (`057`); no extra-bed field.
- Resolution: a written mapping rule (extra beds → adults beyond base occupancy; children per room), confirmed by the owner.

**CRS-13 · note · Small fields with no home.** CRS `specialRequests`, `internalNotes` (`types.ts:436-437`) → PMS `reservations.notes` exists (`005:40`) but the route does not write it. CRS `guests[]` (`types.ts:389-394`) → PMS one guest per booking, rooming list later (`groups.ts:314-316`). PMS `expectedArrival` has no CRS source. A multi-room CRS booking becomes a PMS **group** named "guest — N rooms" (`groups.ts:325-332`); `block_code` could carry the CRS reference.

### C · Money

**CRS-14 · must decide · The two sides will disagree on tax, and sometimes on the total.**
- CRS prices three charges per line (room, extra beds, children), each banded on its own per-night rate, 5% under ₹7,500 and 18% at or above, in paise (`src/lib/tax.ts:4-16, 33-36`; `src/lib/pricing.ts:35-51`). An inclusive rate keeps the typed total exact (`pricing.ts:15-19`; `tax.ts:104-105`). A company discount comes off pre-tax charges (`pricing.ts:79-86`).
- PMS: one `agreed_rate` per room per night (`005:34`), from `app.tariff_for` unless the caller gives `agreedRate` (`groups.ts:379-394`); the night audit posts it nightly with `app.gst_rate(hotel,'room',rate)` and `round(rate × pct / 100, 2)` (`night-audit/nightAudit.ts:427-434`); slabs are per property in `tax_rules` and a missing rule **raises** (`067_tax_rules.sql:43-71, 153-158`). No discount field.
- Worked example: ₹7,000 room + ₹1,000 extra bed. CRS: 5% on 7,000 + 5% on 1,000 = ₹400 tax. PMS, collapsed to `agreedRate` 8,000 with a (0→5%, 7,500→18%) slab: ₹1,440. The voucher and the invoice differ by ₹1,040 a night. Inclusive rates also drift by paise (the PMS recomputes tax from a rounded pre-tax rate).
- Resolution: for owned hotels the PMS is the pricing and invoicing record (its numbered invoice is statutory, `025`). Either the CRS quotes from `GET /:code/tariff` (`inventory/rates.ts:669`) for those hotels, or the voucher for those hotels prints the PMS's figure. The extra-bed banding rule (the CRS's "never pulled into the room's band") must be settled by the owner.

**CRS-15 · must decide · A zero rate or an unset room count passes the CRS and would become a PMS booking at ₹0.**
- CRS drops any charge with `unitRate ≤ 0` or `units ≤ 0` silently (`pricing.ts:42`); blanks become 0 (`NewReservationPage.tsx:227`); `totalRooms: 0` reads "Room count not set" and does not cap (`:981, 1002`; `docs/HANDOVER.md:113-114`). I could not find the validation that sets `errors.sellingRate` (§ 5).
- PMS accepts `agreed_rate ≥ 0` (`005:48`), so a ₹0 `agreedRate` is saved; the night audit would then post ₹0 lines that `015`'s "no zero lines" refuses — the audit fails for that stay. And "no default prices" is the owner's standing rule.
- Resolution: the bridge refuses any line with rate ≤ 0 or rooms < 1 before it calls the PMS; the CRS should refuse to save one for any hotel.

**CRS-16 · note · "Commission" means different things.** CRS: what the partner pays Fidato, per hotel, in `hotels/{id}/private/commercial` (`types.ts:109-119`). PMS: what the hotel pays the channel, per source per hotel, stamped on the booking (`082:157-163, 183-185`). For an owned hotel booked through the CRS the figure is internal. Resolution: the "Fidato CRS" source's commission at 5EL is null (a dash) unless the owner wants an internal transfer price.

### D · Auth and transport

**CRS-17 · blocks · The CRS has nowhere safe to keep a PMS credential, and the PMS accepts nothing the CRS can produce.**
- PMS `/api/*` needs `Authorization: Bearer <Firebase ID token>` for project **poms-fad05** (`api/src/auth.ts:66-103`; `server.ts:146-180`), resolved to a staff row (`auth.ts:112-131`). A token from **crstest-9a0c5** is a different project and is rejected.
- The PMS's two machine credentials are `x-pos-key` (only `/api/pos`, resolves to one outlet's service account at one property — `server.ts:157-167`; `024_pos_bridge.sql:65, 244-247`) and `x-provisioning-key` (creating properties — `029_provisioning_key.sql:33-40, 74-91`). Neither can post a booking. CORS allows only `CORS_ORIGIN` (`api/src/env.ts:78`; `server.ts:61-67`) — a browser POST from `crstest-9a0c5.web.app` fails preflight. **The PMS has no inbound webhook endpoint**; the only "webhook" in `api/src` is outbound (`storage.ts:146-147`, `N8N_ATTACHMENT_WEBHOOK`).
- Any credential the CRS held would live in `settings/*`, readable by every active user (`firestore.rules:581-583`), or in the bundle. That is the CRS audit's HIGH 1 exactly (`docs/HANDOVER.md:135-142`): `settings/webhook.secret` "is NOT a secret from staff — it is in the browser" (`types.ts:815-818`). A PMS token there means every CRS salesperson can write bookings into the PMS.
- Conclusion: **the only safe transport is CRS → n8n → PMS.** The CRS keeps doing what it already does (a signed POST plus a queue row); n8n on the PMS box holds a PMS credential and calls the API. `PENDING.md:117-121` already names an account (`n8n@fidatohotels.com`) and says the path is proved — but as **Viewer**, which should not insert a booking; it needs an identity that may, at 5EL only. Because the box's webhook paths are open to everybody (`HANDOVER.md` § 4), the n8n workflow must check `X-Fidato-Signature` (keeps outsiders out, not staff) and re-read the reservation from Firestore with the `automation` account before writing — the fix HIGH 1 prescribes.

**CRS-18 · must decide · Two n8n instances or one.** The CRS's voucher workflow (Gmail, Drive, WhatsApp, Supabase — `docs/n8n/README.md`, `docs/FLOW.md:89-98`) runs against a URL in `settings/webhook` — one URL for all events (`types.ts:819-821`). The PMS box has its own n8n (`deploy/n8n.sh`; `n8n.fidatohotels.com` does not resolve yet, `PENDING.md:25`). If the PMS credential lives on the PMS box, the CRS's single webhook URL must point there.

### E · Reliability

**CRS-19 · must decide · Retries and queue drains create duplicate PMS bookings; the PMS booking route has no idempotency.**
- CRS push: fire-and-forget (`index.ts:1082 void pushToN8n`), 10 s timeout, no retry (`webhook.ts:43`); `eventId` = the reservation's document id (`index.ts:1082`; `webhook.ts:31`) — a good dedupe key. Queue rows are never closed (`docs/HANDOVER.md:171-179`), so a later drain replays what the push already delivered.
- PMS: `062_idempotency.sql` covers `work_orders` and `banquet_events` only (`:38-47`); the `idempotency-key` header is read in `banquet.ts:296` and `housekeeping.ts:285`, never in `groups.ts`.
- Meaning: one CRS booking, sent twice, is two PMS bookings, two guests nobody can delete, two folios.
- Resolution: PMS change — `POST /bookings` reads `idempotency-key` and stores the CRS id in `ota_reference` under a unique partial index per hotel (owner's yes). Until then n8n must dedupe on `eventId` itself; the Supabase register's unique `fidato_conf_no` (`docs/HANDOVER.md:43`) is the only durable dedupe that exists today.

**CRS-20 · fix on the way · Two CRS bookings can share a reference.** `counters` is writable by any active user (`firestore.rules:592-594`; audit item 4, `docs/HANDOVER.md:152-156`). A duplicate `FH-2026-nnnnn` would be refused by the register and by any PMS unique on `ota_reference` — the second booking is lost. Fix the rule (`next == resource.data.next + 1`, as the audit says).

**CRS-21 · blocks (PMS side) · A refused booking leaves records behind — RES-1.**
- `POST /bookings` inserts the guest (`groups.ts:317-322`) and the group (`:325-332`) before it can refuse at `:352` (source), `:364` (room type), `:369` (party size), `:389` (unpriced); `withSession` commits a refusal (`MEMORY.md` 243; `src/modules/reservations/DEFECTS.md` (RES-1; the file was `MEMORY.md` when this was written)). Read in the code, not reproduced.
- Meaning: every refusal from a machine feed (unknown room type, AI meal plan, ₹0 rate, party too big) leaves a permanent phantom guest, and a retry leaves another.
- Resolution: fix RES-1 before any feed — every check above the first insert, or throw. Owner's yes; a PMS change.

**CRS-22 · note · No ordering, no amendments.** The CRS has no `reservation.updated` event (`types.ts:940-952`); the PMS has no route that changes a booking's dates or rooms from outside. An edit in the CRS after the push diverges silently. v1: create-only, edits by hand with a notification.

### F · Quotas and limits on the CRS side

**CRS-23 · note.** Reads: n8n re-reading reservation, customer, company and hotel per booking is ~4 reads — trivial; a poller scanning `automationQueue` every minute is 1,440+ reads a day and should be avoided (the push already carries the data). Indexes: 181 of 200 used; a new sync collection with two filters and two sorts costs (scopes+filters)×sorts×2 (`src/data/queryPlan.ts:23-27`) — roughly 8 of the 19 left. Rules default-deny (`firestore.rules:604-606`); `integrations` exists, owner/admin only (`:576-579`) — fine for a mapping, **never** for a PMS credential (an owner's browser is still a browser). Public repo: nothing about the PMS's URL, hotel map, key names or n8n path may be committed; the 7 workflow JSONs in `docs/n8n/` are committed — a PMS workflow must not be.

### G · Shared code

**CRS-24 · note · `src/components/ui` has diverged.** CRS 15 files, PMS 19 (adds `Menu.tsx`, `Term.tsx`, `Button.test.tsx`, `primaryAction.test.ts`). Of the 15 shared names, **5 are byte-identical** by MD5 (`Card.tsx`, `Combobox.tsx`, `DatePicker.tsx`, `FilterBar.tsx`, `describeError.test.ts`) and **10 differ** (`Button`, `DataTable`, `Input`, `Misc`, `Overlays`, `PageHeader`, `States`, `StatusPill`, `Toast`, `index.ts`). Runtime libraries are close enough to share a package (table in § 1); the build toolchains are not (Vite 6 vs 8, Vitest 2 vs 4, TS 5 vs 6, PMS's declared ranges older still — `package.json:53-58`).

**CRS-25 · must decide (goal c) · One login means one Firebase project.** The PMS's identity is `staff.firebase_uid` in Postgres with RLS; the CRS's is a `users/{uid}` document with no custom claims (Spark cannot mint them). They are different projects. `MEMORY.md` 264 records what happens when the two environments touch by accident.

**CRS-26 · note (goal d) · Fidato is hard-coded on both sides.** CRS `source: "fidato-crs"` (`webhook.ts:113`) and `FH-` references (`index.ts:1234`); PMS `FH-` references (`groups.ts:398`, `operations.ts:940`), the seeded source "Fidato Hotels" and `'direct' → 'Fidato Hotels'` in `app.booking_source_label` (`082:236, 296`). Selling this to another group means each becomes a setting.

## 3 · What already fits

- The PMS already reserved the seat: `src/modules/crs/MODULE.md` (a brief, "waiting on the CRS's API documents"), and Administration → Integrations lists "Fidato CRS … planned, Stage 11" (`src/modules/admin/data/adminLists.ts:145-148`).
- `POST /:code/bookings` takes most of a CRS booking today: guest name/phone/email, check-in/out, many lines each with `roomTypeId`, `mealPlan`, `adults`, `children`, `rooms` and an `agreedRate`; it makes a group for several rooms and opens a folio per room (`groups.ts:249-271, 442-445`). Meal plan codes EP/CP/MAP/AP are the same strings on both sides.
- Columns waiting for the CRS: `reservations.ota_reference`, `notes`, `company_id` (`005:18, 38, 40`) and `booking_sources.channel_code` built "for what a channel manager will send" (`082:111-112`).
